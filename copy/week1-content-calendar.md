# Buzzend — Week 1 Content Calendar (Launch Week)

## Campaign overview

**Goal:** Introduce Buzzend to the world and drive week-1 installs by hammering one idea into everyone's head: *the AI camera counts your reps.* Camera-verified challenges, live leaderboards, streaks — no honor system, ever. Every post should leave someone thinking "wait, the camera actually counts?"

**Cadence:** 1 post per day per platform for 7 days (Mon–Sun). Each day = one feed asset (IG + FB) and one story/vertical asset (IG Story + TikTok). Same theme everywhere, native voice per platform.

**Posting-time recommendations (local audience time):**

| Platform | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (feed + story) | 11:30–12:30 weekdays · 10:00–11:00 weekends | Lunch scroll peak; stories get a second push at 19:00 |
| Facebook | 13:00–15:00 weekdays · 12:00–13:00 weekends | Early-afternoon comment window; FB threads build slower, post earlier |
| TikTok | 18:00–21:00 daily | Evening watch-time spike; fitness content over-indexes post-workout hours |

Post TikTok *after* IG/FB each day so the day's theme has already landed on feed.

**Placeholders — fill before publishing:**
- `[APP-STORE-LINK]` — App Store URL
- `[PLAY-STORE-LINK]` — Google Play URL
- `[@HANDLE]` — the official Buzzend handle on each platform

Links go in bio (IG), first comment or post body (FB), and bio (TikTok). Never paste raw store URLs into IG captions — "link in bio" every time.

## Engagement playbook

### Reply patterns (first 60 minutes matter most)

- **Reply to every comment on Days 1–3.** Early velocity trains the algorithm. Keep replies short, warm, and in-voice: emoji + one line ("Camera's ready when you are 👀🔥").
- **The skeptic reply:** when someone says "no way the camera actually counts" — never get defensive. Lean in: "That's exactly what everyone says right before it counts their squats out loud. Try it 😏 [link]". Skeptic threads are your best-performing threads; pin one.
- **The tag-chain reply:** when someone tags a friend, reply to *both*: "This is now a Beat-It. We'll wait. 🥇" Turns one tag into a thread.
- **The number reply:** when people comment their max reps (Day 4 FB), respond with a gentle callout: "Bold number. The camera would love to confirm it 👑".
- **Never delete negative comments** unless abusive — answer them fast and honestly. "It miscounted" gets: "It happens with tricky angles — prop the phone lower and full-body in frame. DM us a clip and we'll take a look 🙏".

### Story polls & quizzes (IG Stories, one per day alongside the day's story asset)

- **Mon:** Poll — "Be honest: have you ever faked a rep count in a group challenge?" (😇 Never / 💀 ...maybe)
- **Tue:** Poll — "Who wins a 30-day challenge in YOUR group chat?" (Me, obviously / The quiet one nobody expects)
- **Wed:** Quiz — "What does Buzzend's AI use to count your reps?" (A: Your wearable / B: Pose detection through the camera ✅ / C: Vibes)
- **Thu:** Emoji slider — "How confident are you that your push-up max survives a Beat-It? 💪"
- **Fri:** Poll — "Pick your streak saver: 10 push-ups 🔥 / 10 squats 🍑 / 3,000 steps 🚶"
- **Sat:** Question box — "Drop a name for the challenge YOU'D start. Best one gets made next week."
- **Sun:** Poll — "Week 2 goal: keep the streak alive 🔥 / take a podium 🥇" — then screenshot results into Monday's story.

### UGC prompt (run all week, feature winners in week 2)

> **#CountMyReps challenge:** Film your screen while the AI counts your set — bonus points for your genuine reaction the first time the counter ticks up on its own. Post it with **#CountMyReps** and tag **[@HANDLE]**. Best reactions get reposted on our page and a shoutout in the week-2 recap.

Reshare every tagged entry to Stories same-day (ask permission in a comment first: "This is gold — okay if we share it? 🙌"). Reaction-style UGC is the single most convincing proof asset for the camera-counting claim — prioritize it over polished clips.
