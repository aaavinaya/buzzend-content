# What is Buzzend, and what are we selling?

Read this once, then never invent new wording — copy from here.

## The product, in normal words

Buzzend is a fitness app. You prop your phone up, pick an exercise, and
start moving. The phone's **camera watches you and counts your reps out
loud, in real time** — squats, push-ups, lunges, sit-ups, jumping jacks —
and gives you little coaching tips while you move. Then it tracks your
streaks, daily goals, and lets you run challenges with friends on live
leaderboards.

**The magic moment** (this is what all our content must show): a person
moving, and the number on the screen going up by itself. That 2-second
clip is worth more than any explanation.

## The one sentence we always use

> **"Buzzend uses your phone camera to count your workout reps and coach
> you while you exercise."**

## What success means (memorize this)

A download is NOT success. Success is a person who installs Buzzend and
**completes one real AI workout** (the app counted their reps). We call
that an **activated user**. Everything we make is judged by one rule:

> **The winner rule: good content produces store visits → installs →
> first workouts. Never judge by likes.**

## The goal right now

We are small and learning. This phase is about finding out **which
message, which hook, and which exercise demo makes real people actually
work out** — not about going viral. Budget: ~$0. No paid ads. No paying
influencers.

## Words we NEVER use (legal/trust reasons)

Never write or say: *perfect form · guaranteed · injury-proof · replaces
your trainer · medical-grade · most accurate · world's first ·
scientifically proven.*

Never lead with tech-speak: *AI model, computer vision, pose landmarks,
MediaPipe, algorithm.* Say what it does for the person instead:
"it watches your movement and counts your reps."

## The one unbreakable content law

**Never fake a rep count.** Before filming any demo: test that exact
exercise, on that exact phone, in that exact camera position. If the
counter is wrong, we don't film it — we report it to the product team.
No editing tricks that make the app look like it does something it
doesn't. Our whole pitch is "the camera really counts" — one faked video
destroys it.
