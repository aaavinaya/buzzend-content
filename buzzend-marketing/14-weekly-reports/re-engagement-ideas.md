# Re-engagement ideas — ⚠️ FUTURE IDEAS ONLY, NOT FOR THIS SPRINT

Day-13 deliverable (Avinaya). These are product/notification concepts for a
future release. **No product changes during the sprint** — this doc exists so
the ideas aren't lost. Approved wording only; no banned words.

## Notification copy concepts (for whenever push lands on the roadmap)

| Trigger | Copy concept | Why it might work |
|---|---|---|
| Streak at risk (evening, day incomplete) | "34h becomes 0 at midnight. 10 squats keeps the 🔥" | Streak loss-aversion is the app's own mechanic |
| Friend passed you on a leaderboard | "[Name] just passed you — 12 reps to take it back" | TOGETHER pillar; specific and actionable |
| Challenge day unplayed | "Day 4 of 7 is waiting. The board updates tonight." | Concrete deadline, no guilt language |
| Inactive 3 days | "Your camera still counts. One set today?" | Gentle, product-true, one ask |
| Post-workout share nudge | "That 22-rep set is share-worthy. Challenge a friend?" | Rides the high moment; CTA from approved list |

## Non-push re-engagement (usable without product changes)

- Weekly "board update" story on our own channels naming (consenting) leaders.
- Personal DM to challenge dropouts from the team account — human, not automated.
- "Restart Monday" challenge every Monday: fresh 7-day cohort, fresh board.

## Explicitly out of scope until after the sprint

Push infrastructure, email capture, streak-freeze mechanics, badges — all
product work. Revisit at the retro (Day 15) with retention data in hand.
