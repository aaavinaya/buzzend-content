# 2026 landscape research — findings → what Buzzend does about it

Researched 2026-08-23 (web research across late-2025/2026 sources; key
sources inline). This file is the "why" behind the playbooks. Refresh
quarterly — algorithms moved a lot in the last year and will again.

## The 10 findings that shape this whole system

**1 · Short-form ranking is watch-behavior, not follower count.**
IG ranks Reels on total watch time + likes-per-view + **sends** (DM
shares weighted heaviest); TikTok's bar is ~**70% completion**; Shorts
wants ~65% retention under 30s. (Hootsuite/Dataslayer on the Mosseri
signals; Socialync on thresholds.)
→ **We do:** 10–35s videos, counter moving by second 2, loopable cuts,
send-bait where honest. We track watch %/completion per post, not likes.

**2 · Every platform became a search engine.**
TikTok search, Instagram SEO (captions, spoken words, display name all
indexed) and Shorts ranking in Google/YouTube search are primary
discovery paths. Hashtag stuffing is dead; **3–5 specific tags + spoken
and written keywords** is the 2026 consensus. (Later/Toptal on IG SEO;
EmbedSocial/Stackmatix on TikTok SEO; Miraflow on Shorts search.)
→ **We do:** one search phrase per video, said aloud + on screen + in
caption/title. Keyword display names. Shorts get searchable titles.

**3 · Sends and saves are the growth currencies.**
DM-shares (IG) and saves (TikTok) now outweigh likes everywhere.
→ **We do:** make save-worthy teaching content (form/setup tips) and
send-worthy surprise content (the counter demo). Never ask for likes.

**4 · A small team's realistic winning cadence is 3–7 videos/week, cross-posted.**
Sources conflict on TikTok volume (some say daily×3, Buffer's 11M-post
data says 3–5/wk works); everyone agrees quality/completion beats
volume, and 0→10k typically takes **3–9 months** at consistent cadence.
→ **We do:** 5 videos/week (one is always a cheap win: a comment-reply
or a repurposed cut), each posted natively to TikTok + Reels + Shorts.
No panic before month 3.

**5 · The "camera counts your reps" lane is OPEN.**
Direct rivals exist but none owns the message: Zing AI hides its camera
tech behind generic "AI coach" language; Fitnit ("never count reps in
your head again") is small; GymCam is utilitarian. Meanwhile the
"reps-for-screen-time" boom (Pushscroll ~$30K/mo, Clearspace, PushUp
Time…) **proved camera-verified reps go viral** — framed as
anti-doomscrolling, not fitness.
→ **We do:** say the plain claim loudly and first ("your phone camera
counts your reps"); our wedge vs everyone: **no wearable, no hardware,
no manual logging — just the phone.**

**6 · The counter overlay IS the content format.**
Replo (push-up counter app) grows by users screen-recording their
counted sets and posting them; Cal AI scaled to ~$30M/yr on the
doctrine "the product must be demonstrable in under 3 seconds on
camera." (Starter Story/Stormy on Cal AI.)
→ **We do:** every video shows the live counter; we develop
screen-recorded "AI counted my set" as our native UGC format and
end-of-workout share cards as proof artifacts.

**7 · Unpolished beats produced — polished demos read as ads and get
scrolled.** (TokPortal's tests; Zeely's 92%-want-story stat; Gymshark's
lo-fi memes performing at parity with studio work.)
→ **We do:** real homes, real clothes, honest reactions, messy-bedroom
demos included (pre-empting "does it work in a small dark room?").
Faces beat logos — the team appears on camera; Ladder grew 2,000% by
making its people the creators.

**8 · The beginner emotional lane is gym anxiety + realistic challenges.**
"Shy girl workout" (400M+ views), cozy cardio, and the softening of
75Hard into 75Soft/"30 Realistic" all say the same thing: our audience
wants judgment-free, doable, at-home. (Healthline, Fortune, Yahoo.)
→ **We do:** a whole content pillar on beginner confidence; challenges
marketed as realistic and co-op ("finish the week together"), never
punishing or physique-focused. Gen Z treats fitness as friendship
(Strava's data) — our friends-leaderboard is social connection, not
domination.

**9 · "Nothing to log" answers the #1 quit reason.**
Fitness-app churn research: manual entry, complex onboarding and
unclear value drive abandonment; only ~3% of installers are active at
day 30 industry-wide. Privacy preference is rising (on-device
processing).
→ **We do:** market "no typing, no logging — press start and move"; if
pose data stays on-device, say so plainly ("your camera feed never
leaves your phone") — verify with the product team first.

**10 · Platform-worthiness verdicts.**
Facebook: every upload is a Reel pushed to non-followers; **Groups**
reach 20–40% of members — worth 15 min/day. LinkedIn: B2C fitness = not
worth team effort; founder 1×/week build-in-public only. X: free-account
reach is punitive; claim the handle and park it.
→ Encoded in the platform playbooks in `../08-social-media/`.

## Watch-outs the research flagged

- **TikTok US retrained its algorithm in 2026** under the new joint
  venture — treat old growth lore as expired; our own data is the truth.
- **Duplicate-content throttling** is real: one account per platform,
  clean exports, no multi-account reposting (a tactic some app
  playbooks use — not for us; spam risk + no hands for it).
- One research thread says longer (2–3 min) high-retention videos gained
  reach in 2025. Our default stays 10–35s; we run the occasional
  **"full unedited session, AI counting" 2–3 min experiment** to test it.
- Benchmarks so nobody panics: fitness engagement ~3–4% (IG) / 6–10%
  (TikTok small accounts), hook rate ≥30–40%, bio-link CTR 2–5%,
  early videos at 200–1,000 views = normal.

## What this research changed vs. the original strategy

The strategy (§20–§23) is confirmed in direction — IG/TikTok/Shorts
primary, proof-first content, hooks decide. Research **adds**: platform
SEO as a first-class practice, sends/saves as the metrics to chase,
comment-to-DM automation, FB Groups, the counter-overlay UGC format,
the gym-anxiety lane, and co-op challenge framing. Research **demotes**:
hashtag volume, likes as a signal, polish. No contradictions with the
strategy's guardrails (§7/§40/§47) — all findings fit inside them.
