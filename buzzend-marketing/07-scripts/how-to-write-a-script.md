# How to write a Buzzend video script

A script is ~60–80 spoken words. Writing it takes 20 minutes. Never film
without one — "we'll figure it out on camera" produces 45-second rambles.

## The structure (same every time)

| Seconds | Beat | What it must do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | **HOOK** | Spoken + on-screen text (≤10 words) + the counter already visible/moving. See `../05-hooks/hook-system.md` |
| 2–6 | **PROOF** | Wide shot: person + phone + counter climbing. Real reps, real count |
| 6–15 | **EXPLANATION** | One sentence: "Buzzend uses your phone camera to count reps while you work out." |
| 15–25 | **BENEFIT** | "So you can focus on moving instead of counting." (or the pillar-specific benefit) |
| last 2–3 | **CTA** | End card + spoken: "Try your first AI workout." (challenge content: "Challenge a friend.") |

Videos under 15s may drop the BENEFIT beat. Never drop HOOK, PROOF, or CTA.

## Rules

1. Write it as **spoken words**, read it out loud twice, cut every word
   that sounds like an ad.
2. One idea per video. Two ideas = two videos.
3. The §40 line at the bottom of every script: which phone + camera
   position was tested, and the verified count.
4. On-screen text ≤10 words per screen, high contrast, big.

# Example script 1 — Pillar 1, curiosity hook (V01, squat demo)

**HOOK (0–2s)** — Screen text: "My phone just counted my squats."
Visual: close-up of the counter ticking 6 → 7. Say: "My phone just
counted my squats."
**PROOF (2–6s)** — Wide shot: phone propped on floor, person squatting,
counter climbing 8… 9… 10 with the count audible.
**EXPLANATION (6–15s)** — Say: "Buzzend uses your phone camera to count
your reps while you work out. No wearable, no equipment — just the phone."
**BENEFIT (15–25s)** — Say: "So you can stop counting in your head and
just move."
**CTA (25–28s)** — End card: "Try your first AI workout." + app name.
§40 check: iPhone 13, floor-propped, front camera, 12 squats = 12 counted. ✔

# Example script 2 — Pillar 2, myth hook ("Start with 10")

**HOOK (0–2s)** — Screen text: "Start with 10. That's it."
Visual: person in normal clothes, living room, phone going down.
Say: "Ten squats. That's the whole workout."
**PROOF (2–8s)** — Ten real squats, counter 1→10, at 10 the goal chime.
**EXPLANATION/BENEFIT (8–18s)** — Say: "Your first workout back doesn't
need to be perfect. Ten counted squats beats zero perfect ones — and my
phone does the counting so I literally just move."
**CTA (18–21s)** — Gentle end card: "Try your first AI workout."
§40 check: (fill in device/position/count before filming) ✔

# Example script 3 — Pillar 3, challenge hook (friend battle)

**HOOK (0–2s)** — Screen text: "AI judges who wins. No lying possible."
Visual: two phones side by side, two people mid-squat, both counters live.
Say: "Sixty seconds. Most squats. The camera keeps score."
**PROOF (2–15s)** — Fast cuts between both counters; one person's rep
doesn't count (too shallow) — show the reaction: "IT DIDN'T COUNT IT 😭"
**RESULT (15–22s)** — Result screens side by side: 24 vs 21.
Say: "Twenty-four to twenty-one. The camera doesn't do favors."
**CTA (22–25s)** — End card: **"Challenge a friend."**
§40 check: both phones tested same position; shallow-rep rejection is
real behavior, not staged. ✔
