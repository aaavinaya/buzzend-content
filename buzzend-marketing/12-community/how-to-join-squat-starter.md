# How to join the 7-Day Squat Starter (5 steps)

One action per step. If any step doesn't match what you see, tell Avinaya —
this doc must mirror the app exactly.

1. **Install Buzzend** — iPhone: https://aaavinaya.github.io/buzzend-content/go/ios/ · Android: https://aaavinaya.github.io/buzzend-content/go/android/ — and create your account.
2. **Open the Challenges area** (Discover tab → challenges) and find **7-Day Squat Starter** — it's Public, so it's listed; searching "squat" also finds it.
3. Tap the challenge, then tap **Join Challenge**.
4. Tap **Record Squats**, prop your phone up so your whole body is in frame, and step back.
5. **Do 10 squats** — the camera counts them out loud. When it says 10, you're on the leaderboard. That's Day 1 of 7. Come back tomorrow.

That's it. Your reps are camera-verified — nobody can fake the board.
