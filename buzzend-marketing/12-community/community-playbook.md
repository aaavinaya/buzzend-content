# Community playbook (Person 3's second manual)

Community is our cheapest growth channel: replies are public proof
someone's home, comments feed our hook bank, and early engagement
velocity boosts every post.

## Daily (45–60 min total)

**Comments (every platform, twice a day):**
- Reply to **100%** of comments. Short, warm, human. Emojis fine, corporate
  tone banned.
- Skeptical comments are GIFTS: "no way this is real-time" → reply
  kindly + flag to P2 as a comment-reply video ("Someone said it's not
  real-time. Okay — watch.").
- Questions = future content. Paste the exact wording into
  `../05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (hooks-inbox section).
- Pin the best comment on every post.
- Never argue. Never delete criticism (only spam/hate). "Fair point —
  here's what it can't do yet" builds more trust than any ad.

**DMs:**
- Answer everything within 24h. If comment-to-DM automation sent a link,
  follow up personally on replies.
- Never send unsolicited promo DMs. Ever.

**The first hour after posting:** stay online 30–60 min, reply fast —
early velocity matters on TikTok especially.

## Weekly

- **2–3 Story interactions** (poll/question sticker) — replies and
  sticker taps are heavyweight ranking signals, and answers are market
  research.
- **15 min/day: meaningful comments on big beginner-fitness accounts**
  (genuinely helpful, never "check out our app"). Our name in the right
  neighborhoods.
- **Facebook Groups** (see `../08-social-media/facebook.md`): help first,
  links basically never.
- **Repost users (with permission).** Anyone posting a Buzzend
  screen-record or duet gets asked: "This is great — can we share it?"
  UGC like this is our best converting content (ask P1 to track it).

## Outreach (2–3 messages every Friday)

Targets: **micro creators (5K–100K), beginner-friendly home-workout /
calisthenics / habit niche** — the growwithjo/MadFit archetype, not
shredded gym elites. Check their comments/saves, not follower count.

First message template (personalize the first line, always):
> "Hey [name] — your [specific video] made me actually do the workout,
> which never happens 😅 We're a tiny team building Buzzend: the phone
> camera counts your reps out loud while you work out (no wearable). Free
> app. Would you want to try breaking it on camera? 'Can I cheat the AI'
> videos are doing really well and your audience would love it. No
> scripts, no approval needed from us — happy to send the app + any help."

Rules: no payment offers (budget = $0; if a creator asks about paid, log
it for the founders) · collab post / duet / stitch are the asks · every
creator gets a unique link if possible · log everything in a simple
outreach sheet (name, niche, date, response, result).

## Escalation

Screenshot + send to founders immediately: legal threats, press
inquiries, anything involving minors, safety/injury claims, viral
negative moments. Do not improvise responses to any of these.
