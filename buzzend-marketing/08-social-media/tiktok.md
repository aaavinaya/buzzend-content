# TikTok playbook — PRIMARY

## What it is, for us
TikTok shows every video to a small test audience **regardless of
follower count** — it's the best platform on earth for a zero-follower
account with a visually surprising product. It is also a **search
engine**: our audience literally searches "home workout for beginners"
here.

## What the algorithm rewards in 2026
- **Completion rate is king.** The current bar for big distribution is
  ~70% of the video watched. A 12-second video people finish twice beats
  a 45-second video people abandon.
- **Niche fit beats broad appeal.** TikTok now pushes content to tight
  interest communities. We want to be unmistakably a *beginner home
  fitness* account, not a *tech/app promo* account.
- Note: the US TikTok algorithm was retrained in 2026 under new
  ownership — old growth folklore may not apply. Trust OUR data over
  any guru advice, including this file: retest everything.

**Practical translation:**
1. Default to **10–20 second videos** — the counter reacting in real
   time is the easiest 70%-completion clip we can make.
2. Every video signals the niche: beginner language, home settings,
   bodyweight exercises. No office/startup content here.
3. Cut ruthlessly. If a second doesn't earn its place, it kills
   completion rate.

## TikTok SEO (our biggest free lever)
For every video: pick ONE search phrase real beginners type — e.g.
"how to do squats correctly", "push up form check", "home workout no
equipment" — then:
- **Say it** in the first 3 seconds,
- **Show it** as on-screen text,
- **Lead the caption with it** ("beginner home workout no equipment —
  my phone counts the reps so I don't have to"),
- Add **3–5 focused hashtags** (#GymTok #beginnerfitness #homeworkout +
  exercise tag).
Search-ranked videos keep earning views for months — this compounds.

## Setup (week 1, one-time)
- Switch to a **Business Account** → gives a clickable bio link at zero
  followers. Bio: positioning line + link.
- Pin a comment on every video with the app name ("It's called Buzzend —
  on the App Store & Play Store"). Many installs come from people
  searching the store, not tapping links.

## Posting
- **5–7/week is our ceiling, 3/week the floor.** One good video per day
  beats three sloppy ones — completion rate decides, not volume.
- Post at the fixed evening slot until our own data says otherwise.

## Engagement tactics (Person 3)
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour** — early velocity matters.
- **Video replies to comments** are the highest-ROI content we can make:
  every real question ("does it work in bad lighting?") becomes
  tomorrow's video, with the question shown on screen. Feed these to
  Person 2 daily.
- **Stitch & Duet** big fitness creators: stitch a form video with
  "here's what the AI says about that form", duet a challenge attempt
  with our counter running. Stitches/duets significantly out-engage
  standard posts and borrow big audiences.

## What success looks like
- Fitness niche engagement: 6–10% is normal (3–6% fine for small accounts).
- Hook rate target ≥40%; completion target: climb toward 70% on shorts.
- A few hundred views on early videos = the test audience — normal.
  Judge hooks after the 24h read, not after 2 hours.
