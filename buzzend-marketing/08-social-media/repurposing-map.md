# The repurposing map — one idea, six pieces of content

Never make one post from one shoot. Here is how a single concept becomes
a week of platform-native content. Example concept: **"50 squats, AI
counted"** (works for any exercise/challenge).

## The master shoot (Person 2, one session, ~45 min)
Film once, capture everything:
- Wide shot: person + propped phone, full set of reps
- Screen recording of the app counting (the counter overlay is gold)
- Close-up: the counter ticking over a rep
- The result screen
- One honest reaction moment ("it caught my half-rep??")

## The six children

| # | Output | Platform | How it's adapted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Main video (15–30s)** | TikTok | Hook spoken + on-screen ("Bet you can't do 15 the app actually counts"), query keyword in caption, niche hashtags, pinned comment with app name |
| 2 | **Same master, IG cut** | IG Reels | Same video, IG caption style (keyword first line, send-bait line: "send this to your workout buddy"), 3–5 hashtags, collab-tag if a partner was involved |
| 3 | **Same master, Shorts cut** | YouTube Shorts | Same video, NEW searchable title ("Squat Counter App Test — 50 Squats"), 150-word keyworded description with link in first 3 lines, pinned comment |
| 4 | **Story (top 3 seconds)** | IG Story | The single best moment + poll sticker ("Could you do 50?") or link sticker ("Try your first AI workout ⬇") — publish within 48h of the Reel |
| 5 | **FB Reel** | Facebook | Auto cross-post via Business Suite with the IG caption. Zero extra work |
| 6 | **Alternate-hook version** | best platform, next week | Same footage, different hook category (e.g. curiosity → challenge). One changed variable = a clean test |

**Optional 7th — carousel:** if the concept teaches something (form cue,
camera setup), P2 turns it into a 5-slide carousel using
`../../social-media/templates/` + the carousel template — slide 1 is the
hook, one idea per slide, last slide CTA.

**Optional 8th — LinkedIn:** a build-in-public draft for the founder
("what 50 counted squats taught us" — no tech-speak, lesson-focused).

## Repurposing rules

1. **Never upload a file with another platform's watermark.** Export
   clean masters; TikTok watermarks on Reels/Shorts kill reach.
2. **Captions/titles are per-platform, always.** The video can be
   identical; the words around it never are (TikTok = search phrase,
   IG = keyword + send-bait, YT = search title).
3. **Stories are the second life of every Reel** — best 3 seconds + a
   sticker, within 48 hours.
4. **Winners get children first.** A video that beat our median gets its
   alternate-hook version made before any new concept is filmed.
5. **Comment questions are free concepts.** Every real question ("does
   it work in the dark?") becomes a video reply on TikTok and a fresh
   concept in the backlog.
6. **One account per platform.** We never run multiple accounts posting
   the same clips — duplicate-content detection throttles it, and we
   don't have the hands for it anyway.
