# Facebook playbook — SECONDARY (cheap reach + Groups)

## Why we bother at all
Two 2026 realities make Facebook nearly-free upside:
1. **Every video we upload automatically becomes a Reel** and gets shown
   to non-followers first — a zero-follower Page can get discovered.
   Up to half of feeds are recommendations from unfollowed accounts.
2. **Groups are the hidden lever.** Posts inside active groups reach
   20–40% of members (a Page post reaches ~1–6% of its followers).
   Beginner-fitness and home-workout groups are exactly our audience.

## The rules
**Page (10 min/day, Person 3):**
- Cross-post every Reel via Meta Business Suite (one click posts to
  IG + FB together).
- Keep it 80% value / 20% anything promotional — Facebook actively
  suppresses salesy posts.
- The week-1 static posts + captions
  (`../../social-media/rendered/` + `week1-content-calendar.md`) are
  ready-made FB feed content.

**Groups (Person 3, ~15 min/day, the real work):**
- Join 10–20 groups: home workout, beginner fitness, "gym anxiety",
  fitness accountability, habit building.
- **First two weeks: only help.** Answer form questions, encourage
  people, share genuinely useful tips. Zero links, zero app mentions.
- After you're a familiar name: soft mentions only when directly
  relevant ("I use an app that counts my reps with the camera — happy
  to share the name if useful"). If someone asks → answer. Never paste
  links unprompted.
- **One spammy post can get us banned from a group and torch the brand.
  When unsure, don't post the link.**

## Audience honesty
Facebook skews older than our 18–35 core. Treat results here as bonus,
never move budget/effort here at the expense of TikTok/IG/Shorts.

## What success looks like
Small but real: group members asking for the app name unprompted, FB
Reels occasionally popping, a slow trickle of installs. Zero extra
production cost, so any result is profit.
