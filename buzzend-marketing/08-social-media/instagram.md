# Instagram playbook (Reels + Stories) — PRIMARY

## What it is, for us
Reels = short vertical videos shown mostly to people who DON'T follow us
(discovery). Stories = 24-hour posts shown to people who DO follow us
(relationship). We use Reels to get found and Stories to keep people.

## What the algorithm rewards in 2026 (and what that means for us)
Instagram ranks Reels mainly on **total watch time** (including replays),
**likes-per-view**, and — weighted heaviest — **sends** (people DMing your
Reel to a friend). Practical translation:

1. **Make sendable videos.** "The phone counts your reps" is naturally a
   send-to-a-friend moment. Add send-bait lines where honest: *"Send this
   to your workout buddy who always miscounts."*
2. **Make loopable videos.** A clean rep loop where the end cuts back
   into the start gets replays, and replays count as watch time.
3. Likes matter less than everyone thinks. Don't ask for likes.

## Format rules
- **Length:** 15–35 seconds. Under 15s only for pure loop clips.
- **First frame:** motion already happening + on-screen hook text
  (≤10 words, big, high contrast). No logos, no intros.
- **Captions burned in** — most people watch muted.
- **Cover frame:** person + counter visible (grid consistency).

## Instagram SEO (free reach — do all of these once, then per post)
Instagram searches captions, on-screen text, **spoken words**, bio, and
display name. So:
- Display name: **"Buzzend | AI Home Workout Counter"** (one-time setup).
- Bio line 1 = the positioning sentence + "Try your first AI workout ⬇".
- Caption line 1 carries the keyword naturally: *"AI squat counter for
  home workouts — it counted 47, I counted 45."*
- **Say the keyword out loud** in the first 3 seconds when natural
  ("my phone's counting my squats") — spoken audio is indexed.
- Hashtags: **3–5, specific** (#homeworkout #beginnerfitness #repcounter
  #aifitness + one exercise tag). More is noise; stuffing helps nothing.

## Posting
- **2–4 Reels/week + Stories on most weekdays.** Posting more is allowed
  (no penalty) but never at the cost of quality.
- Fixed time daily (set from our own audience data after week 1).

## Stories playbook (Person 3, ~10 min/day)
- Reshare the day's Reel to Story.
- 2–3×/week: a **poll** or **question sticker** (lowest-effort, highest
  tap-rate stickers; replies and sticker taps are strong ranking signals).
  Examples: "Squats or push-ups next?" / "What number do you lose count at?"
- Story with **link sticker** → "Try your first AI workout ⬇" (one CTA
  per frame, never two).
- Use ready statics from `../../social-media/rendered/` (`day1–7-story.png`).

## Growth tactics unlocked for us right now
- **Comment-to-DM automation** (ManyChat or similar, Meta-approved):
  "Comment SQUAT and I'll DM you the link." Comments boost ranking AND
  the link lands in DMs where it actually gets tapped. Set up in week 1.
- **Collab posts:** any video made with/featuring another creator gets
  posted as a collab (both audiences see it). Ask every micro-creator we
  talk to.
- **Meaningful comments on big fitness accounts** (15 min/day, Person 3):
  genuinely useful replies under big beginner-fitness posts put our name
  in front of the right crowd.
- **Trial Reels** (test on non-followers only): unlocks at 1,000
  followers — start using it the day we cross 1k.
- Editing: use CapCut or Meta's **Edits** app (free, has teleprompter +
  templates + analytics).

## What success looks like (don't panic early)
- Fitness Reels engagement norm: ~3–4% (small accounts often 5–9%).
- Hook rate (3s views ÷ impressions): aim ≥30%.
- Early videos getting 200–1,000 views is NORMAL. We're building a
  library and learning hooks, not chasing one viral hit.
- Bio-link taps: 2–5% of profile visitors is normal.
