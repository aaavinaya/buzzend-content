## Direct lane: camera rep counters — the lane is OPEN

| App | Their positioning | What we learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Zing AI** | "Fitness just got a brain" / AI Coach; camera form-tracking ("Zing Vision") exists but is buried under personalized-plans messaging | Biggest direct rival, and they DON'T lead with the camera. We say the visceral thing plainly: "your camera counts your reps." Don't copy their abstract "AI coach" language |
| **Fitnit** | "Never count reps in your head again"; claims it counts only proper-form reps; on-device privacy | Their form-verified counting claim is strong — our "why didn't this rep count?" content makes the same point *visually*. If our processing is on-device, say it (verify with product first) |
| **GymCam** | "Camera Rep Counting" (Play subtitle) | Proof the plain keyword works in store search; utilitarian, no content presence to fear |
| **Replo** | AI push-up counter; tells users to screen-record counted sets and post them | **Steal the mechanic:** the live counter overlay is itself the content format. Screen-recorded counted sets = our native UGC |

## Adjacent boom: camera-verified reps for screen time
Pushscroll (~$30K/mo, built by two TikTokers), PushUp Time, Pushup
Social, Repscroll, Clearspace (YC), Steppin. They validated that
**camera-verified reps go viral** — but they're anti-doomscrolling
utilities, not fitness apps.
**We learn:** (a) the "can it be cheated?" content angle prints comments
— Pushscroll's cheat-resistance became its selling point; (b) creator
faces beat brand logos (two TikTokers marketing to their own audience);
(c) we can borrow the framing in content ("I made my phone watch me work
out") without pivoting the product.

## Adjacent tech: wearable/hardware counting
Motra ("Strava for lifting", watch-based), Tempo ($495 hardware),
Onyx (no live counting).
**Our wedge, verbatim for content and store copy:** *"No wearable. No
hardware. No manual logging. Just your phone."*

## Big-brand social lessons (not competitors, teachers)

- **Ladder** (+2,000% subs in 2 yrs): coaches became short-form creators;
  humor first; organic winners were later turned into paid ads. → Our
  team appears on camera; winners get scaled first.
- **Gymshark** (6M+ TikTok): lo-fi relatable humor performs at parity
  with studio work. → Home-workout humor archetypes are allowed and
  encouraged.
- **Strava/Runna:** the current wave is *fitness as friendship* — run
  clubs, kudos, belonging. → Our challenges are co-op accountability
  ("finish the week together", "your friends can see if you skipped"),
  not domination.
- **Duolingo:** streaks + an unhinged character carried them to 16M
  followers. → Our character candidate is **the counter itself**: the
  AI that refuses to count sloppy reps, celebrates rep 50, catches your
  half-rep. Give it a consistent voice in captions and on-screen text.
- **Cal AI** (~$30M/yr; camera → calorie count): doctrine — *the product
  must be demonstrable in under 3 seconds on camera*; micro-influencer
  seeding at volume. → We pass the 3-second test natively. Point camera,
  squat, number goes up. Every video proves it again.

## What every competitor does that we will NOT copy

- Leading with "AI-powered" abstraction instead of showing the thing.
- Polished ad-style demos (read as ads, get scrolled).
- Physique-transformation promises (banned words + wrong audience).
- Multi-account clip-farming (throttled + spammy + we lack hands).

## The three sentences that position us against everyone

2. **vs AI coach apps:** "They plan your workout. Buzzend is IN your
   workout — watching, counting, correcting."
3. **vs hardware/wearables:** "No watch. No $500 mirror. Your phone
   already has a camera."
