# The four content pillars

Every piece of content belongs to exactly one pillar. If an idea fits no
pillar, it's off-plan. The four pillars consolidate the strategy's six
(§21) and are validated by the 2026 research (`../01-research/`).

Target mix per month: **P1 ~40% · P2 ~25% · P3 ~25% · P4 ~10%.**

---

## Pillar 1 — PROOF: "The camera really counts" (~40%)

- **Purpose:** demonstrate the product's magic moment; drive installs.
  This is the pillar that converts.
- **Audience:** everyone cold — people who've never heard of us.
- **We talk about:** live rep counting on all 5 exercises · coaching
  cues appearing · trying to cheat the AI (half-reps, pets, siblings) ·
  "what the AI sees" (skeleton overlay) · messy real-world conditions
  (small rooms, bad light) · head-to-head two-phone counts.
- **We do NOT talk about:** accuracy percentages, tech internals,
  competitor bashing, anything the app can't reliably do (§40).
- **Reel/Short ideas:** "I did 30, it says 28 — one of us is wrong" ·
  "Trying to cheat my rep counter" series · "50 squats, AI judged" ·
  full unedited 2-min session experiment · duet a form video with our
  counter running.
- **Carousel idea:** "5 exercises. 1 phone. 0 equipment." (one exercise
  per slide, counter screenshot each).
- **Story ideas:** counter-moment clip + "could you do 50?" poll.
- **Community twist:** ask viewers what to test next ("what should the
  AI count tomorrow?").
- **CTA:** "Try your first AI workout."

## Pillar 2 — CONFIDENCE: beginner & gym-anxiety content (~25%)

- **Purpose:** own the judgment-free at-home lane (shy-girl-workout /
  cozy-cardio energy — huge, underserved, exactly our user).
- **Audience:** At-Home Starters who feel behind, embarrassed, or burned
  out on restarting.
- **We talk about:** starting with 10 · working out where nobody
  watches · "your first workout doesn't need to be perfect" · losing
  count and laughing about it · realistic mini-challenges · the app
  never judging.
- **We do NOT talk about:** body transformations, weight loss promises,
  shame ("still skipping workouts?"), "no excuses" grind culture.
- **Reel ideas:** "The gym for people scared of the gym is your living
  room" · "Nobody claps when you work out alone. Fine — my phone counts
  out loud" · "Day 1 back after 6 months off: 10 squats. Counted." ·
  myth-buster: "'no pain no gain' is why you quit last time."
- **Carousel idea:** "How to actually start again (without hating it)" —
  5 tiny steps, step 5 = try one counted set.
- **Story ideas:** "what number do you lose count at?" question sticker.
- **CTA:** "Try your first AI workout." (gentlest tone)

## Pillar 3 — TOGETHER: streaks, challenges & friends (~25%)

- **Purpose:** show the retention features; fuel the share/invite loop.
  Frame = co-op accountability and belonging, NOT domination (the
  Strava-era insight: fitness is how this generation makes friends).
- **Audience:** Challenge Friends + activated users; warm audience.
- **We talk about:** streak diaries ("day 12 of 30") · friend challenges
  ("the camera keeps score, nobody can lie") · leaderboard moments ·
  "finish the week together" · duet/stitch challenge attempts ·
  celebrating other people's streaks.
- **We do NOT talk about:** punishing stakes, calling out losers,
  75-Hard-style extremity — our challenges are realistic by design.
- **Reel ideas:** "30 realistic days, AI-verified" series · two phones,
  two friends, 60 seconds of squats — AI judges · "my friend swore he
  did 50 push-ups a day. We checked." · streak-save drama ("34 hours
  left, day 21").
- **Story ideas:** weekly challenge poll; repost participants (with
  permission).
- **CTA:** **"Challenge a friend."**
- **Weekend slots belong to this pillar.**

## Pillar 4 — BUILDING: honest behind-the-scenes (~10%)

- **Purpose:** trust, personality, and the tech-curious slice; makes the
  brand human (faces beat logos in every 2026 case study).
- **Audience:** followers, tech-curious, future partners/hires.
- **We talk about:** testing the counter on new moves/people ("my mom,
  58, tested it — 12 for 12") · fixing things users reported · honest
  limitations ("here's when it can't see you — and the fix") · reacting
  to comments on camera.
- **We do NOT talk about:** tech internals (pose landmarks, models),
  accuracy claims, roadmap promises.
- **Reel ideas:** "someone commented 'no way it's real-time'. Okay —
  watch." · "we made the AI stricter. I regret it." · office/home tests
  gone right or wrong (real outcomes only).
- **CTA:** "Try your first AI workout." or the question itself.
