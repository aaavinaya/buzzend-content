# The 3-person team system

Three people, three lanes, working in parallel. Nobody waits on anyone
for more than one handoff per day.

## The three roles

### Person 1 — "The Brain" (Strategy · Research · Analytics)
Owns **what we should make and whether it worked.**

- Audience, competitor, trend, and hook research
- The weekly plan (fills next week's calendar every Friday)
- The metrics sheet + weekly report (`14-weekly-reports/`)
- Decides repeat / change / stop, using the winner rule
- Reviews content before publishing (the approval step)
- Owns: `01-research/`, `02-audience/`, `03-competitors/`,
  `13-analytics/`, `14-weekly-reports/`, `09-content-calendar/`

### Person 2 — "The Maker" (Content · Creative)
Owns **everything the audience sees.**

- Turns calendar slots into ideas → hooks → scripts → filmed/edited video
- Graphics and statics (using `../../brand/design-guide.md` + templates)
- Captions + on-screen text
- Repurposing (one shoot → many cuts, see `08-social-media/repurposing-map.md`)
- Owns: `04-content-pillars/`, `05-hooks/`, `06-content-ideas/`,
  `07-scripts/`, `10-assets/`

### Person 3 — "The Voice" (Publishing · Community · Growth)
Owns **getting content out and talking to humans.**

- Scheduling + native publishing on every platform (see `11-publishing/`)
- Hashtags/keywords per platform
- Replies to EVERY comment and DM (our #1 growth tactic)
- Pastes verbatim audience phrases into the hooks-inbox daily
- Community activities (polls, challenges, reposts) + creator outreach list
- Owns: `08-social-media/`, `11-publishing/`, `12-community/`

**Vacation/absence rule:** each person documents their work so either
other person can run their lane for a week using the playbooks alone.

## Daily rhythm (Mon–Fri, ~2–4 h each)

| Time block | Person 1 (Brain) | Person 2 (Maker) | Person 3 (Voice) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning (30–45 min) | Log yesterday's numbers into the metrics sheet | Check calendar: what's due today + tomorrow | Reply to overnight comments/DMs; paste phrases → hooks-inbox |
| Midday (1–2 h) | Research block (trends, competitors, ideas → backlog) | **Make today's content** (script → film → edit → caption) | Prep today's post (hashtags, caption check, link check) |
| Afternoon | Review Person 2's finished piece (≤30 min turnaround) | Fix review notes; hand off final file + caption | **Publish at the fixed time**; engage 30 min after posting |
| End of day (10 min) | Note one learning in the week's report draft | Update asset register status | Log posting done + any community wins/issues in the calendar row |

**The handoff chain (same every day):**
Person 2 finishes → Person 1 reviews (approve/fix) → Person 3 publishes
→ Person 1 measures next morning. One cycle per day, no pile-ups.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Extra duty on top of daily rhythm |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Team meeting (30 min, agenda below).** P1 presents last week's numbers + this week's calendar. |
| Tue | P2 batch-films: shoot 2–3 videos in one session (cheaper than daily shoots). |
| Wed | P3 community push: poll or question Story; respond to everything. |
| Thu | P2 educational piece; P1 mid-week check — is anything flopping hard? |
| Fri | P1 writes the weekly report + fills next week's calendar. P2 stocks 5 new hooks in the bank. P3 sends 2–3 outreach messages (creators/communities). |
| Weekend | One scheduled community post (pre-made Friday). Rotate who covers replies (30 min/day max). |

## The Monday meeting agenda (30 minutes, never longer)

1. **Numbers (P1, 10 min):** last week's report — best post, worst post,
   store visits, installs. One sentence each on why.
2. **Decisions (10 min):** what do we repeat, change, stop this week?
   (P1 proposes, team agrees — decisions get written into the calendar.)
3. **This week (5 min):** walk the calendar; confirm every slot has an
   owner and nothing blocks anyone.
4. **Blockers + one experiment (5 min):** anything stuck? Pick exactly
   one new thing to test this week (a hook category, a format, a time).

## Monthly review (last Friday, 60 min)

- P1 presents the month: all four weekly reports side by side.
- Answer the three questions: Who responded? What message worked? Did
  they actually use Buzzend?
- Fill the month-review template (`16-templates/`): worked / didn't /
  continue / stop / test next.
- Reset targets for next month based on OUR real numbers (not industry
  fantasy numbers).

## Approvals — what needs one, what doesn't

**Needs Person 1 approval before publishing (5–30 min turnaround, async):**
- Every video/post (the 6-point sanity check in
  `00-strategy/why-people-stop-scrolling.md`)
- Anything mentioning a competitor, a real user, or a number claim
- Outreach messages to creators (first message of each new thread)

**Needs NO approval (just do it):**
- Replying to comments/DMs per the community playbook
- Reposting our own content as Stories
- Adding ideas/hooks to the backlogs
- Fixing typos, re-scheduling within the same day
- Research and analytics work

**Escalate to founders (rare):**
- Anything legal/claims-adjacent, partnerships with money involved,
  press/PR requests, anything involving minors, negative-PR moments.

## Rules that keep three beginners fast

1. **Done beats perfect.** Ship the 80% version; the learning loop fixes
   the rest.
2. **One owner per task.** If two people own it, nobody owns it — the
   calendar's Owner column is law.
3. **Write it down or it didn't happen.** Decisions live in the calendar
   and weekly reports, not in chat memory.
4. **Never block on a missing person.** If P1 is unavailable for review
   >2 hours, P3 may publish content that passes the 6-point check and
   flags it for retro-review.
5. **Disagreements get 5 minutes, then a test.** Don't debate hooks —
   post both versions a week apart and let the numbers decide.
