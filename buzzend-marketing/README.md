# Buzzend Marketing Operating System — START HERE

This folder is the complete marketing system for a **3-person beginner
team**. No marketing knowledge is assumed anywhere inside it. Built
2026-08-23 from (a) the approved Buzzend strategy, (b) fresh research on
the 2026 social landscape, and (c) an audit of everything we already have.

## Your first day (all three of you, ~2 hours)

Read, in this order:
1. `00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` — what we sell, the exact words we use
2. `02-audience/who-we-talk-to.md` — the one person we make content for
3. `15-team-workflow/team-system.md` — your role and your daily rhythm
4. `00-strategy/why-people-stop-scrolling.md` — the 12 levers + our rules
5. Your lane's manual:
   - Person 1: `13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md` + `01-research/`
   - Person 2: `05-hooks/hook-system.md` + `07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md` + `04-content-pillars/pillars.md`
   - Person 3: `11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md` + `12-community/community-playbook.md`

Then open `09-content-calendar/calendar.md` — Week 1 starts with a team
meeting and the setup checklist. That's Monday morning sorted.

## How the system runs (one paragraph)

Person 1 fills next week's calendar every Friday and reads the numbers
every morning. Person 2 turns calendar slots into hooks → scripts →
videos (batch-filming Tuesdays). Person 3 publishes at fixed times,
answers every comment, and feeds the audience's exact words back into
the hook bank. Monday's 30-minute meeting turns last week's numbers into
this week's decisions: repeat, change, stop. Everything is judged by the
**winner rule** — store visits → installs → first workouts, never likes.

## The five rules that outrank everything

1. **Never fake a rep count** (test the exercise on the exact phone
   before filming — it's in every checklist).
2. **No banned words**: perfect form, guaranteed, world's first,
   medical-grade, replaces your trainer (full list in `00-strategy/`).
3. **Never market to children.**
4. **Only `assets/screens-clean/` imagery in public** — never raw device
   screenshots.
5. **One CTA per piece:** "Try your first AI workout." (or "Challenge a
   friend." on challenge content).
