# Who we talk to

Every piece of content is made for ONE imaginary person. Here she/he is.

## The At-Home Starter (our main person)

- **Age:** roughly 18–35.
- **Fitness level:** beginner, or "used to work out, stopped".
- **Where:** at home — bedroom, living room, small space. No equipment.
- **Phone:** always in hand. Finds everything through TikTok/Reels/Shorts.
- **Wants:** to get consistent, feel stronger, see visible progress —
  without a gym, a trainer, or a complicated program.

**Their real frustrations (use these words in content):**
- "I lose count halfway through."
- "I don't know where to start."
- "Am I even doing this right?"
- "I always quit after a week."
- "I don't want anyone watching me at the gym."
- "Workout plans are too complicated."

**Moments they're ready to install (make content for these moments):**
new year / new goal · starting again after stopping · saw a fitness
video and felt motivated · a friend challenged them · decided to work
out at home to save money/time.

## The quick test for any content idea

Ask: **"Would the At-Home Starter stop scrolling for this?"**
If the honest answer is no — drop the idea, even if it's clever.

## Secondary people (okay to occasionally target)

| Person | Their situation | The one message for them |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge Friend** | Loves competition, streaks, beating friends | "Don't just work out. Challenge someone." |
| **Busy Professional** | No time for gym logistics | "No gym. No equipment. No counting. Just move." |
| **Returning Exerciser** | Keeps starting and stopping | "Start small. Build the habit. Keep the streak." |

## Who we do NOT make content for (not now)

Advanced athletes · serious gym lifters · nutrition/diet audiences ·
medical or rehab users · personal trainers · schools · **children**
(never market to kids — this is a hard company rule with legal weight).

If a content idea mainly appeals to any of these groups, it's off-plan —
park it in the idea backlog with a note, don't make it.
