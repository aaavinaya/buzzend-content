# Content templates

Copy the block you need, fill it in, done. Don't improve the templates
mid-week — propose changes at the Monday meeting.

---

## 3 · Reel/Short/TikTok script

```markdown
ID:
Length target: 15–30 s
HOOK (0–2 s) — spoken + on-screen text (≤10 words) + what's VISIBLE:
  Say: "..."
  Screen text: "..."
  Visual: person + counter, counter moving
PROOF (2–6 s) — what we show:
  (wide shot, reps happening, counter climbing — real count!)
EXPLANATION (6–15 s) — one sentence max:
  Say: "Buzzend uses your phone camera to count reps while you work out."
BENEFIT (15–25 s):
  Say: "So you can focus on moving instead of counting."
CTA (last 2–3 s) — end card + spoken:
  "Try your first AI workout."
Notes: (location, outfit, camera position, which phone tested per §40)
```

## 4 · Carousel (feed post, 5–7 slides)

```markdown
ID:
Slide 1 (the hook slide — must work standalone): "..."
Slides 2–5 (ONE idea per slide, ≤20 words each):
Slide 6/7 (recap + CTA): "Try your first AI workout — link in bio."
Design: use social-media/templates/ + brand/design-guide.md rules
```

## 5 · Caption

```markdown
Line 1 (shows before "...more" — restate the hook, no hashtags here):
Lines 2–4 (the value: what they'll get / the story / the answer):
CTA line: "Try your first AI workout — link in bio."
Keywords to work in naturally (platform SEO): counting reps, home workout,
  AI workout, [exercise name]
Hashtags: (per 11-publishing/ platform rules — few and specific)
```

## 6 · CTA picker

| Situation | CTA (exact words) |
|---|---|
| Default — any product/education content | "Try your first AI workout." |
| Challenge/competition content | "Challenge a friend." |
| Community/engagement post | The question itself ("Which number do you lose count at? 👇") |
| Story with link sticker | "Try your first AI workout ⬇" |

One CTA per piece. Never stack "follow + comment + download".
