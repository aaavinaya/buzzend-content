# Outreach templates (creators & collabs)

Written for Avinaya's Day-8 lane. Rewrite the [bracketed] parts per person —
if two sent messages look alike, rewrite one. One ask per message, no payment
promises, no hype words.

## 15 · Creator first DM (IG/TikTok, 60–90 words)

> Hey [name] — your [specific recent post, e.g. "wall-sit challenge from
> Tuesday"] was great, [one genuine line about why].
> We're a tiny team building Buzzend: your phone camera counts your reps
> out loud while you work out — squats, push-ups, jumping jacks — and
> friends compete on camera-verified challenges.
> Would you try it? If it feels like a fit, we'll build a challenge with
> your name on it and point our audience at it. No strings — honest
> feedback is enough. [store link from sprint/config.js]

## 16 · Single follow-up (3+ days later, once, then stop)

> Hey [name], floating this once more in case it got buried — the rep-counter
> app with camera-verified friend challenges. Totally fine if it's not for
> you; if you're curious I'll set you up in 2 minutes. Either way, keep
> making [their content type] — [genuine half-line].

## 17 · Collab agreement (paste in chat once they say yes)

> Amazing! Here's the whole deal, no fine print:
> 1. We create the "[Name]'s [Exercise] Challenge" in Buzzend this week.
> 2. You post about it whenever suits you — your words, no script from us.
> 3. We feature the challenge + your handle to our audience all week.
> 4. Anything you want changed or removed, say the word.
> When works for you to kick it off?

Rules recap: creators 1k–50k in home-workout/bodyweight niche · engagement
eyeball-check ≥3% (likes ÷ followers on recent posts) · one follow-up max ·
log every send with date + status in the tracker Outreach tab.
