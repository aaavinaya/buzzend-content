## Task 1 · 🔥 Batch-film #3 — 6 takes built on what weeks 1–2 of sprint 2 proved (100–120 min)

**Why:** Two weeks of sprint-2 data now exist — the doubled cadence, the experiments, the contest. The last batch of the sprint shouldn't film guesses; it films what the numbers just taught us, while there's still sprint left to use it.
**Where:** Your filming spot, one setup for the whole session.
**Tools:** Phone (Buzzend + camera), prop, the in-app analytics + tracker numbers, the Experiments log, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Read the numbers yourself before planning a single shot: open the in-app analytics and the tracker and rank sprint-2 posts so far by store visits (then saves if unclear — never likes). Check the Experiments log too: if a hook experiment already has a readable verdict, mark it WON/LOST in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` now. Write 3 sentences in the tracker: what format won weeks 1–2, which hook(s) won, what flopped.
2. Plan 6 takes that double down on those 3 sentences: winning format × winning hooks × the exercises that performed, plus at most 1 wildcard extending a winner to an untried feature (Beat-It, streaks, leaderboard moments — whatever the winners point at). Every take traces to a number you can point to.
3. Scripts before camera: 60–80 words each, fixed beats, read aloud twice, banned words out.
4. §40 pre-flight per exercise on this exact phone in this position; swap and flag any miscount, never film around it.
5. Film all 6 back-to-back in the one setup: wide PROOF + close hook take per concept, real reps, real counts, plus each concept's story moment and alternate-hook take per the batching rule in `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/production-pipeline.md`.
6. Upload to `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch3/` labeled `<concept>-<shot>-take<N>.mp4`, log the take list in the tracker with, per concept, the number that justified it ("extends day18 challenge reel — top store visits wk2").

**Deliverable:** 6 justified, logged takes in `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch3/` + the 3-sentence weeks-1–2 verdict in the tracker.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Weekly UGC feature edit (50–60 min)

**Why:** Featuring entries weekly is the contest's heartbeat — participants who get featured post again and bring friends. Regular beats big: one good feature every week outworks a compilation nobody was promised.
**Where:** CapCut; entries via the tracker's contest row.
**Tools:** CapCut, the tracker (permission column — Riya marks CONFIRMED), `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md` (Pillar 3 tone), checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Pick this week's feature from CONFIRMED rows only — the entry with the most genuine moment (a real struggle-then-count beat outshines a big number). Unconfirmed entries stay untouched.
2. Cut 10–15s: their clip framed on **#ff6b00 / #fff8f2**, handle credited big, their count exactly as posted, one warm line, CTA "Challenge a friend."
3. If no confirmed entry exists this week: cut a 15s "community so far" montage from ONLY previously-featured (already-permitted) material plus your own Day-23 streak clip, honest text about where the contest actually stands ("<N> entries so far — join them"). Real number from the tracker.
4. Captions proofed, 1080×1920, cover, self-check against checklist #7.
5. Drop in **READY/** as `day25-countmyreps-feature-<handle>.mp4` (or `day25-countmyreps-week-recap.mp4`), log the path and branch taken in the tracker. Avinaya reviews async.
