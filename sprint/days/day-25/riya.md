# Day 25 — Riya

**Objective:** The contest keeps its promise: the first weekly #CountMyReps feature goes live — permissioned, credited, their exact words — alongside the day's two posts, and the engagement loop stays tight.
**Total time:** ~2.5–3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + weekly UGC feature #1 (90–110 min)

**Why:** "Best entry gets featured" only builds trust if the feature actually appears, on time, done beautifully. A stranger's real counted set converts better than anything we produce — and featuring them well recruits next week's entrants.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows + the feature post; entrant's thread; Growth Tracker; shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone, the Day-23 entry log in the tracker, Canva (the sprint-1 "UGC quote card" / "UGC video frame" templates from Day 13), `brand/design-guide.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md`, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe (READY/ → `social-media/rendered/` fallback → same-day reschedule; caption self-check; URLs into `Social`).
2. Pick the week's best entry from the Day-23 entry log — only entries with a **saved written yes** qualify, no matter how good an unpermissioned one looks. If zero permissioned entries exist yet, feature the week's best community moment instead (a great comment, asked for permission the same way) and push the contest again in a story — the weekly feature slot never goes empty.
3. Build the feature: Aditya's edit from **READY/** if one is there; otherwise fill the sprint-1 Canva UGC templates yourself — the entrant's **exact words**, never paraphrased, and their counter moment never covered by overlays. Energy Orange, one CTA.
4. Message the entrant personally before it goes live ("you're this week's feature — going up at 6pm, tagging you"), then publish with first name + handle and a genuine thank-you in the caption.
5. Stay ~30 min replying; pin the entrant's comment if they leave one.
6. Log the feature URL in `Social`, mark the entry `Featured` in the tracker log, and post a story: "entries stay open — next feature next week."

**Deliverable:** 2 calendar posts + UGC feature #1 live, entrant told first, everything logged.
**Done when:** The feature is live with logged written permission, exact words, and credit — and the entrant knew before the world did.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 23 · Task 1 (the entry log — the step-2 fallback covers an empty one).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Engagement hour (45–60 min)

**Why:** Feature days spike profile visits from the entrant's own circle — fast, warm replies convert that curiosity while it lasts.
**Where:** IG, TikTok, YouTube, FB — all live posts and the niche around them.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Reply to **100%** of comments across all live posts — short, warm, human; pin the best per post.
2. Answer every DM within the day; anyone asking how to enter gets the 3-line how-to from `countmyreps-contest.md`.
3. Leave 10 genuine comments on niche accounts — helpful, zero app plugs, no links.
4. Paste standout audience phrasings into the hooks-inbox in `hook-bank.md`.
5. Log counts + anything that popped in the `Social` tab.

**Deliverable:** All comments and DMs answered, 10 genuine outbound comments, notes logged.
**Done when:** Nothing on today's posts sat unanswered longer than 2 hours.
