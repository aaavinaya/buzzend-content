# Day 19 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Bank more genuine community credit in the places that already welcome us, and turn five real challenge users into honest review opportunities.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Community value round — 3 subreddit answers + 1 Discord answer (75–90 min)

**Why:** Sprint 1 earned us standing in a handful of communities; Sprint 2 spends nothing it hasn't earned. Helpful answers are the deposit — mentions only where the rules allow, always disclosed.
**Where:** The subreddits and Discord servers ranked in `buzzend-marketing/12-community/where-were-welcome.md`.
**Tools:** Reddit + Discord accounts, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/where-were-welcome.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Open `where-were-welcome.md` and work only the top-ranked rows — the communities where our contributions actually landed in Sprint 1.
2. Re-skim each community's rules (2 min each) before posting anything — rules change, and one strike undoes weeks of goodwill.
3. Write 3 genuinely helpful subreddit answers to real beginner questions (staying consistent, counting reps, home setups, starting from zero). Answer the question fully first; the answer must stand on its own with Buzzend deleted from it.
4. Write 1 answer in the Discord server ranked highest — same standard.
5. Mention Buzzend ONLY where that community's row says mentions are allowed, only when it genuinely fits the question, and always with disclosure: "I helped build it." No links where links aren't welcome.
6. Log each answer in the tracker next to the Sprint-1 community notes: community, thread, what you answered, whether Buzzend came up, any replies.

**Deliverable:** 4 logged answers (3 Reddit + 1 Discord), every mention rule-checked and disclosed.
**Done when:** All 4 answers are live and logged, and any Buzzend mention sits in a community whose `where-were-welcome.md` row allows it.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Ask 5 NEW engaged challenge participants for honest reviews (45–60 min)

**Why:** Review velocity is a store ranking factor, and the only reviews worth having come from people genuinely working out with the app. Sprint 1 asked our first users; today we ask five people we haven't asked before.
**Where:** Buzzend app (challenge leaderboards) + wherever we already talk to each person; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Open the challenge leaderboards and list participants with 3+ days of camera-counted workouts — genuine, active users only.
2. Cross-check the `Outreach` tab: strike anyone already asked on Day 7 or Day 13. Today's five are NEW names.
3. Anyone on the list you've never actually spoken with gets a warm conversation first, not a review ask — never ask strangers.
4. Message each of the 5 individually, by name, referencing their real progress. The ask in your own words each time: "If you have 2 minutes, an honest review on the store would genuinely help a small team. Whatever you really think."
5. Hard rules: no incentives of any kind, no asking for 5 stars, no follow-up nagging. One honest ask, then let it go.
6. Log each ask in `Outreach`: name, days active in the challenge, date asked, where you know them from.

**Deliverable:** 5 individual, honest review asks sent to new people, all logged in `Outreach`.
**Done when:** Every ask has an `Outreach` row showing the person is new (not asked in Sprint 1) and 3+ days active.
