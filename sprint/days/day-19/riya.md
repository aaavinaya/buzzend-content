## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + activate collab #1 (90–110 min)

**Why:** A creator saying "yes" is worth nothing until it has a date. Template #17 exists precisely so a warm reply becomes a scheduled launch in one message instead of a week of vague back-and-forth.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows; the creator's DM thread; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab; shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (template #17), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (#5, #6), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe: assets from **READY/**, fallback `social-media/rendered/` or same-day reschedule — never skip; caption self-check (one CTA, no banned words); URLs + times into the `Social` tab.
2. Check DM replies: any creator row at `Replied` or `Interested`? Answer everyone warmly first — speed is respect.
3. For the first genuine yes, paste template #17 into the thread and make it concrete: launch date **this week** · the exact challenge name we'll create ("[Name]'s [Exercise] Challenge" — one of the five counted exercises, camera-verified, nothing the app can't already do) · what we post (announcement with their handle + a week of featuring) · how their audience joins (the exact name to search in Discover; include a join code or link only if the app actually shows one — mirror the app, never invent).
4. Update their `Outreach` row: Status = `Agreed`, date, one-line terms. Write the one-page collab plan (date, challenge name, join instruction, our post plan) and drop a copy in **READY/** for Avinaya's async review — the launch proceeds regardless; review notes become follow-ups.
5. If no yes has landed yet: spend the time answering every creator reply, and pre-write the collab plan skeleton so a future yes activates in 10 minutes. Log statuses either way.
6. Note the agreed go-live date on the Day-24 THEMED calendar row.

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live + either an `Agreed` row with the collab plan in READY/, or every creator reply answered and the plan skeleton ready.
**Done when:** Both post URLs are logged and either a collab has a concrete date this week or no warm reply sits unanswered.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Send 10 more creator DMs (60–75 min)

**Why:** Twenty sends across two days is the volume that reliably produces 2–3 real conversations — and today finishes the batch while yesterday's momentum (and your research) is fresh.
**Where:** Instagram/TikTok DMs from the Buzzend account; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (template #15), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Take the remaining 10 uncontacted batch-2 rows. If fewer than 10 remain, research replacements yourself (Day-16 vetting rules: 1k–50k, home-workout/bodyweight, ≥3% engagement eyeball-check) and continue.
2. Watch each creator's 2–3 most recent posts; rewrite template #15 fresh per person — specific recent post in line one, 60–90 words, one ask, no two first lines alike.
3. Read each back once; cut hype words and second asks. No payment offers.
4. Send spaced through the afternoon; log Date + `Sent` per row.
5. Check replies this evening; flag anything warm for tomorrow's activation window.

**Deliverable:** 10 more personalized DMs sent and logged — batch #2 fully contacted.
**Done when:** All 20 batch-2 rows show `Sent` (or a later status) with dates.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 16 · Task 2 (candidate rows — fallback in step 1 if the bench is short).
