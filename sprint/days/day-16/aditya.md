## Task 1 · 🔥 Batch-film #1 — 6 takes of the proven winning format (100–120 min)

**Why:** Sprint 2 is SCALE: Riya publishes twice a day now, and two posts a day only works if filming happens in batches, not per-post. Sprint 1 proved the machine — today you film a half-week of raw material in one setup, in the format the numbers already picked.
**Where:** Your sprint-1 filming spot, one setup for the whole session.
**Tools:** Phone (Buzzend + camera), prop, `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/next-sprint-video-bank.md` (your Day-15 ranked bank), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`, `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/content-production-sop.md` (your own SOP — follow it), shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Name the winning format yourself before touching the camera: open the in-app analytics and the tracker and check which sprint-1 posts drove the most store visits (store visits beat likes; if unclear, most saves). If the numbers are ambiguous, the default stands: real person on camera + the counter genuinely counting. Note your pick and the numbers you read in the tracker.
2. Pick 3 concepts in that format from ranks 1–5 of `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/next-sprint-video-bank.md`, spread across squats, push-ups, and jumping jacks — one exercise each. Write the three scripts BEFORE filming (60–80 spoken words, the beat structure from `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`, read aloud twice, banned words out).
3. §40 pre-flight per exercise on this exact phone in this exact position: a quick test set, confirm the counter counts right. An exercise that miscounts gets swapped for sit-ups or lunges and flagged in the tracker — never filmed around.
4. Film all 6 takes back-to-back in the one setup: per concept, one wide PROOF take (full body + phone + counter readable) and one counter close-up hook take. Real reps, real counts, vertical 9:16, counter moving by second 2 on the close-ups (the universal rule in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md`).
5. Watch every take back at phone size; re-shoot anything where the count isn't readable or the body leaves frame — this batch feeds three days of posts.
6. Upload to the shared drive under `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch1/`, labeled `<exercise>-<concept>-<wide|close>-take<N>.mp4`, and log the take list in the tracker: concept · hook · exercise · files · verified count · best take starred.

**Deliverable:** 6 labeled, verified takes in `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch1/` + a starred take log in the tracker naming the winning format and the numbers behind it.
**Done when:** Someone else could cut three videos from the take log alone, and every counter shown counted real reps on your exact phone.
