## Task 1 · 🔥 Build the sprint-2 content calendar — 2 posts/day for days 17–30 (100–120 min)

**Why:** Sprint 1 got us 4–5 new users a day on roughly one post a day; doubling the cadence is the cheapest scale lever we have at $0 — but only if every slot is decided today, so publishing days become pure execution instead of daily invention.
**Where:** New file `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md` + a copy in the shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Laptop, Growth Tracker, each platform's own analytics, `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md`, `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Re-read the sprint-1 winners: per-post reach and saves you read **directly in each app's analytics**, the WON hooks in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, and your Day-14 ranked list in the tracker. If a sprint readout sits in **READY/** or `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/`, use it as a cross-check — never wait for it.
2. Create `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`: one row per slot, 2 slots per day for days 17–30 (28 rows). Columns: Day · Platform · Slot time · Source hook · Asset source · Status.
3. Slot times come from the publishing playbook's fixed times — slot 1 at the IG window (~11:30–12:30), slot 2 at the TikTok/Shorts window (18:00–21:00). Same time every day; consistency beats cleverness.
4. Fill **Source hook** winners-first: every WON hook gets at least two variations across our five counted exercises (squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges), then pull from the `next-sprint` tagged rows in `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md` — mark each idea used, never delete.
5. **Asset source** per row is a pair: the file you expect Aditya to drop in **READY/**, AND a named fallback you can ship alone (a specific `social-media/rendered/` file, or "Canva graphic — self-made"). Every row must be publishable by you solo.
6. Mark the themed rows this sprint already owns so ordinary ideas don't take them: Day-18 challenge-week launch, Day-22 #CountMyReps launch, Day-24 collab announcement, Day-30 thanks post.
7. Drop a copy in **READY/** for Avinaya's async review — publishing starts from this calendar tomorrow regardless; any review note becomes a calendar edit or a follow-up, never a hold.
8. Log the calendar path in the tracker `Content` notes.

**Deliverable:** `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md` with all 28 rows filled + a copy in READY/.
**Done when:** Every day 17–30 has 2 rows with platform, fixed time, hook, asset source AND fallback — and the READY/ copy exists.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Build creator outreach batch #2 — 20 new candidates (60–75 min)

**Why:** Sprint 1 proved personalized DMs get replies; a vetted bench of 20 means this week's two DM days never burn sending time on research.
**Where:** Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab; IG/TikTok search; `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`.
**Tools:** Phone or laptop, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (rules recap at the bottom).

**Steps:**
1. Source candidates two ways: creators who are genuinely active in the communities mapped in `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md` (if the map is thin, skip to search), and platform search for *home workout, beginner fitness, 30 day challenge, bodyweight* — the growwithjo/MadFit archetype from the community playbook, never shredded gym elites.
2. Vet each against the rules recap in `outreach-templates.md`: 1k–50k followers, home-workout/bodyweight niche, engagement eyeball-check ≥3% (likes ÷ followers on recent posts), real comments under their posts.
3. Scan the existing `Outreach` rows first — a batch-2 candidate must be genuinely new, not a sprint-1 contact under a different spelling.
4. One row each: Name · Handle · Platform · Followers · Niche note · Why they fit (one specific line naming a recent post) · Status = `Candidate`.
5. Star the 10 you'd DM first — tomorrow's batch pulls starred rows top-down.

**Deliverable:** 20 new `Candidate` rows in the Outreach tab, 10 starred.
**Done when:** All 20 rows carry a "why they fit" line naming a specific recent post, and none duplicates a sprint-1 row.
