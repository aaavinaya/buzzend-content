## Task 1 · 🔥 Build the `Sprint2-Results` tab — every metric vs the Day-16 baseline (100–120 min)

**Why:** Avinaya reviews the sprint tomorrow, async, from this tab alone. If a number is missing or fuzzy, the review is fuzzy — today's job is completeness and honesty, not interpretation.
**Where:** play.google.com/console + App Store Connect + browser + Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Play Console, ASC, https://winjee-dev.firebaseio.com/sprint/links.json, Growth Tracker (`Sprint2-Baseline`, `Daily Numbers`, `Social`, `Experiments`), `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Create a new tracker tab named exactly `Sprint2-Results`, dated today, structured as three columns per metric: **Baseline (Day 16) · Now (Day 28) · Delta** — mirror the `Sprint2-Baseline` tab line for line so nothing gets skipped.
2. **Installs/day both stores:** Days 16–27 dailies from the consoles (fill any `Daily Numbers` gaps while you're in there), plus the Sprint-2 average/day vs the Sprint-1 average.
3. **Conversion rate both stores:** the Days 16–27 window vs the baseline window.
4. **Search terms:** today's Play search-terms snapshot next to the Day-16 one; count of GREW/NEW terms as of the Day-24 marks.
5. **Reviews:** current count + rating both stores vs baseline.
6. **Per-post reach:** Sprint-2 posts from the `Social` tab, same compact table as the baseline, plus totals.
7. **Link clicks:** `/go/ios` + `/go/android` totals from links.json vs the Day-16 numbers.
8. **Challenges:** Squad Squats participants/active vs launch, and 7-Day Squat Starter joins over the window. **Experiment:** the Play experiment's current per-variant numbers (or Play's verdict if it declared one) copied from the console into the row.
9. Every cell gets a number or an explicit "no data — why". Then write a short completeness note for **READY/**, dated "Day 28 — Sprint2-Results ready for review": what's complete, what's missing and why, and which 2 numbers deserve the hardest look tomorrow. Avinaya reviews async — nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** A complete `Sprint2-Results` tab + the completeness note in READY/.
**Done when:** Every baseline metric has its Day-28 counterpart and delta (or an explicit gap note), and READY/ has the dated note.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 16 · Task 1 (the baseline this tab is measured against).
