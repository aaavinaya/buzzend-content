# Day 15 — Riya

**Objective:** Point the creative engine at the next campaign with a one-page brief, and close the sprint in public — honest numbers out, thank-yous to the real people who showed up.
**Total time:** ~3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Write the next-campaign creative brief (70–90 min)

**Why:** The retro sets goals; this brief is how content actually hits them. One page, written while the learnings are hot, means next sprint's Maker never faces a blank calendar.
**Where:** New file `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/next-campaign-brief.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, your Day-14 winners bank + 10 ideas, `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/planning-templates.md` (template #10's calendar row format).

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/next-campaign-brief.md` — hard limit one page.
2. **Theme:** name the winning pillar from your Day-14 ranking (whichever provably moved store visits) and one sentence on why the numbers say so.
3. **5 post concepts:** pull the strongest 5 of your 10 `next-sprint` ideas from `06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md`. Each: hook (from the bank) + format + which winner it extends.
4. **1 new challenge concept — existing functionality only:** a camera-verified challenge built from what the app already does (the five counted exercises — squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges — plus streaks, leaderboards, Beat-It, discover). Something like a lunge or push-up follow-up to the Squat Starter. No new features, no product asks; if an idea needs the app to change, it goes to Avinaya's future-ideas doc instead.
5. **Asset needs:** what must exist before day 1 — footage to batch-film, Canva templates (your UGC templates count), any `assets/screens-clean/` renders.
6. **Rough calendar:** 2 weeks of rows in template #10's format (Day · Platform · Pillar · Topic · Hook direction · Format · CTA · Owner) — rough is fine, owners can shift at the Monday meeting.
7. Link the brief in team chat and in the tracker; flag it as the input for the first post-sprint Monday meeting.

**Deliverable:** One-page `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/next-campaign-brief.md`.
**Done when:** The brief answers theme, 5 concepts, 1 challenge, assets, calendar — and still fits one page.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 14 · Task 1 (the winners bank picks the theme).

## Task 2 · 🔥 Publish the "first 15 days" post and the thank-you stories (75–90 min)

**Why:** This is the sprint's public ending: the honest numbers post builds the audience that watches sprint two, and personally thanking the people who joined makes them the community every future challenge starts from.
**Where:** Instagram (+ Facebook cross-post), TikTok if the carousel translates, Stories.
**Tools:** Phone, your Day-14 approved draft, final numbers from `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-readout.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md` (checklist #8), permissions log from the tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Open your Day-14 draft and replace every `[X]` placeholder with the final corrected numbers from the readout. Double-check each against the doc — a wrong public number costs more trust than a bad post.
2. Run publishing checklist #8: native file, caption first line restates the hook, ONE CTA, link in bio tapped and tested on a phone, cover frame set.
3. Publish at the fixed slot. Stay 30–60 minutes replying to every comment — honest-numbers posts attract questions; answer them all warmly.
4. Then the thank-you stories: one story thanking early users, one celebrating challenge finishers — first names ONLY for people whose permission is logged in the tracker (Day-12/13 threads). Anyone not cleared gets a collective thank-you, not a name. Use the framed leaderboard visuals from Gaurab's Day-13 recordings — never the raw recording.
5. Reshare the main post to Stories with a "we read every reply" sticker or question box; paste notable responses into the hooks-inbox in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.
6. Log everything in the tracker: live URLs, publish times, and mark the Day-14 draft PUBLISHED so Gaurab can archive it today.

**Deliverable:** "First 15 days" post live with final numbers + thank-you stories up + URLs logged.
**Done when:** Post and stories are live, every named person had logged permission, and the tracker row says PUBLISHED.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 14 · Task 2 (the approved draft) — and Avinaya · Day 14 · Task 1 for the final numbers.
