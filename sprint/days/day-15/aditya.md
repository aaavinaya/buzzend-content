## Task 1 · 🔥 Write the Content Production SOP from your proven workflow (90–110 min)

**Why:** "End the 15 days with a repeatable process" was the sprint's goal from day one. This is the production-side twin of the publishing SOP — how content ACTUALLY got made these 13 days, so next sprint (or the next Maker) films on day one instead of re-learning your lessons.
**Where:** New file `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/content-production-sop.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, your Day-2 setup notes + tracker indexes, `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/production-pipeline.md` (the 14 stages — note where reality differed), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md` (checklist #7).

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/content-production-sop.md`. Open with one line: "This is how content was ACTUALLY made for 15 days — update it whenever reality changes."
2. **Gear:** the real kit — which phone, what you prop it against, the light that worked, CapCut + Canva, where the shared drive folders live (`buzzend-footage/master-set/`, `day8-batch/`, `reactions/`, `READY/`).
3. **Filming checklist:** the never-fake-a-rep rule first (§40 pre-flight: test the exact exercise on the exact phone in the exact position — a wrong counter gets reported, never filmed around), then your setup notes made law: framing, phone height/angle per exercise, counter readable at phone size, full body in frame, vertical 9:16, two takes (wide + close), real reps only.
4. **Edit steps:** script before camera (60–80 words, the beat structure; the Maker writes their own in 10 minutes from the hook bank if none is in READY/) → hook on frame 1 (≤10 words, counter moving by second 2) → auto-captions proofed → 15–30s → one CTA end card → clean 1080×1920 export, no watermark → cover frame (person + counter).
5. **Delivery format:** file naming (`dayN-<what>-master.mp4` + platform variants), where masters land (READY/), the tracker index columns, and the one-master-many-children rule (story cut + alternate hook from every shoot).
6. **The no-waiting rule, written down:** the Maker finishes the piece, self-checks it against the review card (checklist #7 — real counts on the exact phone, one CTA, no banned words), and drops the master in READY/ named `dayN-<what>` — Avinaya verifies async the same day and fixes ship as follow-ups; nothing in the lane ever waits on an approval, a script, or another person's capture (missing inputs get filmed/recorded by the Maker on their own device; raw captures are always framed, never published raw). Batch-filming day rhythm (one setup, 2–3 scripts) included.
7. Keep it under 2 pages, link `publishing-sop.md` where your process ends and publishing begins (READY/ master → live post), then commit, push, and link it in the tracker + team chat before the retro.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/content-production-sop.md` — the repeatable-process deliverable of the content lane.
**Done when:** A new Maker could deliver a self-checked, genuinely-counted Reel to READY/ using only this doc and the files it links.

## Task 2 · 🔥 Build the next-sprint content bank — 10 ranked hook→video concepts (60–75 min)

**Why:** The winners data says WHAT won; the content bank turns that into filmable concepts with footage sources attached. Next sprint's first batch-filming day should start from row 1 of this file.
**Where:** New file `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/next-sprint-video-bank.md` — filed beside the backlog, complementing the next-campaign brief in `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/`.
**Tools:** Code editor, your Day-14 winner picks in the tracker, the `next-sprint` rows in `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md` (if any), `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-readout.md` (if written), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (WON/LOST statuses), your banked openers from Day 14.

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/next-sprint-video-bank.md` with one table: rank · hook · category · pillar · exercise/feature · footage source · which winner it extends.
2. Seed from what's proven: the two Day-14 openers are ranks 1–2 (they're already cut). Then turn the strongest `next-sprint` rows in `idea-backlog.md` into video treatments if any exist — reference and extend them ("backlog: 'same hook, lunges' → wide+close lunge set, master-set style"). If the backlog has none, mine the hooks-inbox and 15 minutes of store reviews/comments yourself and write treatments from what real users said.
3. Fill to 10 with your own: WON-status hooks on untried exercises (five to choose from), winning formats on untried features (Beat-It, streaks, leaderboards, discover), and the strongest unanswered hooks-inbox phrases.
4. Rank all 10 by the numbers visible in the tracker/app analytics — how directly each extends something that moved store visits (never likes; if unclear, most saves). One line of "why this rank" per row.
5. For each row, name the footage source: existing (`buzzend-footage/` index) or "film — batch day 1", so the next batch list writes itself.
6. Banned-words pass, commit, push, link the file in team chat, and flag ranks 1–3 as the retro's suggested first week — no sign-off needed.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/next-sprint-video-bank.md` — 10 ranked, footage-sourced concepts.
**Done when:** Each row names its hook, pillar, footage source, and the winner it extends — and ranks 1–3 are flagged for the retro.
