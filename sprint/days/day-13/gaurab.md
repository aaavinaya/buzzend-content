# Day 13 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Ask our genuinely engaged challenge users — and only them — for honest reviews, and capture the leaderboard footage the final two days of content will need.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Ask up to 5 engaged challenge participants for an honest review (75–90 min)

**Why:** Review count is a store ranking factor, and the only reviews worth having come from people who genuinely use Buzzend. Someone who has done 3+ days of counted squats has an honest opinion — asking them isn't begging, it's giving them a place to say it.
**Where:** Buzzend app (7-Day Squat Starter leaderboard) + wherever we already talk to each person (DM thread, comment thread), Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Open the 7-Day Squat Starter leaderboard and list everyone who has completed 3+ days of camera-counted workouts — these are our genuine, engaged users.
2. Cross-check the list against Riya's Day-12 invite log and our DM/comment history: pick up to 5 people we already have a real interaction with. Someone we've never spoken to gets a warm conversation first, not a review ask — never ask strangers.
3. Message each one individually, by name, referencing their actual progress ("Day 4 of the squat challenge — you're ahead of most of us"). The ask, in your own words each time: "If you have 2 minutes, an honest review on the store would genuinely help a 3-person team. Whatever you really think."
4. Hard rules: no incentive of any kind, no asking for 5 stars or "positive" reviews, no follow-up nagging if they don't do it. One honest ask, then let it go. If anyone already left a review, thank them instead (Avinaya is replying to reviews today — coordinate).
5. Note today's review count on both stores in the tracker (`Store-iOS`/`Store-Android`) and add a "review asks sent" row in `Outreach`: name, day count in challenge, date asked. Check the delta on Day 15.

**Deliverable:** Up to 5 individual, honest review asks sent + review-count baseline row in the tracker.
**Done when:** Every ask is logged with a name and a reason ("3+ challenge days, existing DM thread"), and today's review counts are recorded.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 12 · Task 1 (her invite log tells you who's already in a warm thread).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Record the "challenge results so far" leaderboard footage (45–60 min)

**Why:** Day 14–15 content (the building-in-public post, the thank-you stories) needs real proof the challenge happened — and the leaderboard IS that proof. Capturing it today means nobody scrambles for footage on publishing day.
**Where:** Buzzend app on your Android device (and an iPhone pass with Avinaya if the iOS rendering looks better).
**Tools:** Android phone screen recorder, Buzzend app, `video/` folder in this repo.

**Steps:**
1. In the app, open the 7-Day Squat Starter leaderboard. Screen-record a slow, steady 20–30s scroll: participant count, the top of the board, the day-streak indicators.
2. Record a second take capturing your own challenge progress screen (a real user's view beats an admin's view).
3. Privacy pass before anything leaves your phone: only first names/usernames visible, nothing personal in notifications (turn on Do Not Disturb before recording). Message the people whose names are clearly readable and get their OK to appear — Riya needs the same permissions tomorrow, so log the answers in the tracker.
4. Save the best takes into `video/` with clear names (e.g. `video/challenge-leaderboard-day13.mp4`) and note the paths in the tracker `Content` notes.
5. Remember the brand rule: raw device recordings are raw material, never published as-is — Riya frames them with `assets/screens-clean/`-style treatment before anything goes public.

**Deliverable:** 2 usable leaderboard recordings in `video/`, permissions logged.
**Done when:** Riya can build Day-14/15 content from the files without re-recording anything.
