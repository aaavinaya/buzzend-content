# Day 13 — Riya

**Objective:** Get our first user-generated clips moving — ask the three most active users to film themselves with the counter — and have the testimonial template ready the moment a clip lands.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Ask our 3 most active users for a short honest clip (75–90 min)

**Why:** A stranger saying "it counted my squats" converts better than anything we can produce ourselves — the community playbook calls user content our best-converting content. Our most active users already love talking to us; the ask is small and flattering.
**Where:** DM threads where we already talk to these people (Instagram/TikTok), Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the 3 most active users of this sprint: cross-reference your Day-12 invite log, the challenge leaderboard regulars (Gaurab's list from today), and whoever comments on everything. Existing conversation threads only.
2. Message each one personally, by name: would they record a 15–30s honest clip of themselves working out with Buzzend — their real setup, the counter counting out loud? Be clear it's their honest take, unscripted, and we'd love to feature them on our profiles (that's the offer — visibility, not payment; there is no budget and no incentive).
3. If someone says yes, send the simple filming tips as a short friendly list: prop the phone so the camera sees your whole body, decent light, counter volume up, one continuous take, film vertical (9:16) — and don't polish it, real beats pretty.
4. Ask for permission explicitly in the same thread: "OK if we post this on our Instagram/TikTok with your first name?" Screenshot/save the yes.
5. Log each ask in `Outreach`: name, platform, date, status (asked / yes / clip received / posted), permission noted.
6. No pressure and no follow-up nagging — if someone goes quiet, they stay logged as "asked" and we move on.

**Deliverable:** 3 personal UGC asks sent, statuses + permissions logged in the tracker.
**Done when:** All 3 rows exist in `Outreach` and anyone who said yes has the filming tips in hand.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 12 · Task 1 (the invite log identifies who's genuinely active).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Build the testimonial post template in Canva (45–60 min)

**Why:** The first clip could arrive tonight. If the template exists, a raw user video becomes a publishable post in 20 minutes; if it doesn't, the clip sits in a DM losing momentum.
**Where:** Canva.
**Tools:** Canva, `brand/design-guide.md` (Energy Orange palette), `assets/screens-clean/`.

**Steps:**
1. Create two linked templates: (a) a 1080×1350 quote card — big pull-quote area for the user's own words, first name + "Buzzend user" attribution line, Energy Orange accent from `brand/design-guide.md`; (b) a 1080×1920 video frame — space for the user's vertical clip with a slim branded header/footer so it reads as featured-by-us, not made-by-us.
2. Put placeholder text in the quote slot ("PASTE THEIR EXACT WORDS — never paraphrase a testimonial") so nobody forgets the rule.
3. Use only `assets/screens-clean/` imagery for any app visuals — no raw device screenshots.
4. Keep the user's footage area untouched by overlays — their counter moment is the proof; don't cover it.
5. One CTA slot only ("Try your first AI workout."). No banned words anywhere on the template.
6. Save both in the shared Canva space named clearly (e.g. "UGC quote card" / "UGC video frame") and drop the links in the tracker + team chat.

**Deliverable:** Two ready-to-fill testimonial templates, linked in the tracker.
**Done when:** A test-fill with dummy text takes under 20 minutes start to export.
