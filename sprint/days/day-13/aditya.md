## Task 1 · 🔥 Testimonial production — edit the first UGC clips (or film the honest placeholder) (75–90 min)

**Why:** A stranger saying "it counted my squats" converts better than anything we produce ourselves. Riya's UGC asks are out today and her Canva templates are ready — the moment a clip lands it should become a post in one sitting, not sit in a DM losing momentum.
**Where:** CapCut + Riya's Canva templates.
**Tools:** CapCut, Canva ("UGC quote card" / "UGC video frame" templates from Riya's Day-13 Task 2), the tracker `Outreach` tab (permissions), shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Check with Riya which clips (if any) have arrived and — before touching them — verify in `Outreach` that the sender's posting permission is logged (first name + "OK to post" saved in the thread). No logged permission, no edit.
2. **If a clip arrived:** drop it into the "UGC video frame" template — slim branded header/footer, the user's footage area untouched (their counter moment is the proof; never cover it). Trim to 15–30s keeping the count audible. Any quoted words on screen are their EXACT words — never paraphrase a testimonial. Attribution: first name + "Buzzend user".
3. **If no clip yet:** film your own honest 15s "my streak so far" placeholder — your real streak screen, one counted set, one plain line on what's kept you going. You're a genuine user; say only what's true. Frame it with the same template so the format debuts either way.
4. One CTA ("Try your first AI workout."), banned-words pass, auto-captions proofed.
5. Export the master + cover frame to the shared drive, log the path, and send to Avinaya for the approval pass (number claims and real names are always-approve territory).
6. Note in the tracker which of Riya's UGC rows this used, so her statuses ("clip received / posted") stay true.

**Deliverable:** One testimonial post master (UGC or honest placeholder) in the shared drive, approved-or-in-review, logged.
**Done when:** The post could publish tomorrow with permission, exact words, and real counts all verifiable from the tracker.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 13 · Task 1 (the UGC asks + permissions), Riya · Day 13 · Task 2 (the templates).
