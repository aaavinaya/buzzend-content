# Day 3 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Build a reusable Android b-roll library covering every social feature, and find out which exercise the counter demos most reliably — so future filming days never start from zero.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Record a 10-clip Android b-roll library (90–120 min)

**Why:** Every future video needs cutaway shots of the app actually doing things. Recording them once, labeled, means editors (usually Avinaya in CapCut) grab clips instead of scheduling re-shoots.
**Where:** Buzzend on your Android device, using the built-in screen recorder.
**Tools:** Android phone, Do Not Disturb ON, shared drive, tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Prep the phone so nothing personal leaks into footage: Do Not Disturb on, notifications cleared, battery above 50%, a clean profile name in the app (remember the public-imagery rule — raw personal screens never go out; these clips get cut into edits, so record them clean).
2. Record these flows as separate clips, 10–20 seconds each, in this order: (1) create a challenge, (2) invite a friend, (3) join a challenge with a code, (4) leaderboard with live standings, (5) a Beat-It attempt flow, (6) the streak screen with the flame, (7) the discover page of trending challenges, (8) daily goal completing, (9) the rep-counter counting a short real set (prop the phone, do the reps — never a faked count), (10) the workout summary screen.
3. Name every file the same way: `broll-01-challenge-create.mp4`, `broll-02-invite.mp4`, … `broll-10-summary.mp4`. Re-record any clip with stutter or a mis-tap — 10 clean clips beats 15 messy ones.
4. Upload the folder to the shared drive.
5. In the tracker, add one row per clip: filename · what it shows · length · date. This list is what Avinaya and Riya will search, so write the "what it shows" column for someone who hasn't seen the clip.
6. Drop the drive folder link in the team chat.

**Deliverable:** 10 labeled clips in the shared drive + a 10-row index in the tracker.
**Done when:** A teammate can find and download "the leaderboard clip" from the tracker alone, without asking you.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Test the rep counter on 3 exercises and rank their demo reliability (30–45 min)

**Why:** Content lives or dies on the counter visibly being right (rule 1: never fake a rep count). Knowing TODAY which exercise counts most reliably on camera tells us what to film for every future demo — and what to avoid.
**Where:** Buzzend on your Android device, enough floor space for push-ups.
**Tools:** Android phone, something to prop it against, tracker `Notes` or `Experiments` tab.

**Steps:**
1. For each of squats, push-ups and jumping jacks: prop the phone, do a set of 10 real reps, and record what the app counted.
2. Do each exercise twice — once in your best setup, once in a lazy setup (worse angle or light) — so we know how forgiving each exercise is.
3. Log per exercise in the tracker: counted X of 10 (best) / X of 10 (lazy) · phone position that worked · anything that broke it (distance, framing, clothing, light).
4. Rank the three from most to least demo-reliable and write one line: "Film demos with ___ first."
5. If any exercise miscounts even in the best setup, don't work around it on camera — write it up and flag it to the product team, per the content law in `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`.
6. Share the ranking in the team chat — it feeds every filming decision from Day 4 on.

**Deliverable:** A 3-exercise reliability table + one-line filming recommendation in the tracker.
**Done when:** All three exercises have best/lazy counts logged and the team has the ranking.
