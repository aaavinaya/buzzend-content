# Day 26 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Health-check Squad Squats halfway through its run — real numbers, fresh leaderboard footage, and a warm personal nudge to the people who drifted — then data pull #3.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Squad Squats health check + re-invite 5 lapsed participants (75–90 min)

**Why:** Day 26 is roughly halfway through the 14-day challenge — the point where people quietly drop off. A challenge that visibly stays alive converts; one that goes quiet teaches everyone to ignore the next one. Warm, named nudges — never a blast.
**Where:** Buzzend app on your Android phone; Growth Tracker; the shared drive READY/ folder.
**Tools:** Android phone, screen recorder, Growth Tracker (`Outreach` tab + challenge log), `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Open the Squad Squats leaderboard and record the health numbers into the tracker next to the Day-21 launch row: total participants, how many were active in the last 2 days, top scores, and your own position.
2. Screen-record a 10–15s slow leaderboard scroll and grab 2–3 clean stills. Privacy pass first: Do Not Disturb on, and only permission-cleared names visible — check against the Sprint-1 cleared list in the tracker; message anyone new whose name is clearly readable and get their OK before the frame leaves your phone.
3. Drop the recording + stills in **READY/** dated "Day 26 — Squad Squats leaderboard" and note the paths in the tracker — this is content raw material (it gets the `assets/screens-clean/`-style treatment before anything goes public).
4. List up to 5 lapsed participants: people who joined and logged workouts but have gone quiet for 2+ days — and whom you've actually interacted with before.
5. Re-invite each one personally, by name, referencing their real progress ("you were 3rd on day 4 — the board misses you"). Warm and light, one message, no guilt, no follow-up if they don't bite. Never message strangers.
6. Log each nudge in `Outreach`: name, last active day, date nudged.

**Deliverable:** Health numbers in the tracker, cleared leaderboard footage in READY/, up to 5 personal re-invites logged.
**Done when:** The health row is filled, every visible name in the footage is permission-cleared, and each nudge has an `Outreach` row.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 21 · Task 1 (the challenge being checked).

3. Write the 3-line trend note (installs vs baseline · biggest mover · one suggested action) and drop it in **READY/** dated "Day 26 — data pull #3".

**Deliverable:** Days 22–25 complete in `Daily Numbers` + dated trend note in READY/.
