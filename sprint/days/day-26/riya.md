## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + personally invite 20 new engagers to Squad Squats (90–110 min)

**Why:** Sprint 2 has produced a fresh crop of people who comment, DM, and like everything — the exact profile that converted in sprint 1's invite rounds. A named invite that quotes what they said converts many times better than any story CTA.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows; DM and comment threads; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squad-squats.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe (READY/ → `social-media/rendered/` fallback → same-day reschedule; caption self-check; URLs into `Social`).
2. Build the invite list: scroll every sprint-2 post's comments, the DM inbox, and repeat likers **since Day 16**. Cross-check `Outreach` so nobody invited on Day 18 gets a second invite — cap at 20, real engagers only, nobody we've never interacted with.
3. Write each invite individually: their name, what they actually said or did ("you asked if it counts lunges — it does, out loud"), the join steps from `how-to-join-squad-squats.md` (fallback: the Squat Starter steps if Squad Squats isn't live), ending on a question, not a pitch.
4. Deliver where the relationship exists: their DM thread if there is one, otherwise a reply to their comment. The community playbook bans unsolicited promo DMs — one-time likers get nothing.
5. Absolute rule: no copy-paste blast. If two invites read identical, rewrite one.
6. Log every invite in `Outreach`: name, platform, what they engaged with, date sent; fill replies as they come.

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live + up to 20 logged personal invites, each visibly its own message.
**Done when:** Every invite row shows an individually-worded message sent (or a reason skipped), and no Day-18 invitee was re-invited.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Research 5 fresh community venues for sprint 3 (45–60 min)

**Why:** The GREEN communities we have are finite; sprint 3 needs new neighborhoods scouted **before** it starts, rated with the same discipline that kept us welcome everywhere this sprint.
**Where:** Facebook group search, Reddit, Discord discovery (disboard.org); `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`.
**Tools:** Phone or laptop, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md` (the Day-6 rating method), `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Search for *home workout, workout challenge, fitness accountability, beginner fitness, 30 day challenge* across FB groups, subreddits, and Discord servers.
2. Shortlist the 5 most alive (posts today, real comments — not link dumps), skipping anything already in the map.
3. Read each venue's rules on app mentions before rating — quote the written rule where one exists.
4. Add 5 rows to `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`: name · platform · size · activity · rules on app mentions · GREEN/YELLOW/RED. If the map file doesn't exist, create it with the Day-6 column format and continue.
5. Join nothing and post nothing yet — this is sprint-3 material; note your top-2 "we could genuinely help here" picks in the tracker.

**Deliverable:** 5 new rated rows in `community-map.md` + top-2 picks noted in the tracker.
**Done when:** All 5 rows carry a rating grounded in the venue's actual rules, and zero posts were made.
