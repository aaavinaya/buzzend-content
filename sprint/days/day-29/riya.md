## Task 1 · 🔥 Write the sprint-3 creative brief (90–110 min)

**Why:** The winners file says what worked; the brief says what we'll do about it. One page written while the learnings are hot means sprint 3's first publishing day never faces a blank calendar — the same move that made this sprint's Day 16 possible.
**Where:** New file `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/sprint3-campaign-brief.md` + a copy in the shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Laptop, `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/sprint3-winners.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (including your Day-27 starred phrase), `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/planning-templates.md` (template #10's calendar row format).

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/sprint3-campaign-brief.md` — hard limit one page.
2. **Theme:** the winning pattern from `sprint3-winners.md` (the pillar/hook family that provably moved store visits via the `/go/` counts), plus one sentence on why the numbers say so. If the winners file is somehow missing, rebuild the top-3 from what you can read in each app in 20 minutes and continue.
3. **5 post concepts:** each names its hook (from the bank — your Day-27 starred audience phrase should be one), format, and which sprint-2 winner it extends.
4. **1 challenge concept — existing functionality only:** built from the five counted exercises (squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges) plus streaks, leaderboards, Beat-It, discover. A natural follow-up to Squad Squats (e.g. a lunge or push-up sequel). No new features, no product asks — anything needing the app to change goes to the future-ideas doc, not the brief.
5. **Asset needs:** what must exist before sprint-3 day 1 — footage to batch-film, Canva templates (the UGC templates count), `assets/screens-clean/` renders.
6. **Rough calendar:** 2 weeks of rows in template #10's format (Day · Platform · Pillar · Topic · Hook direction · Format · CTA · Owner) — rough is fine.
7. Drop a copy in **READY/** for Avinaya's async review and link the brief in the tracker — it's the input for the sprint-3 kickoff, and review notes become edits, never holds.

**Deliverable:** One-page `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/sprint3-campaign-brief.md` + copy in READY/.
**Done when:** The brief answers theme, 5 concepts, 1 challenge, assets, calendar — and still fits one page.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 28 · Task 1 (the winners file picks the theme — step-2 fallback if it's missing).
