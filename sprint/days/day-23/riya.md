## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + engagement hour + repost every permissioned #CountMyReps entry (100–120 min)

**Why:** The first 48 hours decide whether a contest feels alive or dead. Every early entrant who gets a fast genuine reply and a same-day repost becomes an evangelist; every ignored entry teaches people not to bother.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows; the #CountMyReps tag on IG + TikTok; entrants' own threads; Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Phone, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe (READY/ → `social-media/rendered/` fallback → same-day reschedule; caption self-check; URLs into `Social`).
2. Engagement hour: reply to **100%** of comments on all live posts, pin the best on each, lean into skeptics kindly, and paste great audience phrasings into the hooks-inbox in `hook-bank.md`.
3. Search **#CountMyReps** on IG and TikTok. For each real entry: comment genuinely first — a person reacting to their set, not a brand scanning for content.
4. Then ask in their own thread: "This is great — can we share it on our profiles with your first name/handle?" Screenshot every yes — written permission in the thread is the rule from `countmyreps-contest.md`, no exceptions, ever.
5. Repost only entries with a saved yes: story reshares same-day, credited by handle. Anything involving a minor or an injury/safety claim goes straight to the founders per the community playbook — never reposted.
6. Log every entry in the tracker: handle, platform, entry URL, permission status, reposted-or-not. This log picks Day-25's feature.

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live, all comments answered, every permissioned entry reshared, entry log started in the tracker.
**Done when:** No entry sits unacknowledged, no repost lacks a saved written yes, and the entry log has one row per entry found.
**Depends on:** —
