## Task 2 · 🟡 Record interim Play experiment results (30–40 min)

**Why:** The Day-17 experiment has had ~6 days. Play won't declare a winner this early at our volume, but writing down the interim numbers keeps us honest — and stops anyone from "remembering" the result differently later.
**Where:** Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Store listing experiments; `Experiments` tab.
**Tools:** Play Console, Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Open the running experiment and read what Play shows per variant: installers, and the performance range it reports.
2. **If there's data:** copy the per-variant numbers into the experiment's `Experiments` row, dated, marked "interim — Day 23". No decision yet: at 4–5 installs/day, a week of data is a hint, not a verdict. Do NOT stop the experiment unless Play itself has declared a result.
3. **If Play shows "not enough data":** write exactly that in the row, dated. That's a real finding, not a failure.
4. Add one line: when to check next (Day 28, with the results assembly).

**Deliverable:** A dated interim entry in the `Experiments` row — numbers or an explicit "not enough data".
**Done when:** The Experiments row shows today's reading and the experiment is still running (unless Play declared a winner).
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 17 · Task 2 (the experiment it checks on).
