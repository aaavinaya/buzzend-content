# Day 21 — Riya

**Objective:** Two posts ship, the challenge gets shared where we're genuinely welcome (and only there), and the three sprint-1 UGC asks get one last polite close-the-loop.
**Total time:** ~2.5–3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + share the challenge in the GREEN communities (90–110 min)

**Why:** The Day-6 community map exists exactly for this moment: groups where value-posts are welcome convert warm, curious people for free — and one rule-breaking post in the wrong group burns a community forever.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows; the Facebook groups/Discords rated GREEN in `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`.
**Tools:** Phone or laptop, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`, `buzzend-marketing/08-social-media/facebook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squad-squats.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe (READY/ first, `social-media/rendered/` fallback or same-day reschedule; caption self-check; URLs into `Social`).
2. Open `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md` and take only the GREEN-rated groups/Discords (value-posts welcome). Re-read each group's written rule before posting; YELLOW means comment-help only, RED means never. If the map is missing or thin, rate 2–3 groups you're already a member of yourself first (Day-6 method) and continue.
3. Share the **Squad Squats** challenge as a help-first post in your own words per `facebook.md` and the community playbook: lead with the experience (free, the phone camera counts your squats out loud, friends compete on a camera-verified board), the join steps from `how-to-join-squad-squats.md`, a link only where the group's rules explicitly allow — otherwise "it's called Buzzend" and answer questions in the comments. If Squad Squats isn't live in the app, share the 7-Day Squat Starter with `how-to-join-squat-starter.md`'s steps instead.
4. Stay 30–60 min answering replies in each group like a member, not a brand — no hard sells, no arguing with skeptics.
5. Log each community post in the tracker (group, link or screenshot, early response) and paste standout member phrasings into the hooks-inbox in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live + value-first challenge shares in every GREEN community, all logged.
**Done when:** Every share respected the group's written rules, and every reply within the first hour got your answer.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Close the loop on the 3 sprint-1 UGC asks (40–50 min)

**Why:** Three people were asked for honest clips on Day 13. A single warm nudge now either lands a clip for the Day-25 feature or lets us close those rows with grace — either outcome beats letting them rot at "asked".
**Where:** The existing DM threads; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Open the three Day-13 UGC rows and their current statuses (asked / yes / clip received / posted).
2. Anyone at `yes` without a clip: one warm, final nudge — re-offer the filming tips, make it easy ("even 15 seconds of the counter counting is gold"), close with "no pressure at all, the offer stands".
3. Anyone still at `asked`: this message is their single follow-up, then we stop forever — the one-follow-up rule applies to users exactly like creators.
4. Any clip that has arrived: thank them by name, confirm written permission in the thread ("OK if we post this with your first name?"), save the yes, and queue the clip for the Day-25 feature slot.
5. Update every status in `Outreach`; note in the tracker whether Day-25 has a sprint-1 clip available.

**Deliverable:** All 3 UGC rows at a current, final-or-active status; any received clip has logged written permission.
**Done when:** No UGC row still shows a stale status, and nobody has been nudged more than once.
