## Task 1 · 🔥 Squad Squats challenge content — announcement reel + 2 stories (80–100 min)

**Why:** Squad Squats is sprint 2's in-app group challenge, and Pillar 3 says challenges are how warm audiences turn into inviters — "finish the week together", never domination. The announcement has to show the real challenge screen, because the camera-verified leaderboard IS the pitch.
**Where:** CapCut + Canva; screens from READY/ or your own phone.
**Tools:** Phone (Buzzend), CapCut, Canva, `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md` (Pillar 3 do/don't lists), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, shared drive, tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Get the challenge screens: if fresh Squad Squats screens/recordings are already in the shared drive **READY/** folder, use them — never wait. If READY/ has none, join the challenge on your own phone, do a real counted set as your entry, and screen-record the flow yourself: joining, the workout screen counting, the leaderboard after. Real reps, real position on the board.
2. Script the reel (15–25s): hook from the Challenge/Together shelf ("Try 20 squats with me." or a "the camera keeps score" line), PROOF — the challenge screen with the count climbing, then the leaderboard, then how to join (find it in the app, join, squat), CTA **"Challenge a friend."**
3. Frame every screen recording — brand frame or device mockup in CapCut/Canva, never a raw screen dump as a post. Captions proofed, 1080×1920, cover frame.
4. In Canva (**#ff6b00 / #fff8f2**), make 2 stories: Story 1 — "Squad Squats is live" + a framed leaderboard moment + join steps in 3 lines; Story 2 — a poll/question sticker slot ("how many squats can your squad do this week?") + the challenge name. ≤10 words per text block, Pillar-3 tone: co-op and realistic, no calling anyone out.
5. Self-check against checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`: counts shown are genuinely counted (yours or from READY/ material), one CTA, no banned words.
6. Drop in **READY/**: `day21-squadsquats-announce.mp4` + cover, `day21-squadsquats-story-1.png`, `-2.png`. Log paths in the tracker. Avinaya reviews async; fixes ship as follow-ups.

**Deliverable:** Squad Squats announcement reel + 2 stories in READY/, cut from real challenge screens.
**Done when:** The reel shows the actual challenge experience end to end, and a viewer knows how to join without asking.
