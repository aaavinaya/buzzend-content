# Day 21 — Avinaya

**Objective:** Review-only day: verify what the team shipped and give fast, exact verdicts — never blocking anyone.
**Total time:** ~1.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Daily verification sweep (20–25 min)

**Why:** The team ships solo all day — this sweep is the only quality gate, done after the fact so nobody ever waits on you.
**Where:** Shared drive READY/ folder + every post that went live today.
**Tools:** Approval card (checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`), Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Open READY/ and today's live post URLs (tracker `Social` tab).
2. Check each item: every count real · exactly one CTA · no banned words · caption line 1 restates the hook · links point at the go/ pages.
3. Anything off: write ONE exact fix in the tracker and ping the owner — fixes ship as follow-ups; published posts stay up unless a rep count is fake (only then: immediate takedown).
4. Mark each item ✅ verified / 🔁 follow-up in tracker Notes.

**Deliverable:** Everything shipped today marked verified or follow-up.
**Done when:** Nothing in READY/ or today's posts is unreviewed.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Mid-sprint numbers review — repeat / change / stop (30–40 min)

**Why:** Five days of the new cadence is enough signal to steer: double what moves installs, kill what doesn't.
**Where:** Shared drive READY/ + Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Tracker, approval card, browser.

**Steps:**
1. Read Gaurab's data pulls + trend notes in READY/ (installs/day vs 4–5 baseline, clicks, joins).
2. Rank the week's posts by store-visit/install correlation, never likes.
3. Write 3 verdict bullets in the tracker: REPEAT (the winning format), CHANGE (one thing), STOP (one thing). The team reads it tomorrow morning.

**Deliverable:** The 3-bullet verdict in the tracker Notes + READY/.
**Done when:** All three teammates can quote your verdicts.
**Depends on:** —
