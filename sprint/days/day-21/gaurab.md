# Day 21 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Launch challenge #2 — "Squad Squats", bigger and public — seed it with your own real entry, capture fresh screens for content, and post it where we're actually welcome.
**Total time:** ~2.5–3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Set up the "Squad Squats" team challenge and seed it (90–110 min)

**Why:** The 7-Day Squat Starter was our best conversion tool in Sprint 1 — a second, bigger, public challenge is the most direct "scale what worked" move we have, and it costs nothing. Existing app features only; no product asks.
**Where:** Buzzend app on your Android phone.
**Tools:** Android phone, Buzzend app, screen recorder, Growth Tracker, the shared drive READY/ folder.

**Steps:**
1. In the app, create the new challenge with existing features only: name "Squad Squats", exercise = squats (camera-counted), duration = 14 days, visibility = public so anyone can find it in Discover.
2. Double-check the setup as a stranger would find it: open Discover, search/scroll to Squad Squats, read the name and details as they render. Fix anything unclear before anyone else sees it.
3. Seed it with your own REAL entry: prop the phone, do an actual squat workout, let the camera count it. A challenge with a live leaderboard entry reads alive; an empty one reads dead. Never fake a rep count.
4. Capture fresh screens for content: the challenge card in Discover, the join screen, and the leaderboard with your entry on it. Screen-record a 10–15s slow scroll too.
5. Privacy pass: only your own name visible at this point — if anyone else has already joined, only permission-cleared names appear in captures (the Sprint-1 rule from Day 13).
6. Drop the recordings and stills in **READY/** dated "Day 21 — Squad Squats screens" and note the paths in the tracker. Raw captures are raw material for Aditya/Riya — never published as-is; they get the `assets/screens-clean/`-style treatment first.
7. Log the challenge in the tracker: name, start date, duration, join path (from `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-invite-friends.md`), and today's participant count (1 — you).

**Deliverable:** Squad Squats live and findable in Discover with your real entry + fresh screens/recording in READY/.
**Done when:** The challenge appears in Discover, the leaderboard shows your camera-counted workout, and the captures are in READY/ with paths logged.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Post Squad Squats where self-promo is allowed (40–50 min)

**Why:** A public challenge is only public if people hear about it. We announce it exactly where communities have told us promo is welcome — and nowhere else.
**Where:** The communities whose `buzzend-marketing/12-community/where-were-welcome.md` rows allow self-promo.
**Tools:** Reddit/Discord/Facebook accounts, `where-were-welcome.md`, `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` (approved wording), Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Open `where-were-welcome.md` and list only the rows where self-promo or app mentions are explicitly allowed. That list is the whole distribution plan — no exceptions.
2. Re-read each target community's own rules (2 min) — right channel/flair, link policy, disclosure requirements.
3. Write each post individually for its community — never copy-paste the same text twice. Shape: the honest story ("we're a small team, our first challenge got strangers doing squats daily, so we opened a bigger one"), what it is (14 days, camera counts your squats, live leaderboard), how to join (the steps from `how-to-invite-friends.md`), full disclosure that you helped build it.
4. Use the store links from `sprint/config.js` only where links are allowed; otherwise name the app and let people find it.
5. Post, then stay reachable for an hour — answer every reply like a member, not a rep.
6. Log each post in the tracker: community, link, time, and early replies. These threads get a thank-you pass on Day 29.

**Deliverable:** Squad Squats announced in every allowed community, each post individual and disclosed, all logged.
**Done when:** Every allowed-promo row in `where-were-welcome.md` either has a live post logged or a one-line reason it was skipped.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 21 · Task 1 (the challenge must be live and seeded first).
