## Task 3 · 🟡 Test-film 3 raw counter clips (35 min)

**Why:** The magic moment (number going up by itself) has filming gotchas — framing, light, phone angle, where the counter shows. Learn them on throwaway takes, not on Day-3 production.
**Where:** Any room with floor space and daylight.
**Tools:** Your phone (Buzzend + camera), something to prop the phone against.

**Steps:**
1. Film vertical (9:16): one squat set, one push-up set, one jumping-jack set — 15–20s each, counter visible and counting, real reps only (rule 1: never fake a rep count).
2. Try two setups per clip: phone on the floor angled up vs. table height.
3. Watch them back: is the count readable at phone size? Is your full body in frame? Note what works.
4. Drop the best takes in the shared drive under `test-takes/` and your setup notes in the tracker.

**Deliverable:** 3 labeled test clips + setup notes.
**Done when:** You know your repeatable filming setup for Day 3's production.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 1 · Task 3 (app installed, one real workout done).
