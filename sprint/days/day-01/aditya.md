# Day 1 — Aditya

**Objective:** Onboard fast — know exactly what Buzzend is, how we talk about it, and have your creator toolkit ready. (The team already finished Days 1–2; do this and Day 2 together on your first day.)
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Read the five docs that define everything you'll make (60 min)

**Why:** Every piece of content you produce must use approved wording, proven hooks, and the four pillars — all already written down. One hour of reading prevents weeks of remakes.
**Where:** This repo, `buzzend-marketing/`.
**Tools:** Laptop.

**Steps:**
1. Read `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` — the one-liner, approved short versions, banned words. This is law.
2. Read `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/why-people-stop-scrolling.md` — the hook levers.
3. Read `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md` — PROOF · CONFIDENCE · TOGETHER · BUILDING.
4. Read `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` — the 14 hook categories you'll pull from daily.
5. Read `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md` + the 3 example scripts.
6. Write 3 lines in the tracker `Notes`: the one-liner from memory, your favorite hook category, one content idea it sparked.

**Deliverable:** The 3-line note in the Growth Tracker.
**Done when:** You can say the one-liner and list the 4 pillars without looking.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🔥 Set up your creator toolkit and access (45 min)

**Why:** Day 3 onward you produce daily — every missing login or app found today is a delay avoided tomorrow.
**Where:** Your phone + laptop.
**Tools:** CapCut (free), Canva, phone camera.

**Steps:**
1. Install CapCut on your phone/laptop (our editing tool: auto-captions, text hooks).
2. Get Canva access from Riya (team templates + Energy Orange brand colors #ff6b00 / #fff8f2 / ink #222).
3. Get from Avinaya: Growth Tracker edit access, the shared drive folder (b-roll library lives there), and posting logins (IG @buzzend.official, TikTok @buzzend.official, YouTube @buzzend.official, FB page).
4. Bookmark your day pages: `sprint/day.html?p=aditya` and the dashboard.
5. Test the pipeline once: make a 5-second test clip with a text hook + auto-captions in CapCut, drop it in the shared drive.

**Deliverable:** Test clip in the shared drive; all logins working.
**Done when:** You can open every tool and account without asking anyone.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 1 · Task 1 (the tracker exists), Riya · Day 3 · Task 1 (profiles/logins are polished).

## Task 3 · 🟡 Do one real AI workout in the app (20 min)

**Why:** You cannot film the magic moment until you've felt it. This also makes you a genuine user — your reactions become content.
**Where:** The Buzzend app on your phone (links in `sprint/config.js`).
**Tools:** Phone, floor space.

**Steps:**
1. Install Buzzend, create your account, prop the phone up.
2. Do a real set of 10 squats with the counter counting out loud.
3. Join the team's "7-Day Squat Starter" challenge (join steps in Avinaya's Day-5 doc).
4. Note in the tracker: where the counter impressed you, where it confused you — both are hooks.

**Deliverable:** Your account in the flagship challenge + 2 observations in the tracker.
**Done when:** The leaderboard shows your name with a camera-verified score.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 5 · Task 1 (the flagship challenge exists).
