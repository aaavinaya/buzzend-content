## Task 1 · 🔥 Tear down 10 competitor fitness apps (75–90 min)

**Why:** Their titles/screenshots show which keywords and promises already work; our ASO and content copy from the winners instead of guessing.
**Where:** App Store + Play Store on your phone.
**Tools:** Phone, a new doc `buzzend-marketing/03-competitors/sprint-teardown.md` (or a Google Doc if easier — link it in the tracker).

**Steps:**
1. Search these terms on both stores: *AI workout, rep counter, fitness challenge, workout with friends, home workout*.
2. Pick the 10 most relevant results overall (mix of big names and small apps that keep appearing).
3. For each app record: Name · Rating & review count · Title + subtitle/short description exactly as written · What their first 3 screenshots emphasize · Their obvious keywords · One thing they do better than us · One gap Buzzend fills (nobody else has camera-verified challenges with friends).
4. Finish with a 5-line summary: the 3 most-repeated keywords, the most common screenshot style, and the positioning hole we should own.
5. Ping Avinaya and Gaurab when the keyword rows are in — their ASO lists merge yours today.

**Deliverable:** 10-app teardown doc + 5-line summary, linked in the tracker.
**Done when:** All 10 rows complete and both teammates have the link.

## Task 2 · 🔥 Audit our ready-to-post assets and shortlist 5 (30–45 min)

**Why:** We already have 14 finished social visuals, a 13-second teaser video, and store screenshots in this repo. Posting starts Day 4 — today we choose what goes out first so nothing is made twice.
**Where:** This repo — `social-media/rendered/`, `video/`, `social-media/week1-content-calendar.md`.
**Tools:** Laptop, tracker `Social` tab (or a new `Content` tab).

**Steps:**
1. Open every image in `social-media/rendered/` (14 files, day1–day7 feed + story) and `video/teaser-1080x1920.mp4`.

4. Shortlist the first 5 posts in posting order (teaser video first — video beats stills everywhere).
5. Write the shortlist in the tracker with: asset path · platform(s) · caption source · target publish day.

**Deliverable:** 5-post shortlist with asset paths and caption sources in the tracker.
**Done when:** Anyone could publish post #1 using only your shortlist row.

## Task 3 · 🟡 Pick 10 hooks for this sprint (30 min)

**Why:** Hooks (the first 1–2 seconds of text) decide whether anyone watches. We have a researched hook system — choosing from it beats inventing.
**Where:** `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` and the hook bank beside it.
**Tools:** Laptop, `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/` backlog.

**Steps:**
1. Read the 14 hook categories (20 min max — skim the examples).
2. Choose 10 hooks that fit our two strongest pillars for a launch sprint: **PROOF** (the counter really counts) and **TOGETHER** (friends, challenges, Beat-It).
3. For each chosen hook write one line: hook text → which app feature proves it (AI counter / challenge / streak / Beat-It).
4. Add them to the `06-content-ideas/` backlog tagged `sprint-15`.

**Deliverable:** 10 hooks with matching features, saved in the ideas backlog.
**Done when:** All 10 are in the backlog and tagged.
