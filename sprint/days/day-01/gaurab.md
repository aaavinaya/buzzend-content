# Day 1 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Capture the Android baseline and build the Play keyword list, plus verify what a brand-new user actually experiences.
**Total time:** ~3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Record the Play Console baseline (45–60 min)

**Why:** Same reason as iOS — day-15 proof needs day-1 numbers. Play also shows which search terms already bring people in; that's free research.
**Where:** play.google.com/console.
**Tools:** Play Console login, `Store-Android` tab of the Growth Tracker (Avinaya creates the sheet this morning — Task 1).

**Steps:**
1. In Play Console → Statistics, set range to the last 7 days and record into `Store-Android`: Store listing visitors, Store listing acquisitions, Conversion rate, New installs.
2. Go to Store performance → Search (if data exists) and copy every search term Google already shows for us, with its numbers — these are keywords we demonstrably rank for.
3. Note our current rating and review count.
4. Fill the Android columns of today's `Daily Numbers` row.

**Deliverable:** `Store-Android` baseline complete + existing search-terms list.
**Done when:** Every metric above has a number (or an explicit "no data yet").
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 1 · Task 1 (the sheet must exist first).

## Task 2 · 🔥 Research 20 Play Store keywords (60–90 min)

**Why:** Play indexes the whole description, so knowing real search phrases changes what we write on Day 2.
**Where:** Play Store app on a real Android device.
**Tools:** Android phone, `Store-Android` tab.

**Steps:**
1. Use the same 8 seeds as Avinaya (*AI workout, rep counter, squat counter, workout with friends, fitness challenge app, home workout, push up counter, workout streak*) — keeping the lists parallel lets us compare stores later.
2. Type each seed into Play search slowly; record every suggestion. Aim for 30–40 raw terms.
3. Open the top 3 apps ranking for each seed; skim their short + full descriptions and note exact phrases they repeat (that repetition is deliberate keyword placement).
4. Build the same table as iOS: Keyword · Relevance · Guessed volume · Competition; shortlist 20, bold a top 5 for the short description.
5. Add terms from step 2 of Task 1 (terms we already rank for) — those are the cheapest wins.

**Deliverable:** 20-keyword Play table with top 5 bolded, in `Store-Android`.
**Done when:** Table complete and top 5 chosen.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 3 · 🟡 Fresh-eyes install test on both stores (30–40 min)

**Why:** We know where we rank and what a new user hits only by doing exactly what a stranger does.
**Where:** Play Store (your device) + App Store (borrow/use an iPhone with Avinaya).
**Tools:** Both phones, screenshots, `Store-Android` tab.

**Steps:**
1. Search "buzzend" on both stores — screenshot our listing position and how the screenshots look in the results.
2. Search 5 of your shortlisted keywords; record whether Buzzend appears at all and at what position (scroll max 50).
3. Uninstall, reinstall, and go from open → first AI workout as a brand-new user. Time it.
4. Log every point of confusion or friction in the tracker `Notes` — these become review-defense and content ideas, not product tickets.

**Deliverable:** Ranking screenshots + a friction list (3+ observations).
**Done when:** All 5 keyword positions recorded for both stores.
