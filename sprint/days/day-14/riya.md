# Day 14 — Riya

**Objective:** Turn 15 days of posts into a ranked winners bank plus 10 ready-to-make ideas for next sprint, and draft the honest "first 15 days" post for tomorrow.
**Total time:** ~3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Build the winners bank and write 10 next-sprint ideas (100–120 min)

**Why:** We paid for these learnings with 15 days of work — the winners bank is the receipt. Next sprint should open with a backlog of ideas that repeat what provably worked, not a blank page.
**Where:** Growth Tracker + `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md`.
**Tools:** Laptop, Growth Tracker, Avinaya's readout, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`.

**Steps:**
1. List every post we published this sprint (Reels #1–3, challenge announcement, carousel, Day-12 hook re-cut and timing repost, stories that had numbers worth noting).
2. Rank the list twice: once by reach (each platform's own analytics), once by store-visit correlation — take the store-visit attribution straight from Avinaya's readout, don't re-derive it. A post that won reach but moved zero store visits is NOT a winner (the winner rule).
3. Mark the top 3 by store visits as WON in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (their hooks earned the status); mark clear flops LOST — never delete them.
4. Write 10 next-sprint content ideas that repeat or extend the winners: same hook category on a different exercise (we have five: squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges), same format on a different feature (camera-verified challenges, Beat-It, streaks, leaderboards), a series continuing the challenge story.
5. Each idea = one row in `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md`: idea + hook + format, tagged `next-sprint`, under its pillar section. Every hook comes from or goes into the hook bank.
6. Add any real audience phrases from this sprint's comments to the hooks-inbox section while you're in the file — those become hooks before anything invented.

**Deliverable:** Ranked winners list in the tracker + 10 tagged ideas in `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md` + WON/LOST statuses updated in the hook bank.
**Done when:** Each of the 10 ideas names its hook, pillar, and which winner it extends.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 14 · Task 1 (the readout's store-visit numbers drive the ranking — start listing posts while you wait).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Draft the "our first 15 days building in public" post (50–60 min)

**Why:** BUILDING-pillar honesty is our most differentiating content — nobody expects a tiny team to publish its real numbers, flops included. Drafting today means tomorrow is just paste-final-numbers-and-publish, on the sprint's busiest day.
**Where:** Canva (visual) + the tracker `Content` notes (caption text).
**Tools:** Canva, `brand/design-guide.md`, Avinaya's readout draft, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (caption template #5).

**Steps:**
1. Pick the format: a 5–7 slide carousel usually fits this best — slide 1 hook ("We told the internet our real numbers. Day 15 of building Buzzend."), then: what we shipped, the honest numbers, what flopped (name it — e.g. the post nobody watched), what worked (the challenge, the winning hook), what's next.
2. Pull the structure and numbers from Avinaya's readout draft, but leave every number as a marked placeholder (`[X installs]`) — final numbers get pasted tomorrow after the readout is corrected.
3. Honesty rules: real numbers even if small, flops stated plainly, no banned words, no dressing up. Small-but-real is the whole appeal of this pillar.
4. Build the slides in Canva on the Energy Orange palette (`brand/design-guide.md`), app imagery from `assets/screens-clean/` only. Leaderboard visuals can use Gaurab's Day-13 recordings — framed, never raw.
5. Write the caption with template #5: first line restates the hook, one CTA ("Try your first AI workout." — link from `sprint/config.js` in bio).
6. Send draft to Avinaya for the approval pass (it's full of number claims — that's an always-approve category) and log it in the tracker as READY for Day 15.

**Deliverable:** Finished carousel draft (numbers as placeholders) + caption, approved and marked READY.
**Done when:** Tomorrow's publish needs only final numbers and the scheduled slot.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 13 · Task 2 (leaderboard footage for the results slide).
