## Task 1 · 🔥 Write the 15-day analytics readout (120–150 min)

**Why:** This is the payoff of every number we logged since Day 1. The readout is what we show the founders, what Day 15's retro runs on, and what next sprint's goals come from. It's assembly, not analysis theater: every figure already sits in the tracker — your job is to line them up so they speak.
**Where:** New file `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-readout.md` (beside Day 11's `sprint-feedback.md`), Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Code editor, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/planning-templates.md` (templates #11 and #12 as skeletons), `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md` if any term feels shaky.

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-readout.md`. Structure it with template #11's "numbers first, adjectives never" spirit, expanded to 15 days.
2. **Installs:** Day-1 baseline vs today, both stores, from `Daily Numbers`. Absolute numbers and the delta.
3. **Store conversion:** conversion rate before/after each change we made — Day-2 metadata update, Day-10 audit — from `Store-iOS`/`Store-Android`. One line per change: date, what changed, conversion before → after.
4. **Social:** per platform (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook): followers Day 1 → today, total reach, best post with its numbers, engagement. From the `Social` tab.
5. **Challenge:** 7-Day Squat Starter — total joins, day-5+ actives, the retention figure from yesterday's snapshot (quote its source honestly, proxy or console).
6. **Outreach:** the funnel from the `Outreach` tab: creator messages sent → replies → collabs in motion; plus Riya's personal invites and UGC asks with their statuses.
7. **Experiments:** both Day-12 rows (hook re-cut, timing repost) — numbers so far, control vs variant. Mark them "verdict on Day 15" — the 72h window closes tomorrow; do NOT call a winner early.
8. End with **"3 things the numbers prove"** — three one-sentence claims, each pointing at its number. If a claim needs an adjective to sound true, it isn't proven; pick another.
9. Rule check before saving: only numbers already in the tracker. If you catch yourself wanting a metric we never logged, write "not tracked this sprint" — do not go build new tooling today.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-readout.md`, complete with all six sections + the three proofs.
**Done when:** Someone who ignored the sprint entirely could read it in 5 minutes and repeat the three proofs back to you.
**Depends on:** —

**Why:** Gaurab and Riya lived different halves of this sprint — the readout is only "the team's truth" once they've checked it against what they saw. Their comments also seed tomorrow's retro so the 45 minutes start warm, not cold.
**Where:** Team chat + the readout file.
**Tools:** Team chat, code editor.

**Steps:**
1. Post the readout link in team chat with one line: "Read before tomorrow's retro — reply with ONE comment: a number that surprised you, or something the readout gets wrong."
2. Ping Gaurab specifically on the store-conversion section (his Play numbers) and Riya on the social/outreach sections (her invites and UGC statuses) — they'll spot errors you can't.
3. Collect both comments and append them at the bottom of the readout under a "Team comments" heading, with names.
4. Fix any factual error they catch today, not tomorrow — the retro must run on corrected numbers.

**Deliverable:** Readout shared; two named comments appended; corrections applied.
**Done when:** The "Team comments" section has one entry from Gaurab and one from Riya.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 14 · Task 1.
