## Task 1 · 🔥 Make 2 content pieces from real user questions — a reply video + an FAQ carousel (100–120 min)

**Why:** Riya's synthesis (her Day-11 Task 1) collects every real question the sprint has produced — and the hook system's highest rule is that the viewer's words beat ours. A video that answers an actual comment, hooked with that comment's exact wording, is the most honest content we can make.
**Where:** CapCut + Canva; source doc `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-feedback.md`.
**Tools:** CapCut, Canva, `buzzend-footage/`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` (category 11 — social proof), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (templates 1, 4), `assets/screens-clean/`.

**Steps:**
1. Read `sprint-feedback.md` and pick from the top-5 insights: ONE question for the reply video (the most-asked or most skeptical — "no way this is real-time" energy) and 4–6 questions for the FAQ carousel. Users' exact words, verbatim — typos included where they appear on screen as quotes.
2. **Reply video (20–30s):** open on the real question as the hook (screen-record the actual comment where permission/visibility allows, or set the verbatim phrase as on-screen text with initials only — Riya's doc keeps private names as initials). Answer on camera in one plain sentence, then PROVE it: a real counted set or the real screen that answers them. §40 pre-flight if you film a new demo. End card: one CTA, "Try your first AI workout."
3. Auto-captions proofed, 1080×1920 clean export `reply-video-1-master.mp4` + cover frame → shared drive, ping Avinaya for the approval pass.
4. **FAQ carousel (5–7 slides, 1080×1080):** slide 1 hook = the best question verbatim in quotes; one question per slide with a one-line human answer (no tech-speak — say "it watches your movement and counts your reps"); imagery only from `assets/screens-clean/`; last slide = one CTA. Match the approved store-shot look, Energy Orange palette.
5. Banned-words pass on both pieces against `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`.
6. Deliver: video master to Avinaya for review, carousel draft link to Riya for publishing; log both in the tracker and mark the answered questions "turned into content" in the hooks-inbox of `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Deliverable:** `reply-video-1-master.mp4` + a 5–7 slide FAQ carousel draft, both delivered and logged.
**Done when:** Both pieces open with a user's exact words and the hooks-inbox rows they came from are marked used.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 11 · Task 1 (the feedback synthesis).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Refresh the hook bank with 3 hooks from the edit room (30 min)

**Why:** Editing all week means you've watched the footage more than anyone — the moments that made YOU stop scrolling are hooks nobody has written down yet. The bank only compounds if the people closest to the material feed it.
**Where:** `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` (categories + writing rules).

**Steps:**
1. Write down the 3 strongest moments you kept noticing while editing (a rejected rep, the sound of the count, a streak flame appearing…) — one line each.
2. Turn each into a hook with its category's formula: ≤10 words on screen, a real number beats an adjective, passes the sound-off test.
3. Say each out loud; keep the wording that sounds like a person.
4. Add all 3 to the Team additions table in `hook-bank.md`: hook · category · source ("edit-room, sprint-15") · status NEW.
5. Commit, push, and drop the 3 in team chat — one may fit Day 12's experiment or the weekend push.

**Deliverable:** 3 new NEW-status rows in the hook bank's Team additions table.
**Done when:** The rows are pushed and the team has seen them in chat.
