## Task 1 · 🔥 Make 2 content pieces from real user questions — a reply video + an FAQ carousel (100–120 min)

**Why:** The hook system's highest rule is that the viewer's words beat ours. A video that answers an actual comment, hooked with that comment's exact wording, is the most honest content we can make — and the raw material (reviews, comments) is readable by anyone with 15 minutes.
**Where:** CapCut + Canva; source: `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-feedback.md` if it exists, else the store reviews + post comments directly.
**Tools:** CapCut, Canva, `buzzend-footage/`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` (category 11 — social proof), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (templates 1, 4), `assets/screens-clean/`.

**Steps:**
1. Get the questions: use the newest `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/sprint-feedback.md` if it's there. If it's missing or stale, source them yourself — 15 minutes reading the store reviews and every comment on our posts, copying real questions verbatim (typos included; private names as initials). Pick ONE question for the reply video (the most-asked or most skeptical — "no way this is real-time" energy) and 4–6 for the FAQ carousel.
2. **Reply video (20–30s):** open on the real question as the hook (screen-record the actual comment where permission/visibility allows, or set the verbatim phrase as on-screen text with initials only). Answer on camera in one plain sentence, then PROVE it: a real counted set or the real screen that answers them. §40 pre-flight if you film a new demo. End card: one CTA, "Try your first AI workout."
3. Auto-captions proofed, 1080×1920 clean export + cover frame.
4. **FAQ carousel (5–7 slides, 1080×1080):** slide 1 hook = the best question verbatim in quotes; one question per slide with a one-line human answer (no tech-speak — say "it watches your movement and counts your reps"); imagery only from `assets/screens-clean/`; last slide = one CTA. Match the approved store-shot look, Energy Orange palette.
5. Banned-words pass on both pieces against `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`, plus the full review-card self-check (checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`): real counts on the exact phone, one CTA each.
6. Drop both in **READY/** — `day11-reply-video-master.mp4` (+ cover) and `day11-faq-carousel` (export + Canva link) — log both in the tracker and mark the answered questions "turned into content" in the hooks-inbox of `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`. Avinaya verifies async same day; fixes ship as follow-ups. Nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** `day11-reply-video-master.mp4` + a 5–7 slide FAQ carousel draft, both in READY/ and logged.
**Done when:** Both pieces open with a user's exact words and the hooks-inbox rows they came from are marked used.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Refresh the hook bank with 3 hooks from the edit room (30 min)

**Why:** Editing all week means you've watched the footage more than anyone — the moments that made YOU stop scrolling are hooks nobody has written down yet. The bank only compounds if the people closest to the material feed it.
**Where:** `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` (categories + writing rules).

**Steps:**
1. Write down the 3 strongest moments you kept noticing while editing (a rejected rep, the sound of the count, a streak flame appearing…) — one line each.
2. Turn each into a hook with its category's formula: ≤10 words on screen, a real number beats an adjective, passes the sound-off test.
3. Say each out loud; keep the wording that sounds like a person.
4. Add all 3 to the Team additions table in `hook-bank.md`: hook · category · source ("edit-room, sprint-15") · status NEW.
5. Commit, push, and drop the 3 in team chat — one may fit Day 12's experiment or the weekend push; no replies needed.

**Deliverable:** 3 new NEW-status rows in the hook bank's Team additions table.
**Done when:** The rows are pushed and posted in team chat.
