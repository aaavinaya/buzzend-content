# Day 11 — Avinaya

**Objective:** Turn creator replies into one or more concrete collabs, bump the silent Day-8 batch exactly once, and sync the team on what the collab needs.
**Total time:** ~2h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Creator follow-ups + lock the first collab (75–90 min)

**Why:** Most creator replies come after a gentle second touch — but only one, and only after a respectful gap. And a reply without a concrete next step dies; today "interested" becomes a named challenge with a date.
**Where:** IG/TikTok/YouTube DMs from the Buzzend account; `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (the follow-up variant).

**Steps:**
1. In `Outreach`, filter the **Day-8 batch** with status `No response` — sent 3 days ago, they get today's single bump. The Day-9 batch is only 2 days old: it waits until Day 12+. Nobody ever gets a second bump.
2. Send the 2-line follow-up variant from `outreach-templates.md` — friendly, zero pressure. Coordinate with Riya first so nobody gets double-messaged.
3. For every `Replied` / `Interested` creator: answer personally and put the concrete offer on the table — a camera-verified challenge with their name on it (built like our "7-Day Squat Starter", e.g. "[Name]'s Push-Up Week"), using our real exercises (squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges); we build it, they announce it to their audience, we feature them to ours.
4. For anyone who says yes, agree the specifics in the thread: challenge name, exercise + goal, start date, who posts what and when.
5. Record every agreement in `Outreach`: Status = `Agreed` + columns for challenge name, start date, next step + owner. Anything still fuzzy stays `Interested` with a next-step note.
6. If a creator raises payment: don't negotiate — log it for the founders (playbook rule) and keep the free collab on the table.

**Deliverable:** Every Day-8 non-responder bumped once; every responder answered; agreements in `Outreach` with name + date.
**Done when:** No row from Days 8–9 has a stale status, and any `Agreed` row could be executed by someone else from the sheet alone.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 9 · Task 1 (statuses must be current before you bump anyone).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Run the 15-minute team sync (15–20 min)

**Why:** Outreach, store experiments, and content now feed each other daily — 15 focused minutes keeps three people rowing the same direction without meetings eating the sprint.
**Where:** Call or huddle; decisions land in team chat + the tracker.
**Tools:** Growth Tracker, team chat.

**Steps:**
1. Agenda, 5 minutes each, no drift:
2. **Outreach scoreboard** — sent / replied / interested / agreed from `Outreach`; what's working in the DMs that got replies.
3. **The collab** — what the first agreed (or closest-to-agreed) collab needs: challenge build, announcement content, dates; assign each item an owner and a day.
4. **Gaurab's local-gym list** (Day 8 · Task 2) — approve, park, or drop each of the 5; approved ones get an owner and a send day.
5. Post the decisions in team chat and add owners/dates to the tracker before you hang up.

**Deliverable:** Decisions + owners in team chat and the tracker.
**Done when:** Every open item from the three agenda points has a name and a day next to it.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 11 · Task 1 (bring current statuses to the call).
