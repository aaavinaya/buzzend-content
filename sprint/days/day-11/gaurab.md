# Day 11 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Earn genuine standing in the two best Discord servers, then write the map of exactly where each community lets us show up.
**Total time:** ~2h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Discord participation day — help, zero links (75–90 min)

**Why:** Discord bans pitchers and remembers helpers. Two servers from your Day-6 research looked promising — today we become a familiar, useful name in them, which is the only currency that later makes a mention welcome.
**Where:** The 2 most promising servers from your Day-6 research.
**Tools:** Discord account, your Day-6 rules summaries, Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the 2 servers from your Day-6 notes with the most active beginner-fitness channels and the friendliest rules.
2. Re-read each server's rules (2 min each) — where self-promo is allowed, link policy, which channels are for questions.
3. Spend ~35–40 minutes per server: answer questions in help/beginner channels properly (form doubts, staying consistent, counting reps, home setups), share one genuinely useful tip of our own, join an ongoing conversation like a member — not a rep.
4. Zero links and zero app mentions unless someone directly asks what you use — then one honest line with full disclosure: "I helped build it."
5. Log in the tracker next to your Day-6 notes: server, channels, what you answered, any reactions or DMs that came back.

**Deliverable:** Real participation logged in both servers — questions answered, zero unprompted promo.
**Done when:** 3+ genuine answers per server are logged, and any Buzzend mention in the log was asked for and disclosed.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Write the "where we're welcome" summary (30–45 min)

**Why:** Between Day-6 research, two Reddit rounds, and today's Discord work, we now know how each community actually treats us. Writing it down once means Days 12+ spend effort only where it can land.
**Where:** This repo — new file `buzzend-marketing/12-community/where-were-welcome.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, your Day-6 rules summaries, Day-9 Reddit logs, today's Discord notes, Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Gather everything: Day-6 Reddit/Facebook-group/Discord rules summaries, Day-9 round-2 thread reactions, today's participation log.
2. One table row per community: **Name · Platform · Size/activity · What's allowed** (links? app mentions? required disclosure?) **· How we've shown up so far · Opportunity rank 1–5**.
3. Rank honestly: rules-friendliness × audience fit × how our contributions were actually received — not how big the community is.
4. Close with 3 lines: the top 2 communities to keep investing in, one to drop, and why.
5. Commit, push, link the file in the tracker, and post the top-2 conclusion in team chat.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/12-community/where-were-welcome.md` on `main`, linked in the tracker.
**Done when:** Every community we've researched has a row with a rank, and the top 2 are named with reasons.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 11 · Task 1 (today's notes feed the ranking).
