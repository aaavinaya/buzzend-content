# Day 10 — Riya

**Objective:** Turn the most-asked question — "how does it actually count?" — into a clear 5-slide carousel, plus a story version people can tap through to the app.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Create and publish the "How the AI rep counter works" carousel (90–120 min)

**Why:** Half our comments boil down to "does this really work?". A carousel answers it once, keeps getting saved and shared, and lives on the grid where every profile visitor sees it.
**Where:** Canva; publish to Instagram feed + Facebook.
**Tools:** Canva, `assets/screens-clean/` (ai-counter.png, capture.png, real-ai.png, leaderboard.png), `brand/design-guide.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (templates 4–6), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. New Canva design, 1080×1080, 5 slides, Energy Orange palette — match the look of the finished store shots in `store-assets/ios/` (that phrasing and style is already approved; rules in `brand/design-guide.md`).
2. **Slide 1 — hook:** pick from the hook bank, e.g. "Can AI count my push-ups?" or "Why wasn't this rep counted?" — big text over one clean screen (`ai-counter.png` or `real-ai.png`).
3. **Slide 2 — "Point your camera":** `capture.png`, one line on propping the phone so it can see you.
4. **Slide 3 — "Do your reps — it counts out loud":** `real-ai.png`; name the five exercises it counts: squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges.
5. **Slide 4 — "Your camera-verified score posts to the challenge":** use a still from Gaurab's leaderboard capture if it has arrived; otherwise `assets/screens-clean/leaderboard.png`. Don't block on him.
6. **Slide 5 — CTA:** "Try your first AI workout." — one CTA, store links stay in bio (from `sprint/config.js`).
7. Proofread against the banned-words list in `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`; only `screens-clean/` or permission-cleared imagery on any slide.
8. Export and publish to IG + Facebook; caption per templates 4 (Carousel) and 5 (Caption); log both URLs in the tracker `Social` tab; reply to everything in the first hour.

**Deliverable:** 5-slide carousel live on IG + FB, URLs in the tracker, Canva link in team chat.
**Done when:** Both post URLs are logged and every slide passes the imagery + wording check.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 10 · Task 2 (slide-4 footage — use it if it arrives, never block on it).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Story version with a "try it" link sticker (30 min)

**Why:** The feed post explains; the story converts — a link sticker is the only one-tap route from Instagram to the store.
**Where:** Instagram Stories (Buzzend account).
**Tools:** Canva, `sprint/config.js`, the finished carousel slides.

**Steps:**
1. In Canva, resize the 3 strongest slides (hook, "counts out loud", CTA) to 1080×1920 — reposition text for the story safe zones.
2. Post them as a 3-frame story sequence.
3. On frame 2, add a poll or question sticker ("Would you trust AI to count your reps?") — sticker taps are ranking signals and the answers feed the hook bank.
4. On the final frame, add the link sticker labeled "try it" pointing at the store link from `sprint/config.js`.
5. Save the sequence to an appropriate highlight, and note the story in the tracker `Social` tab.

**Deliverable:** 3-frame story live with poll + working link sticker.
**Done when:** Tapping the sticker on a phone opens our real store page.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 10 · Task 1.
