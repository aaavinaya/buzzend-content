## Task 1 · 🔥 Cut the creator-collab pitch demo — 20s, every aspect ratio (75–90 min)

**Why:** Creator follow-up DMs go out tomorrow, and "want to see it count?" converts far better with a 20-second clip attached than with a paragraph. The clip is the offer made visible: the camera counts for real, and a challenge can carry their name.
**Where:** CapCut; footage from your master set + whatever b-roll is banked.
**Tools:** CapCut, `buzzend-footage/master-set/` (best wide + counter close-up), `broll-04-leaderboard.mp4` if banked, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the strongest 3 moments from the library: your best counter close-up (number visibly climbing), the best wide proof shot, and a leaderboard/challenge beat — use `broll-04-leaderboard.mp4` if it's in READY/ or the drive, otherwise screen-record the real leaderboard yourself on your own device. The collab offer is a camera-verified challenge with the creator's name on it, so the challenge machinery must appear.
2. Cut a tight 20 seconds: counter close-up first (moving by second 1), wide proof, challenge/leaderboard payoff. On-screen text ≤10 words per screen, e.g. "The camera counts — for real." then "Challenges keep the score honest."
3. No hard sell: this is a demo for a DM, not an ad — end on the app name, no CTA end card, no store link (that conversation happens in the thread; links come from `sprint/config.js` when needed).
4. Auto-captions proofed (it will be watched muted inside a DM). Banned-words pass on all text.
5. Export three aspect ratios — `collab-pitch-9x16.mp4` (1080×1920), `collab-pitch-1x1.mp4` (1080×1080), `collab-pitch-16x9.mp4` (1920×1080) — keeping each small enough to send in a DM without a download link (roughly under 20 MB; trim bitrate, not seconds).
6. Drop all three in **READY/** under a `day10-collab-pitch` prefix, log paths in the tracker with one line saying which file fits which platform DM. Avinaya verifies async same day; fixes ship as follow-ups. Nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** Three pitch-demo exports in READY/, paths and per-ratio notes logged in the tracker.
**Done when:** All three exports sit in READY/ with names that say the ratio, each under ~20 MB and playing clean muted at phone size.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 3 · Task 1 (the master set).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Design the UGC filming-tips one-pager in Canva (40–50 min)

**Why:** On Day 13 our most active users get asked for honest testimonial clips, and they'll need filming tips. A friendly one-page graphic beats a wall of text in a DM — and it quietly protects the footage quality of our best-converting future content.
**Where:** Canva.
**Tools:** Canva, `brand/design-guide.md` (Energy Orange palette), `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (tone).

**Steps:**
1. Create a 1080×1350 Canva design (reads well in a DM and on a phone), brand colors `#ff6b00` / `#fff8f2` / ink `#222`, headline like "Filming yourself with Buzzend — 6 quick tips".
2. The six tips, short and warm: 1 prop the phone so the camera sees your whole body · 2 decent light, window in front of you not behind · 3 counter volume up so we hear it count · 4 one continuous take, no cuts needed · 5 film vertical (9:16) · 6 don't polish it — real beats pretty.
3. Add the one rule as a friendly footer: "Real reps only — the whole point is the camera counting for real."
4. No CTA, no store link — this goes to people who already use the app.
5. Banned-words pass, export as PNG, save the template in the shared Canva space named "UGC filming tips", drop the export in **READY/** named `day10-ugc-tips.png` and log the links in the tracker + team chat for the Day-13 asks — no confirmation needed.

**Deliverable:** "UGC filming tips" one-pager — Canva template + exported PNG in READY/, paths logged.
**Done when:** The template is saved in the shared Canva space and the PNG is in READY/ with its tracker row filled.
