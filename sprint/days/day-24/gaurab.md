## Task 1 · 🔥 Search-terms + conversion deep dive → "what search is telling us" one-pager (90–110 min)

**Why:** The Day-17 metadata round is a week old — old enough for the stores to react. This one-pager is what decides the Day-27 ASO iteration, so it has to say things plainly enough to act on.
**Where:** play.google.com/console + App Store Connect; the shared drive READY/ folder.
**Tools:** Play Console, ASC, Growth Tracker (`Sprint2-Baseline`, `Store-Android`, `Store-iOS`), `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Play Console → Store performance → Search: pull every search term since Day 17 with visitors and conversion. Paste into `Store-Android`, dated, next to the `Sprint2-Baseline` snapshot.
2. Play Console → Statistics: store listing visitors, acquisitions, and conversion rate for Days 17–23 vs the baseline window.
3. App Store Connect → Analytics: iOS search-traffic volume and conversion (ASC won't give the words — note the volume honestly and say so, same as Sprint 1 did).
4. Mark every term against the baseline snapshot: **GREW** (more visitors than baseline), **FLAT**, **DIED**, or **NEW** (appeared since the metadata round). NEW terms are the metadata round talking.
5. Write the one-pager — keep it to one page, plain words: (a) the 5 terms bringing the most people and their trend, (b) which Day-17 changes visibly worked (NEW/GREW terms we targeted), (c) which did nothing (DIED/FLAT terms we bet on), (d) both stores' conversion vs baseline in one line each, (e) the 3 phrase changes Day 27 should make, each justified by a term.
6. Drop it in **READY/** dated "Day 24 — what search is telling us" — Avinaya reviews async — and note its location in the tracker.

**Deliverable:** Fresh search/conversion data in the tracker + the one-pager in READY/.
**Done when:** Every baseline term has a GREW/FLAT/DIED mark, NEW terms are listed, and the one-pager names 3 justified phrase changes.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Ask 5 more genuine users for honest reviews — different people (40–50 min)

**Why:** Review velocity works as a steady drip, not a burst. Five more real users, five more honest asks — and Squad Squats has been running long enough to have its own engaged people now.
**Where:** Buzzend app (both challenge leaderboards) + wherever we already talk to each person; `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. List participants with 3+ active days across the 7-Day Squat Starter and Squad Squats leaderboards.
2. Cross-check `Outreach`: strike everyone asked on Day 7, 13, or 19. Today's 5 are new names — Squad Squats joiners you've actually interacted with are ideal.
3. Anyone you've never spoken with gets a warm conversation first, not a review ask.
4. Message each of the 5 individually, by name, referencing their real progress. Your own words each time; honest review, whatever they really think.
5. Hard rules hold: no incentives, no asking for stars, no nagging. One ask, then let it go.
6. Log each in `Outreach`: name, challenge + days active, date, where you know them from.

**Deliverable:** 5 new honest review asks, logged.
**Done when:** All 5 `Outreach` rows show new names with 3+ active days and a sent date.
