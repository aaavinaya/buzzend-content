## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish the collab announcement + coordinate cross-posting (75–90 min)

**Why:** A creator's audience trusts the creator, not us — the whole value of the collab is their handle next to a challenge they chose to put their name on. Go-live day is about making them look great and making joining frictionless.
**Where:** IG + TikTok, the creator's DM thread, Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab, shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone, the Day-19 collab plan, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, Canva, `brand/design-guide.md`, `assets/screens-clean/`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (template #17 — the promises we made).

**Steps:**
1. Confirm in the app that the creator's challenge is live in Discover exactly as agreed — name spelled right, joinable. If it isn't there yet, or no collab was agreed this week, the slot never dies: publish your strongest challenge-week content in its place, send the creator a warm "we're ready when you are" in the existing thread, and move the announcement to the next open slot — same-day decision, no waiting on anyone.
2. Pull the announcement asset from **READY/** (Aditya's edit). Fallback: build the card yourself in Canva — Energy Orange, `assets/screens-clean/` imagery only, the creator's handle big and unmissable.
3. Publish with the creator's @handle in the caption and ONE CTA: join their challenge (the exact Discover search name from the collab plan). No banned words, no overclaiming their endorsement — they said what they said, nothing more.
4. Coordinate in the DM thread: send them our live URL, ask when their post goes up, offer to reshare theirs to our story the moment it's live — their words, no script, exactly as template #17 promised. Anything they want changed or removed, do it same-day.
5. Reshare their post to our story when it appears; reply to every comment on the announcement and route challenge-join questions to `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squad-squats.md`-style steps.
6. Log: announcement URL in `Social`, the collab row in `Outreach` updated to `Live` + date, and a one-line status copy in **READY/** for Avinaya's async sweep.

**Deliverable:** Collab announcement live with the creator's handle (or the fallback slot published + new date set), cross-posting coordinated, everything logged.
**Done when:** The announcement URL is in the tracker, the creator has our link and we have their timing, and the Outreach row reads `Live` — or the step-1 fallback is fully executed and logged.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 19 · Task 1 (the collab plan — the step-1 fallback covers a missing or delayed collab).
