## Task 1 · 🔥 Finalize the collab launch video with the creator's end-card → READY/ early (60–75 min)

**Why:** Today a creator posts with us, and the one thing that can't slip is our side of the assets. Kit v2 was built for exactly this morning: personalize, check, ship — before their audience wakes up, not after.
**Where:** CapCut + Canva (the Day-19 end-card template).
**Tools:** The Day-19 kit (`day19-collab-demo-20s.mp4`, the end-card Canva template linked in the tracker), the tracker's collab row (creator handle + any footage they sent, saved to READY/ by whoever runs the collab), checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. First thing in the morning: read the tracker's collab row for the confirmed creator handle, and check **READY/** for any clip the creator sent for the challenge video.
2. Duplicate the Canva end-card template, drop the creator's handle into the `@CREATOR-HANDLE` slot, export.
3. Assemble the launch video: if creator footage is in READY/, cut it to the challenge beats (their attempt + our counter proof from the kit demo) with the personalized end-card; if no creator footage landed, ship the kit's 20s demo with their personalized end-card — it was built pitch-quality for exactly this fallback. If even the handle isn't confirmed in the tracker by mid-morning, export with the blank-slot end-card, name it `-HANDLE-PENDING`, and note it in the tracker — the swap takes 5 minutes whenever the handle lands. Never wait.
4. One CTA ("Challenge a friend." — it's challenge content), captions proofed, 1080×1920, cover frame featuring both the counter and the creator credit.
5. Self-check against checklist #7 — extra care that nothing implies counts or results the creator's footage doesn't actually show — and drop in **READY/** as `day24-collab-launch-master.mp4` + cover + `day24-collab-endcard-<handle>.png`. Log paths and flag the row LAUNCH ASSET — LIVE TODAY.

**Deliverable:** Finalized collab launch video + personalized end-card in READY/ before midday.
**Done when:** The launch needs nothing further from you — the file is named, logged, and the end-card carries the right handle (or is flagged HANDLE-PENDING with a 5-minute swap path).
