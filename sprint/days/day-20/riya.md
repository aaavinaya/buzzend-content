## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish 2 posts + write the #CountMyReps contest rules (90–110 min)

**Why:** User clips of the counter counting are our best-converting content, but a contest without written rules turns into DM chaos and permission mistakes. Writing it two days early means Avinaya reviews async and Day 22 is pure launch.
**Where:** Platforms per today's calendar rows; new doc `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md`; shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Laptop, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (#5, #6), `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` (approved wording).

**Steps:**
1. Publish today's 2 calendar slots on the Day-17 recipe (READY/ first, `social-media/rendered/` fallback or same-day reschedule; caption self-check; URLs into `Social`).
2. Create `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md` in plain language — **how to enter:** post publicly on IG or TikTok with **#CountMyReps** showing a real counted set — the Buzzend counter visible or audible counting your squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, or lunges. Real sets only; any of the five exercises counts.
3. **What entrants get:** the best entry each week is featured on Buzzend's profiles (first name + handle, their exact words). That's the whole prize — no purchases required, no payment, no incentives beyond being featured. Budget is $0 and the contest stays honest because of it.
4. **Permission rule, written into the doc:** we repost or feature an entry only after the entrant confirms in writing in their own thread. Anything involving a minor or an injury/safety claim goes to the founders per the community playbook escalation list — never featured.
5. Pre-write the Day-22 launch kit in the same doc: launch caption per templates #5–6 (first line restates the hook, ONE CTA: "Post your counted set with #CountMyReps"), the pinned-comment text, and a 3-line story version. No banned words anywhere.
6. Drop a copy of the doc in **READY/** for Avinaya's async review and log the path in the tracker — launch happens Day 22 regardless; review notes become edits before launch or follow-ups after.

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live + `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md` (rules + launch kit) with a copy in READY/.
**Done when:** The doc answers how-to-enter / what-winners-get / the permission rule, the launch captions exist, and the READY/ copy is dropped.
