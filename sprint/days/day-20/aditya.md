## Task 1 · 🔥 Batch-film #2 — 6 takes, at least 2 marked hook experiments (100–120 min)

**Why:** Repeating winners keeps the machine running; experiments keep it learning. Batch 2 does both: four takes bank the proven format, two test NEW hooks from the bank — marked, so when the numbers land nobody has to guess which video was the experiment.
**Where:** Your filming spot, one setup for the whole session.
**Tools:** Phone (Buzzend + camera), prop, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`, `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`, shared drive, tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Plan the 6 on paper first: 4 takes in the proven winning format (your Day-16 pick — rotate exercises so the feed doesn't repeat), 2 takes as EXPERIMENTS — each with a hook you haven't filmed yet, status `NEW` in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (e.g. "Put your phone down." or "What happens when Buzzend can't see you?"). One changed variable per experiment: the hook. Everything else stays the proven format.
2. Write all scripts before the camera comes out — 60–80 spoken words, the fixed beats, read aloud twice, banned words out. 20 minutes each, no more.
3. §40 pre-flight per exercise used today, on this exact phone in this exact position. A miscounting exercise gets swapped and flagged, never worked around.
4. Film all 6 back-to-back: wide PROOF + counter close-up per concept, 2 takes minimum per shot, real reps and real counts throughout. Each concept also yields its children per the batching rule in `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/production-pipeline.md`: one best-3-seconds story moment and one alternate-hook first-2-seconds take.
5. Upload to `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch2/`, labeled `<concept>-<shot>-take<N>.mp4`, experiments labeled with an `EXP-` prefix.
6. Log the take list in the tracker and add both experiments to the Experiments section: hook · category · changed variable · file names — and mark those hooks "in test" in the hook bank so they get a WON/LOST verdict later.

**Deliverable:** 6 logged takes in `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch2/`, with 2 clearly marked hook experiments in the tracker and the hook bank.
**Done when:** The take log tells anyone which two takes are experiments and what single variable each one tests, and every count shown was real.
