## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish Reel #1 to Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (60–75 min)

**Why:** Everything since Day 1 was preparation; this is the first content that can actually produce store visits. Posting at the playbook times and logging URLs today is what makes Day-8 timing decisions and Day-15 proof possible.
**Where:** Instagram + YouTube apps, steps per `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md` (Per-post publishing steps).
**Tools:** Phone, the `day-4-launch` kit rows in the tracker, bitly.com (free), publishing checklist #8 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Before anything: create one free Bitly account and shorten the App Store link from `sprint/config.js`. This is our first trackable link — Bitly's free click count tells us which bio actually sends people.
2. Put the Bitly link in the IG bio and the YouTube channel description, then tap both on your phone to confirm they open the store (publishing sin #3).
3. At the staged time (11:30–12:30): upload the Reel #1 master natively to IG Reels — caption exactly as staged (line 1 = hook restated, one CTA "Try your first AI workout — link in bio", 3–5 hashtags), cover frame = person + counter, share to feed ON.
4. After posting, reshare the Reel to your IG Story.
5. At the staged Shorts time (alongside TikTok, evening): upload the same clean master to YouTube Shorts — searchable title under 60 chars from the kit, 150-word description with the link in lines 1–3, pin a comment with the app name.
6. Run checklist #8 on both posts; record post URL + exact publish time for each in the tracker `Social` tab and today's `Daily Numbers` row.
7. Stay 30–60 min replying after the IG post goes live — Riya owns the full engagement pass, but the first hour is everyone's job.

**Deliverable:** Reel #1 live on IG + Shorts, Bitly link live in both bios, URLs + times logged in the tracker.
**Done when:** Both post URLs are in the tracker and tapping each bio link opens our store page.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 3 · Task 1 (the master) + Riya · Day 3 · Task 2 (the staged kit).

## Task 2 · 🔥 Personal-network outreach: list 20, message 5 (45–60 min)

**Why:** Our first honest users come from people who already trust us. Five personal messages from you today beat five hundred impressions from strangers — and their confusion becomes our best product feedback.
**Where:** Your own contacts (WhatsApp/Messages/DMs) + the tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (first-message template — for tone, not copy-paste), CTA discipline from template #6 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md`.

**Steps:**
1. List 20 real contacts in the `Outreach` tab — friends, family, coworkers, gym partners — with one column for "why Buzzend fits them" (does home workouts, competitive with friends, always losing count…).
2. Pick today's 5. For each, write a message from scratch in your own voice — use the community-playbook template's shape (personal first line about THEM, one plain sentence on what Buzzend does, one honest ask) but rewrite every line per person. If two messages look alike, rewrite one.
3. The ask is always: "try it and tell me honestly what confused you" — never "please download", never "leave a rating" (ratings come only from genuine users who love it, unprompted).
4. Include the right store link from `sprint/config.js` (App Store or Play depending on their phone). One ask per message — no "download AND follow AND share".
5. Send all 5. Note replies as they come in.
6. Mark the 5 as SENT in the `Outreach` tab with today's date (Riya is setting up the logging format today — match her columns).

**Deliverable:** 20-contact list + 5 personalized messages sent, logged in `Outreach`.
**Done when:** 5 messages are sent, each visibly different from the others, and all 20 rows exist for the coming days.
