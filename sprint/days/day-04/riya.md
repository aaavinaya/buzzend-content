## Task 1 · 🔥 Engagement hour: answer everything, follow 20, comment 10 (60–75 min)

**Why:** Early reply velocity is a ranking signal on every platform — the first hours of our first posts train the algorithms. And commenting genuinely on others' posts puts our name in the beginner-fitness neighborhood without spending anything.
**Where:** IG, TikTok, YouTube, FB — on today's live posts and around the niche. Reply patterns from `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` and the Engagement playbook section of `social-media/week1-content-calendar.md`.
**Tools:** Phone, the day-4-launch tracker rows (for each post's live time), notifications ON for all four apps today.

**Steps:**
1. Set an alarm for each post's staged time (IG midday, FB early afternoon, TikTok + Shorts evening). Your window starts when each goes live.
2. Reply to **every** comment on today's posts within 2 hours — short, warm, human; corporate tone banned. Pin the best comment on each post.
3. Use the playbook reply patterns: skeptics get leaned into, never argued with ("That's exactly what everyone says right before it counts their squats out loud 😏"); tag-chains get replies to both people; never delete criticism, only spam.
4. Paste any question or great audience phrasing into the hooks-inbox in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` — audience words become future hooks.
5. Follow 20 relevant accounts per platform: home-workout and fitness-challenge creators, beginner-fitness communities — the growwithjo/MadFit archetype from the community playbook, not celebrities and not shredded gym elites.
6. Leave 10 genuine comments on other people's fitness posts across platforms — actually helpful or encouraging, zero app plugs, no links. We're being a good neighbor, not an ad.
7. Log in the tracker `Social` tab: comments received/answered per post, and anything that flopped or popped.

**Deliverable:** 100% of comments answered within 2 hours, 20 follows per platform, 10 genuine outbound comments, notes in the tracker.
**Done when:** No unanswered comment older than 2 hours exists on any of today's posts by end of day.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 4 · Task 1 + Gaurab · Day 4 · Task 1 (the posts must be live).

## Task 2 · 🔥 Personal-network outreach: list 20, message 5 (45–60 min)

**Why:** Three lists of 20 gives the team 60 warm contacts and 15 honest first messages this week — that's more likely to produce our first activated users than anything the algorithm does today.
**Where:** Your own contacts (WhatsApp/Messages/DMs) + the tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (first-message template — tone reference only), CTA discipline from template #6 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md`.

**Steps:**
1. List 20 real contacts in the `Outreach` tab with a "why Buzzend fits them" column — think of who complains about gym prices, does home workouts, or is competitive in the group chat.
2. Pick today's 5 and write each message from scratch in your voice: personal first line about them, one plain sentence on Buzzend ("the phone camera counts your reps out loud"), one honest ask.
3. The ask: "try it and tell me honestly what confused you" — never beg for downloads, never ask for ratings. If two messages look alike, rewrite one.
4. Include the right store link from `sprint/config.js` for their phone. One ask per message.
5. Send all 5 and watch for replies during your engagement windows.

**Deliverable:** 20-contact list + 5 personalized messages sent.
**Done when:** 5 visibly-different messages are out and the 20 rows exist for the coming days.
