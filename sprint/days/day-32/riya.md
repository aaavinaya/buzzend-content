# Day 32 — Riya

**Objective:** Close the #CountMyReps contest properly and keep honest reviews flowing.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Contest wrap: feature the winners (75–90 min)

**Why:** A contest that ends silently burns the people who entered; featuring them is free loyalty and free content.
**Where:** IG/TikTok/FB + the contest entries.
**Tools:** Canva, tracker Outreach tab (permissions column).

**Steps:**
1. Collect every #CountMyReps entry; confirm written permission for each you'll feature (no permission = no repost).
2. Publish the winners post (use Aditya's feature edits from READY/ if present, else your own Canva quote-cards).
3. Thank every entrant personally in comments/DM — individual messages.
4. Log reach + entries count in the tracker for the readout.

**Deliverable:** Winners featured + every entrant thanked.
**Done when:** Winners post live and permissions logged.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Review-velocity round (30–40 min)

**Why:** Reviews move store rank; genuine engaged users are the only ones we ever ask.
**Where:** In-app leaderboards + DMs.
**Tools:** Tracker Outreach tab.

**Steps:**
1. Pick 5 challenge participants active 3+ days whom we've never asked.
2. Individual, honest asks — no incentives, no star-steering; log each in Outreach.

**Deliverable:** 5 asks logged.
**Done when:** All 5 sent and logged with dates.
