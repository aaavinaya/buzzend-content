# Day 17 — Riya

**Objective:** The 2-a-day cadence goes live — both calendar slots published at their fixed times, no slot ever skipped — and the first 10 creator DMs of sprint 2 go out, each one visibly its own message.
**Total time:** ~2.5–3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish both calendar slots at their fixed times (75–90 min)

**Why:** Today the calendar stops being a plan and becomes a habit. The doubled cadence only compounds if day one of it ships clean — right times, one CTA, everything logged.
**Where:** The platforms named in today's two rows of `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`; assets from the shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone, `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (templates #5 caption, #6 CTA picker), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md` (checklist #8), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Open today's 2 rows in the calendar and pull each planned asset from **READY/**.
2. If a planned asset isn't in READY/, never wait and never skip: publish the best fit from `social-media/rendered/` for that slot's hook, or reschedule the slot to later **today** — either way the calendar row gets a note saying what happened.
3. Self-check each caption against templates #5–6: first line restates the hook, **one** CTA, store links from `sprint/config.js` via the bio (never a typed-out URL in an IG caption), and zero banned words ("perfect form", "guaranteed", "world's first").
4. Run publishing checklist #8, then publish slot 1 at the IG window and slot 2 at the TikTok/Shorts window — the fixed times from `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md`.
5. Stay ~30 min after each post replying to every early comment (early velocity is the whole game); pin the best comment.
6. Log both URLs + publish times in the tracker `Social` tab and mark both calendar rows PUBLISHED (or note the swap/reschedule).

**Deliverable:** 2 posts live at their fixed times, URLs in the `Social` tab, calendar rows updated.
**Done when:** Both rows show PUBLISHED with URLs and every first-30-minutes comment has a reply.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 16 · Task 1 (the calendar — if it's somehow missing, rebuild today's 2 rows in 10 minutes from the WON hooks in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` and continue).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Send 10 personalized creator DMs from template #15 (75–90 min)

**Why:** Ten messages that prove we watched someone's content beat a hundred blasts — sprint 1 already showed the reply rate. Doubling the send volume only works if the personalization survives.
**Where:** Instagram/TikTok DMs from the Buzzend account; Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone (logged into the Buzzend account), Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (template #15), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Take the 10 starred batch-2 rows from the `Outreach` tab, top-down. If fewer than 10 uncontacted candidates exist, research replacements yourself (25 min max, Day-16 vetting rules) and continue.
2. Watch or read each creator's 2–3 most recent posts properly — you need one genuine, specific thing to say per person.
3. Rewrite template #15 fresh for each: first line names a **specific recent post**, 60–90 words, one clear ask, store link from `sprint/config.js`. No two DMs may share a first line; if two look alike, rewrite one.
4. Read each back once before sending — any hype word or second ask gets cut. No payment offers ever; if a creator asks about paid, log it for the founders.
5. Send all 10 spaced through the afternoon (never a burst — that reads as spam).
6. Log each row: Date sent + Status = `Sent` (statuses stay: `Sent / Replied / Interested / Agreed / Declined / No response`). Check for replies this evening and answer within hours.

**Deliverable:** 10 personalized DMs sent, 10 `Outreach` rows updated with date + status.
**Done when:** All 10 rows show `Sent` today and each DM references a different specific post.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 16 · Task 2 (the candidate rows — fallback in step 1 if the bench is short).
