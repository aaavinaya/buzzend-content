# Day 30 — Riya

**Objective:** Sprint 2 ends in public and in order: the "30 days of Buzzend" thanks post goes out with real numbers and real first names (permissioned only), and every log, status, and calendar row is closed so sprint 3 opens with zero archaeology.
**Total time:** ~3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Publish the "30 days of Buzzend" community-thanks post (100–120 min)

**Why:** This is the sprint's public ending and sprint 3's opening handshake: honest numbers earn the audience that watches what we build next, and thanking real people by name turns participants into the community every future challenge starts from.
**Where:** Instagram (+ FB cross-post, TikTok if the format translates), Stories; shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone + laptop, the Growth Tracker, each platform's analytics, the `/go/` link click counts, Canva, `brand/design-guide.md`, `assets/screens-clean/`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (#5), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md` (checklist #8).

**Steps:**
1. Pull real numbers only from what you can read yourself: tracker tabs, each platform's analytics, the tracked `/go/ios` + `/go/android` click counts, the challenge leaderboards in the app. No number you can't see goes in the post; small-but-real is the whole appeal. If a sprint readout sits in **READY/**, cross-check against it — never wait for it.
2. Build the post: Aditya's edit from **READY/** if one is there, else a carousel you make in Canva — Energy Orange from `brand/design-guide.md`, app imagery from `assets/screens-clean/` only, flops stated plainly next to wins, no banned words.
3. Thank challenge finishers and collab creators by **first name only where permission is logged in the tracker** (Day-13/18/23 threads); everyone else gets the collective thank-you. When unsure, collective — a missing name costs nothing, a non-permissioned one costs trust.
4. Caption per template #5: first line restates the hook ("30 days ago we started posting our real numbers…"), ONE CTA, link in bio from `sprint/config.js`. Run checklist #8 and publish at the fixed slot; stay 30–60 min replying to everything — honest-numbers posts attract questions.
5. Stories: a thank-you story for finishers/entrants/creators + a question sticker ("what should we build next?"); paste the answers into the hooks-inbox in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.
6. Log URLs + times in `Social`, mark the calendar's Day-30 THEMED row PUBLISHED, and drop a copy of the published post in **READY/** for Avinaya's async sweep — any number fix ships as a follow-up story or comment, never a deletion.

**Deliverable:** Thanks post + stories live with real, self-read numbers and permissioned names only; copy in READY/; URLs logged.
**Done when:** The post is live, every named person has logged permission, and the READY/ copy exists.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Archive and hand off — Outreach current, calendar closed (50–60 min)

**Why:** The difference between a sprint that compounds and one that evaporates is thirty minutes of closing the books: sprint 3 should inherit statuses and files, not mysteries.
**Where:** Growth Tracker (`Outreach`, `Social`, `Content`), `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Laptop, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/sprint3-winners.md`, `buzzend-marketing/09-content-calendar/sprint3-campaign-brief.md`.

**Steps:**
1. `Outreach` tab: every sprint-2 row ends at a real status — never-contacted `Candidate` rows get marked `Carry to sprint 3`; anything `Sent`/`Bumped` and silent becomes `No response` (and is never contacted again); collab and UGC rows reflect today's truth (`Live`, `Featured`, permission notes attached).
2. Close out `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`: every row shows PUBLISHED + URL, or a one-line note (swapped/rescheduled/killed and why). No blank Status cells.
3. Verify `sprint3-winners.md` and `sprint3-campaign-brief.md` are both linked in the tracker — the two files sprint 3 starts from.
4. Tidy **READY/**: current files stay, stale drafts move to an archive subfolder so Avinaya's sweep sees only what's live.
5. Write one handoff note in the tracker `Content` notes: contest state (entries, features, open permissions), collab state (who's live, who's warm), invite counts, and the two sprint-3 file paths.

**Deliverable:** Outreach fully current, calendar closed out, READY/ tidy, one handoff note.
**Done when:** A stranger could open the tracker and the two sprint-3 files and run day 31 without asking you anything.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 30 · Task 1 (the thanks post's URLs and permissions are part of the close-out).
