## Task 1 · 🔥 Build the 20-creator micro-influencer list (75–90 min)

**Why:** Micro creators (1k–50k followers) actually read their DMs, and their audiences trust them more than any ad. Riya sends the first 5 DMs this afternoon — the quality of her targets is decided right here.
**Where:** Instagram + TikTok search on your phone; `Outreach` tab of the Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (Outreach section).

**Steps:**
1. Re-read the Outreach section of `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (5 min) — we want the beginner-friendly home-workout archetype, not shredded gym elites.
2. Search IG and TikTok: *#homeworkout, #bodyweightworkout, #fitnesschallenge, #beginnerfitness, #noequipment, "30 day challenge"* — open creators these surface, plus "suggested" accounts next to any good one.
3. Qualify each: 1k–50k followers; posts home-workout / bodyweight / challenge content; comment sections are real conversations, not bot walls.
4. Eyeball engagement — open their 3 most recent posts and divide likes by followers: above roughly 3% is good at this size (e.g. 300+ likes on a 10k account). Below ~1%, skip — the audience isn't listening.
5. Add every keeper to `Outreach` with columns: **Handle · Platform · Followers · Why they fit** (one specific line — name an actual post) **· Best contact route** (DM, or email in bio). Leave Status and Date columns empty for Riya.
6. Stop at 20. Star your top 5 — best fit + best engagement — Riya starts with those.

**Deliverable:** 20 creator rows in the `Outreach` tab, all five columns filled, top 5 starred.
**Done when:** Riya can pick any row and know who they are, why we chose them, and how to reach them — without asking you.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Write the creator outreach DM template (30–40 min)

**Why:** Riya personalizes every message, but the offer must be consistent and pre-approved so nobody promises something we can't deliver.
**Where:** This repo — new file `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md`.
**Tools:** Code editor, `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` (approved wording), `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` (first-message example to adapt).

**Steps:**
1. Create `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md`; head the section `## 15 · Creator outreach DM` (continues the 14 numbered templates).
2. Structure: `[personal first line — reference one specific post of theirs]` → who we are (tiny team, free app, the phone camera counts your reps out loud) → the offer → one ask.
3. The offer is value-first and concrete, three things: the app free; we build a **camera-verified challenge with their name on it** (same machinery as our live "7-Day Squat Starter"); we feature them and their challenge to our audience.
4. One ask only: something like "Want to try it and see if it counts your reps?" — nothing else in message one.
5. Add the rules under the template: 60–90 words · no payment promises (if a creator asks about paid, log it for the founders — never negotiate) · no hype words (banned list in `what-is-buzzend.md`) · single follow-up max.
6. Add a 2-line follow-up bump variant (friendly, zero pressure) — Day 11 uses it.
7. Commit, push, ping Riya in the team chat.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` on `main` with the DM template + follow-up variant + rules.
**Done when:** Riya confirms she can write DM #1 from it without asking a question.
