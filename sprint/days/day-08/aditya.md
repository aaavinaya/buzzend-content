# Day 8 — Aditya

**Objective:** Batch-film three concepts in one setup — a week of footage in one session — and rough-cut the strongest one while it's fresh.
**Total time:** ~2.75h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Batch filming day — 3 concepts: 2 PROOF + 1 TOGETHER (100–120 min)

**Why:** The marketing OS batches filming on purpose ("Film Tuesdays" in `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/production-pipeline.md`): one setup, several scripts, a week of proof footage in one session. Yesterday's production list says exactly what week 2 needs — today it gets shot.
**Where:** Your filming spot, one setup for the whole session.
**Tools:** Phone (Buzzend + camera), prop, your Day-7 production list, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the 3 concepts from your production list's "film" rows — 2 from the PROOF pillar, 1 from TOGETHER (mix per `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`). Where the list leaves a free choice, take hooks from the bank, e.g. PROOF: "I did 30 squats. My phone says 28. One of us is wrong." or a trying-to-cheat-the-counter attempt (film the real outcome, whatever it is); TOGETHER: a streak-save or challenge moment.
2. Write the three scripts BEFORE touching the camera — 60–80 spoken words each, the fixed beat structure, read aloud twice, banned words out. 20 minutes per script, no more.
3. §40 pre-flight once per exercise used today: test it on this exact phone in this position; a miscounting exercise gets swapped out and flagged, never worked around.
4. Film all three back-to-back in the one setup: full takes per script (hook close-up, proof wide, any story beat), 2 takes minimum per shot, real reps and real counts throughout.
5. Per the batching rule, each concept must also yield its children: one story-cut moment (best 3 seconds) and one alternate-hook take (same video, different first 2 seconds) — cheap now, gold on Day 12.
6. Upload everything to `buzzend-footage/day8-batch/`, labeled `<concept>-<shot>-take<N>.mp4`, and log the take list in the tracker: concept · hook · files · best take starred.

**Deliverable:** Full logged takes for 3 concepts (plus story cuts and alternate hooks) in `buzzend-footage/day8-batch/`.
**Done when:** All three concepts could be edited by someone else from the take log alone, and every counter shown counted for real.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 7 · Task 2 (the production list names the concepts).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Rough-cut one of the three same-day (40–50 min)

**Why:** The edit you start while the shoot is fresh keeps decisions you'll forget by Thursday — which take breathed, where the hook lands. One rough-cut tonight also derisks the week: if something's missing, you can re-shoot tomorrow in the same setup, not on publish day.
**Where:** CapCut.
**Tools:** CapCut, today's take log, shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the concept with the earliest publish slot in the week-2 plan.
2. Rough-assemble to the script's beats: hook take on frame 1, proof wide, CTA end card. Don't polish — structure first.
3. Auto-captions on, one pass for glaring errors only.
4. Watch it muted at phone size: does the hook read in one glance? Does the counter carry the proof? Note what the fine cut needs.
5. Export `<concept>-roughcut.mp4` to `buzzend-footage/day8-batch/`, note in the tracker what's missing (re-shoot, still, sound), and flag any re-shoot for tomorrow morning while the setup knowledge is fresh.

**Deliverable:** One rough-cut in the shared drive + a fine-cut punch list in the tracker.
**Done when:** The rough-cut plays start to finish and the punch list says exactly what the final edit needs.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 8 · Task 1.
