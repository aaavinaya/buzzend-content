# Day 8 — Riya

**Objective:** Start the first 5 real creator conversations and keep the feed alive with week-2 post #1.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Personalize and send the first 5 creator DMs (75–90 min)

**Why:** Creators get copy-paste pitches daily and delete them on sight. Five messages that prove we actually watched their content will out-perform fifty blasts — and this is the day outreach becomes real.
**Where:** Instagram/TikTok DMs from the Buzzend account; `Outreach` tab of the Growth Tracker.
**Tools:** Phone (logged into the Buzzend account), Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Read the DM template and its rules in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md` (5 min).
2. Take Avinaya's 5 starred creators from `Outreach`. For each: watch/read their 2–3 most recent posts properly — you need one genuine, specific thing to say.
3. Write each DM fresh from the template: first line references a **specific recent post** (name it — "your no-equipment leg day from Tuesday"), 60–90 words total, **one clear ask**. No two DMs may share a first line.
4. Read each back once before sending: would you reply to this? Any hype word or second ask — cut it.
5. Send all 5 from the Buzzend account, spaced through the afternoon (not five in five minutes — that reads as spam).
6. Log each in `Outreach`: Date sent + Status = `Sent`. Statuses we use from here: `Sent / Replied / Interested / Agreed / Declined / No response`.
7. Check for replies this evening and again tomorrow morning — answer within hours, and flag anything warm to Avinaya.

**Deliverable:** 5 personalized DMs sent, 5 `Outreach` rows updated with date + status.
**Done when:** All 5 rows show `Sent` with today's date, and each DM references a different specific post.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 8 · Task 1 (the starred list) + Avinaya · Day 8 · Task 2 (the template).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Publish week-2 post #1 (30–40 min)

**Why:** Outreach only works if a creator who checks our profile finds an active, alive account. Week 2's first post goes out on schedule — the plan you wrote on Day 7 already says what it is.
**Where:** Instagram (+ any second platform the plan names).
**Tools:** Phone, your Day-7 week-2 content plan (linked in the tracker), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (caption + CTA templates), `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. Open your week-2 plan from Day 7 and take today's slot: asset, platform(s), caption source.
2. Prep the caption — check it against templates 5 (Caption) and 6 (CTA picker) in `content-templates.md`; one CTA only; store links come from `sprint/config.js` (bio link, never a typed-out URL in an IG caption).
3. Publish at the planned time.
4. Stay online 30–60 min after posting — reply to every early comment (community playbook: early velocity matters).
5. Log the post URL in the tracker `Social` tab.

**Deliverable:** Week-2 post #1 live, URL in the `Social` tab.
**Done when:** The post is up per the plan and every comment from the first hour has a reply.
