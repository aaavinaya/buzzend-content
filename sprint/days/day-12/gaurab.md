## Task 1 · 🔥 Repost the strongest TikTok in a new time slot and log the timing experiment (75–90 min)

**Why:** Every post so far went out at the fixed evening slot. We've never tested whether a different hour reaches a different (maybe bigger) slice of our audience. Reposting our proven video at a new time isolates timing as the only variable — a clean, free experiment.
**Where:** TikTok app + Growth Tracker `Experiments` tab.
**Tools:** Android phone, TikTok, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. In the tracker, find the TikTok with the most link taps / store-visit correlation so far (winner rule — not likes). Note exactly when it was originally posted.
2. Open TikTok analytics → Followers → most-active hours. Pick the strongest activity window we have NEVER posted in (the playbook's starting slot was 18:00–21:00 — so this is probably a morning or midday window).
3. Repost the same video as a new post: same file, same caption, same hashtags, same cover frame, same pinned comment ("It's Buzzend — App Store & Google Play 🔗 in bio", links from `sprint/config.js` via the bio page). Time is the ONLY thing that changes.
4. Stay 30–60 min after posting replying to comments, same as the original got.
5. Log it in `Experiments` using template #13 from `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/planning-templates.md`: Hypothesis ("We believe posting at [new slot] will improve views + link taps because our followers are most active then"), the ONE variable (posting time), Control (original post + its first-72h numbers), Metric (views, link taps, store visits in 72h), Review date = Day 15.
6. Check Avinaya's experiment row from this morning — confirm you two used different videos.

**Deliverable:** Repost live at the new slot + one complete `Experiments` row with a Day-15 review date.
**Done when:** Both experiment rows sit in the tab, each with exactly one variable and a review date.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Android polish pass on everything we've published (45–60 min)

**Why:** We publish from iPhones and check on iPhones. Half our audience watches on Android, where compression, cropping, and caption size behave differently. Fifteen minutes of watching like a user catches problems no analytics dashboard will ever show.
**Where:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook apps on your Android device.
**Tools:** Android phone, Growth Tracker `Notes` (or a `Content` tab row per post).

**Steps:**
1. Open our profile on each platform on your Android phone and play every published video (Reels #1–3, the challenge announcement, the carousel, today's repost) at normal brightness, no headphones.
2. For each, check: does it play smoothly, is the burned-in caption text readable on a small screen, does the counter number on screen stay legible, is anything important cropped by the UI (buttons, captions, progress bar)?
3. Check the carousel: do all slides render sharp, or did compression blur the text?
4. Log every issue in the tracker as a future-edit note, e.g. "caption font too small on 6.1-inch screens — go bigger from now on" or "counter sits under the TikTok caption zone — keep it upper-third".
5. Turn the notes into a short "Android checks" list and paste it into the team chat — Riya and Avinaya apply it to every future edit (and Day 15's SOP will absorb it).

**Deliverable:** Per-post check notes in the tracker + the "Android checks" list in team chat.
**Done when:** Every published video has been watched on Android and has a ✓ or a written fix note.
