# Day 12 — Riya

**Objective:** Turn the weekend into a challenge-joining moment: countdown stories planned for both days, plus a personal invite to every human who has actually engaged with us this sprint.
**Total time:** ~2.5–3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Plan the weekend countdown stories and personally invite our engaged people to the challenge (90–110 min)

**Why:** The 7-Day Squat Starter challenge is our best conversion tool, and weekends are when beginners actually have time to start. A named, personal invite converts many times better than any broadcast — and we already know exactly who cares: they told us in the comments and DMs.
**Where:** Instagram + TikTok (comments/DM threads), Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Plan 2 countdown stories — one for Saturday, one for Sunday: story 1 = "Weekend plan: join the 7-Day Squat Starter — day 1 is just 10 squats, the camera counts them" with the countdown/poll sticker; story 2 = a real leaderboard moment or participant shout-out (first names only, with permission) + "still time to join". Write the exact text for both in the tracker and note the posting times (Saturday morning, Sunday morning).
2. Build the invite list: scroll every post's comments, our DM inbox, and repeat likers from this sprint. List every person who commented, DM'd, or has liked us repeatedly — cap at 20. Real engagers only; nobody we've never interacted with.
3. Write each invite INDIVIDUALLY, using their name and referencing what they actually said or did ("You asked if the counter works in a small room — it does, and the squat challenge is the easiest way to see it"). Include the 3 join steps: download Buzzend (link from `sprint/config.js` in our bio) → open Discover → join "7-Day Squat Starter". End with a question, not a pitch.
4. Deliver each invite where the relationship already exists: reply in the DM thread if there is one, otherwise reply to their comment. No cold promo DMs to people who only liked once — the community playbook bans unsolicited promo DMs, and one warm invite beats five awkward ones.
5. Log every invite in the `Outreach` tab: name, platform, what they engaged with, sent date, reply (fill in as they come).
6. Absolute rule: no copy-paste blast. If two invites read identical, rewrite one.

**Deliverable:** 2 written weekend stories (text + times) + up to 20 logged personal invites.
**Done when:** Every row in the invite list shows a personalized message sent (or a reason skipped), and both story texts are ready to post.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Make the "join us" story graphic in Canva (40–50 min)

**Why:** The weekend stories and every future challenge mention need one reusable, on-brand graphic that answers "how do I join?" in three seconds — instead of us retyping the steps forever.
**Where:** Canva (story size, 1080×1920).
**Tools:** Canva, `brand/design-guide.md` (the Energy Orange palette), `assets/screens-clean/` for any app imagery.

**Steps:**
1. Create a 1080×1920 story canvas. Headline: **7-Day Squat Starter** (the real challenge name, nothing invented).
2. Add the 3 join steps as short numbered lines: 1. Get Buzzend (link in bio) · 2. Open Discover · 3. Join "7-Day Squat Starter".
3. Use Energy Orange from `brand/design-guide.md` as the dominant color — this is the shipped app's palette, not teal, not anything from old mockups.
4. If you add app imagery, use only `assets/screens-clean/` renders — never a raw device screenshot.
5. One CTA only ("Challenge a friend." or the join steps themselves — not both a download push and a challenge push).
6. Export and drop the file in the shared assets folder; note the path in the tracker so it slots straight into tomorrow's stories. Leave space in the layout to paste a participant count later.

**Deliverable:** Exported 1080×1920 "join us" story graphic, path logged in the tracker.
**Done when:** The graphic passes a 3-second squint test: challenge name + 3 steps readable on a phone at arm's length.
