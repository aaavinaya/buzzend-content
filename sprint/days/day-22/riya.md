## Task 1 · 🔥 Launch the #CountMyReps contest across platforms (90–110 min)

**Why:** A contest whose only prize is being featured filters for exactly the users we want: real people doing real counted sets in public. Every entry is proof-pillar content we could never fake — and it costs $0.
**Where:** IG + TikTok (+ FB cross-post via Meta Business Suite), Stories, pinned comments; shared drive **READY/** folder.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/countmyreps-contest.md` (your Day-20 launch kit), Canva, `brand/design-guide.md`, `assets/screens-clean/`, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md` (checklist #8).

**Steps:**
1. Check **READY/** first: if Avinaya's async review left notes on the contest doc, fold them in now; if Aditya dropped a contest launch graphic or video, use it. Neither there? Launch anyway — build your own Canva card (Energy Orange, `assets/screens-clean/` imagery, the how-to-enter in 3 short lines) or take the best fit from `social-media/rendered/`. Never wait, never skip.
2. Publish the launch post on IG and TikTok with the captions you pre-wrote Day 20 — first line restates the hook, ONE CTA ("Post your counted set with #CountMyReps"), no banned words. Cross-post to FB via Meta Business Suite. Run checklist #8 before each.
3. Pin the how-to-enter comment on every launch post (from the Day-20 kit): the tag, "counter visible or audible", and "best entry each week gets featured".
4. Post the IG story version with a question sticker ("Which exercise are you entering with?") — squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, lunges.
5. Stay 30–60 min replying to every question warmly; skeptical comments get leaned into, never argued with.
6. Log all URLs + times in the `Social` tab; search the tag tonight and personally welcome any early entry (genuine comment first, feature talk later).

**Deliverable:** Contest live on all platforms — posts + story + pinned comments — URLs logged.
**Done when:** The launch post is live on IG and TikTok with pinned how-to-enter comments, and every launch-hour question has an answer.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 20 · Task 1 (the rules + launch kit — if the doc is missing, write 3-line rules and a caption in 15 minutes and launch anyway; the full doc catches up same-day).
