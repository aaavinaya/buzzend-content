# Day 22 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Put the gym-pilot idea in front of three real local gyms — person to person, from the list we built in Sprint 1 — and keep the daily numbers honest.
**Total time:** ~2h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Send the gym collab pitch to 3 local gyms (75–90 min)

**Why:** A friendly inter-gym challenge ("which crew logs more camera-counted squats?") is a real collab that costs nothing and reaches people who already work out. The Day-8 research found the gyms and named the humans — today we finally send.
**Where:** IG DM / Facebook page / email — whatever route each gym's `Outreach` row names.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab (the Day-8 rows tagged `local`), the 3-line pitch from the shared drive READY/ folder, `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Open the `Outreach` tab and pick the 3 strongest `local` rows from Day 8 — active online, a named contact person, a working route.
2. Grab the 3-line pitch from **READY/** (dropped there on Day 8). If it's not there, redraft it yourself in 10 minutes and continue: line 1 — who we are (local team, free app, camera counts reps); line 2 — the idea (a friendly inter-gym challenge on Buzzend, live leaderboard, their name on it); line 3 — the ask (15 minutes to show them how it works). No hype, no invented numbers.
3. Personalize line 1 for each gym — name the contact person and one true, specific thing about their gym or their posts. If two messages read identical, rewrite one.
4. Mention that a public challenge is already running (Squad Squats) so they can see a live leaderboard before saying yes.
5. Send all 3, one at a time, to the named human — never a generic info@ blast when a person is listed.
6. Update each `Outreach` row: date sent, route used, exact first line, status = "sent — follow-up Day 27 if quiet". No same-day chasing.

**Deliverable:** 3 personalized pitches sent to named contacts, all 3 `Outreach` rows updated.
**Done when:** Each of the 3 rows shows a sent date, the route, and the personalized first line.
