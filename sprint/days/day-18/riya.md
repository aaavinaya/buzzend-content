# Day 18 — Riya

**Objective:** "Challenge a Friend" week opens: the join-steps doc exists and mirrors the app exactly, both posts and a story carry it, and up to 20 sprint-1 engagers get a personal, named invitation.
**Total time:** ~3h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Launch Challenge-a-Friend week — join doc, both posts, story, 20 personal invites (110–130 min)

**Why:** A challenge with friends in it is our best conversion tool, and the people most likely to join are the ones who already talked to us in sprint 1. A named invite converts many times better than any broadcast — and we know exactly who they are.
**Where:** The Buzzend app (Discover), a new doc in `buzzend-marketing/12-community/`, IG + TikTok posts/stories, DM and comment threads, Growth Tracker `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squat-starter.md` (the pattern to copy), `social-media/sprint2-content-calendar.md`, Canva, `brand/design-guide.md` (Energy Orange), `assets/screens-clean/`, `sprint/config.js`.

**Steps:**
1. In the app, open Discover → challenges and find this week's challenge, **Squad Squats**. If it isn't live in the app yet, run the week on the **7-Day Squat Starter** (still live) — never wait; swap the name in a follow-up story once Squad Squats appears.
2. Write `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squad-squats.md` modeled line-for-line on `how-to-join-squat-starter.md`: 5 steps, one action each, store links the same tracked ones the Squat Starter doc uses. Walk every step fresh on your own phone — the doc must mirror the app exactly; anything that doesn't match goes to Avinaya as a note, not a blocker.
3. Publish today's 2 calendar slots as the challenge-week launch posts: assets from **READY/**, fallback the best fit from `social-media/rendered/` or your own Day-12-style Canva join graphic (Energy Orange, `assets/screens-clean/` imagery only, never a raw screenshot). Caption CTA: "Challenge a friend." — one CTA only, no banned words.
4. Post an IG story with the join steps (update the Day-12 "join us" layout to the new challenge name) plus a poll or question sticker.
5. Invite every engaged commenter from sprint 1 personally — max 20, built from your Day-12 invite log plus anyone who engaged since. Each invite is individually written: their name, what they actually said or did, the join steps, ending on a question not a pitch. Deliver where the relationship already exists (their DM thread, else a reply to their comment). No cold DMs to one-time likers, no blast — if two invites read alike, rewrite one.
6. Log everything: post/story URLs in `Social`, each invite in `Outreach` (name, platform, what they engaged with, date sent).

**Deliverable:** `how-to-join-squad-squats.md` committed + 2 launch posts + join-steps story live + up to 20 logged personal invites.
**Done when:** The join doc matches the app screen-for-screen (you walked it), both posts have URLs in the tracker, and every invite row is visibly its own message.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 Engagement hour — reply everything, 10 genuine comments (45–60 min)

**Why:** Launch-day posts live or die on early reply velocity, and being a good neighbor on other accounts keeps planting us in the beginner-fitness neighborhood for free.
**Where:** IG, TikTok, YouTube, FB — today's live posts and the niche around them.
**Tools:** Phone, notifications ON, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Reply to **100%** of comments on today's posts — short, warm, human; lean into skeptics kindly, never argue, never delete criticism (only spam).
2. Pin the best comment on each post.
3. Paste every question or great audience phrasing into the hooks-inbox in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.
4. Leave 10 genuine comments on niche accounts (home-workout/beginner-fitness creators and communities) — actually helpful, zero app plugs, no links.
5. Log comment counts + anything that popped or flopped in the tracker `Social` tab.

**Deliverable:** All comments answered, best comments pinned, 10 outbound genuine comments, notes in the tracker.
**Done when:** No comment on today's posts sat unanswered longer than 2 hours.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 18 · Task 1 (the posts exist to engage on — if a slot slipped to evening, run the hour around whatever is live).
