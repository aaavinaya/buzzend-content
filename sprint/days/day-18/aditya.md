## Task 1 · 🔥 "Challenge a Friend" pack — 1 reel + 3 join-steps story graphics (90–110 min)

**Why:** Sprint 2 scales through the share loop, and Pillar 3 (`buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md`) is how: friend challenges where "the camera keeps score, nobody can lie." This pack gives Riya a challenge reel plus stories that literally show someone how to join — the piece sprint 1 never had.
**Where:** CapCut (reel) + Canva (stories); footage from `buzzend-footage/master-set/` and `buzzend-footage/sprint2-batch1/`.
**Tools:** CapCut, Canva, `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md` (example script 3 — the friend battle is the blueprint), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (Challenge category), shared drive.

**Steps:**
1. Script first, 20 minutes: follow example script 3's beats — hook ("Challenge your friend." or "Bet you can't do 15 push-ups the app actually counts." from the bank), fast-cut PROOF, result moment, CTA **"Challenge a friend."** (the Pillar-3 CTA — the only CTA in the piece).
2. Build the two-phones/duet feel from existing footage — no new shoot: split-screen two different counter takes side by side in CapCut (e.g. a squat wide from `master-set/` vs a batch-1 take), counts climbing in both halves. It reads as a head-to-head; never claim it's a live simultaneous battle it isn't — the text says "challenge a friend", not "we raced".
3. Auto-captions proofed, 15–25s, 1080×1920 export + cover frame.
4. In Canva, make 3 story graphics on brand colors **#ff6b00 / #fff8f2**: Story 1 — the hook + a counter still (crop one from `refrence-asset/buzzend_how_landmark_is_shown_1.png` framed in the story, never posted raw); Story 2 — the join steps, numbered (open Buzzend → pick the exercise → send the challenge to a friend → camera keeps score); Story 3 — "who are you challenging?" prompt + "link in bio" pointing at the store link from `sprint/config.js`. On-screen text ≤10 words per screen, big, high contrast.
5. Self-check the pack against checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`: any count shown is real, one CTA per piece, no banned words.
6. Drop in **READY/**: `day18-challenge-reel-master.mp4` + cover, `day18-challenge-story-1.png` … `-3.png`. Log paths in the tracker. Avinaya reviews async; fixes ship as follow-ups.

**Deliverable:** Challenge reel master + 3 story graphics in READY/, logged.
**Done when:** A viewer of the three stories knows exactly how to challenge a friend, and the reel plays proof-first with a single "Challenge a friend." CTA.
