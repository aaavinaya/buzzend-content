# Day 6 — Gaurab

**Objective:** Lay honest Reddit groundwork — learn each community's rules and earn credibility with real help, zero links — and mirror Reel #2 to TikTok.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Map 5 subreddits and their self-promotion rules (60–75 min)

**Why:** Reddit destroys brands that show up selling. The communities we'll eventually share value in have written rules — reading them BEFORE posting anything is the difference between a growth channel and a permanent ban.
**Where:** Reddit (your personal account, logged in).
**Tools:** Phone or laptop, a new doc `buzzend-marketing/12-community/reddit-notes.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Find 5 subreddits where our person (beginner, home workouts, no equipment) actually hangs out — start from r/bodyweightfitness, r/homegym, and r/GetMotivated-type communities, then browse "similar communities" for 2 more (size 50k+ and active today).
2. For each, open the sidebar/wiki rules and read them fully — especially anything about self-promotion, surveys, app mentions, and "Saturday show-off threads"-style exceptions.
3. In `reddit-notes.md`, one block per subreddit: name · members · posts/day (rough) · exact self-promo rule quoted · what IS allowed (e.g. mention in comments if asked, weekly thread only, never) · tone of the top posts this week.
4. Mark each subreddit GREEN (value posts welcome), YELLOW (comments only), or RED (never mention the app).
5. End the doc with a 3-line plan: which 2 subreddits we could serve first, with what kind of genuinely useful post — no links, per `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/12-community/reddit-notes.md` with 5 rated subreddits.
**Done when:** All 5 blocks have a quoted rule and a GREEN/YELLOW/RED rating.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🔥 Genuinely answer 3 fitness/tech questions — zero links (45–60 min)

**Why:** Reddit accounts earn the right to be heard by helping first. Three real answers today start the credibility we'll need next week — and the questions people ask are free content research.
**Where:** The 5 subreddits from Task 1 (plus r/fitness-style question threads).
**Tools:** Your Reddit account, your actual experience testing Buzzend and doing home workouts.

**Steps:**
1. Sort by New in your 5 subreddits and find 3 questions you can genuinely answer — beginner squat/push-up form struggles, staying consistent at home, phone/camera/app tech questions.
2. Write real answers from experience: specific, personal, actually useful. 4+ sentences each, the kind of answer you'd want.
3. Hard rules: zero links, zero app mentions, zero "I work on a fitness app" — this is help, not marketing. The no-spam rules in `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md` apply verbatim.
4. Save the 3 question URLs + the exact wording of each question at the bottom of `reddit-notes.md` — real user language feeds the hook bank later.
5. Check back before end of day and answer any replies to your comments.

**Deliverable:** 3 helpful answers posted + question wording logged in `reddit-notes.md`.
**Done when:** All 3 answers are live, link-free, and logged.
**Depends on:** Gaurab · Day 6 · Task 1

## Task 3 · 🟡 Mirror Reel #2 to TikTok (20–30 min)

**Why:** TikTok is our biggest cold-reach channel and Avinaya's hands are full — the clean master exists, mirroring it is 20 minutes.
**Where:** TikTok app.
**Tools:** Android phone, the clean master from Avinaya, `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Get the clean master from Avinaya (never a watermarked re-upload — publishing sin #1).
2. Upload natively in the evening slot (18:00–21:00): caption starts with the search phrase, 3–5 hashtags, cover frame = person + counter visible.
3. Immediately pin the comment: "It's Buzzend — App Store & Google Play 🔗 in bio" (links in bio come from `sprint/config.js`).
4. While it processes, do today's 10 counted squats — your streak and the challenge leaderboard need to stay honest before tomorrow's review-asks.
5. Stay 30–45 min replying to early comments, then log the URL in the `Social` tab.

**Deliverable:** Reel #2 live on TikTok with pinned comment; URL in `Social`.
**Done when:** URL logged and the bio link opens the Play Store page when tapped.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 6 · Task 1
