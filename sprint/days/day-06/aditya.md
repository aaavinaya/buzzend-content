## Task 1 · 🔥 Produce Reel #2 — Beat-It, the TOGETHER pillar (90–110 min)

**Why:** Reel #1 proved the counter; Reel #2 shows why friends make it fun — you post your set, friends get one camera-verified shot to beat it, nobody can lie. It publishes today at the playbook slots, so have the master in READY/ by midday.
**Where:** CapCut; footage from your master set + whatever Android b-roll is already banked.
**Tools:** CapCut, `buzzend-footage/master-set/`, Android b-roll if banked (`broll-05-beatit.mp4`, `broll-02-invite.mp4`, `broll-04-leaderboard.mp4` — index in the tracker), `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md` (example script 3 is the friend-battle shape), `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md` (Pillar 3 rules), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Write your own 60–80-word script first — 10 minutes, never wait for one (structure from `how-to-write-a-script.md`): pick a Challenge-category hook from `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (e.g. "Bet you can't do 15 push-ups the app actually counts."), write 3 wordings, say them aloud, keep the most human one. If a Reel #2 script is already in **READY/**, use it instead.
2. Pre-flight the feature: run a real Beat-It attempt in the app yourself so you know exactly what the screens show — the flow in the edit must be the flow that exists. Note your §40 line (phone, position, verified count).
3. Assemble in CapCut: HOOK 0–2s from your master set (counter already moving), then the story — your set posted → the invite → the Beat-It attempt → the leaderboard result. Cutaways: use the Android b-roll if it's in READY/ or the drive; otherwise screen-record the invite, attempt and leaderboard screens yourself on your own device during step 2's real attempt — never wait. Real counts on every screen.
4. Keep Pillar 3's frame: friends and belonging, not domination — no calling out losers, no punishing stakes.
5. Auto-captions on and proofed; 15–30s; end card + spoken CTA **"Challenge a friend."** (the challenge-content CTA from `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`).
6. Self-check against the review card (checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`): real counts on the exact phone, one CTA, no banned words. Export the clean 1080×1920 master + cover frame (two-person or person+leaderboard moment) to **READY/** named `day6-reel2-beatit-master.mp4`, log the path. Avinaya verifies async same day; fixes ship as follow-ups. Nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** `day6-reel2-beatit-master.mp4` + cover frame in **READY/**, path in the tracker.
**Done when:** The master is in READY/ by midday, every on-screen number is real, and the self-check passes.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 3 · Task 1 (the master set).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Batch 5 caption + hashtag sets for the coming week (40–50 min)

**Why:** Captions written the morning of a post get sloppy; batching five now means publish days only need platform tweaks — the pipeline's caption stage done a week ahead.
**Where:** A captions doc linked in the tracker.
**Tools:** Laptop, `buzzend-marketing/11-publishing/publishing-playbook.md` (hashtag/keyword rules), `social-media/week1-content-calendar.md` (the voice to keep), `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md` (templates 5 and 6).

**Steps:**
1. List the next 5 pieces from your content queue (today's Reel #2 onward, through the week-2 slots you already know about).
2. Draft each caption with template 5: line 1 restates the hook / search phrase (no hashtags on line 1), 1–3 value lines, exactly ONE CTA per template 6 — "Try your first AI workout — link in bio." or "Challenge a friend." on challenge content, never both.
3. Hashtags per the playbook quick reference: 3–5, base set #homeworkout #beginnerfitness #aifitness #repcounter, plus the exercise tag and one honest trend-cluster tag.
4. Links are "link in bio" everywhere — the store links come from `sprint/config.js`; never paste a URL from memory into a caption.
5. Banned-words pass against `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md`, then drop the doc in **READY/** named `day6-captions` and link it in the tracker — Avinaya verifies async; fixes ship as follow-ups. Nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** 5 caption + hashtag sets in a doc, in READY/ and linked in the tracker.
**Done when:** Any of the five could publish with only platform tweaks, and the doc is in READY/ with its tracker link live.
**Depends on:** Aditya · Day 2 · Task 2 (the content queue).
