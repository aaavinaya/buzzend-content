# Day 6 — Riya

**Objective:** Map where our people already gather (Facebook groups + Discords) without promoting anything yet, and bank authentic in-app moments by actually living the challenge.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Build the community map — 10 Facebook groups + 3 Discord servers (75–90 min)

**Why:** Groups are where home-workout beginners actually ask for help, but every group has its own tolerance for apps. Mapping rules and vibe FIRST means that when we do share value next week, we're welcome — not banned.
**Where:** Facebook (your personal profile) + Discord.
**Tools:** Phone or laptop, a new doc `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Search Facebook groups for: *home workout, workout challenge, fitness accountability, beginner fitness, 30 day challenge*. Shortlist the 10 most alive ones (posts today, real comments — not link dumps).
2. Search Discord (disboard.org or Discord's own discovery) for home-workout / fitness-challenge servers; shortlist 3 with visible daily activity.
3. In `community-map.md`, one row per community: name · platform · size · activity (posts/day) · rules on app mentions (quote the rule if written) · value-posts welcome? (GREEN/YELLOW/RED, same scale Gaurab uses for Reddit).
4. Join the 5 most promising (answer join questions honestly as a person, not a brand).
5. Introduce NOTHING yet — observe only: note in the doc what questions repeat, what posts get real engagement, and the exact phrases people use about counting reps / staying consistent.
6. Ping the team with the doc link and your top-2 "we could genuinely help here" picks.

**Deliverable:** `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-map.md` with 13 rated communities, 5 joined.
**Done when:** All 13 rows are complete and you're a member of 5 groups with zero posts made.
**Depends on:** —

## Task 2 · 🟡 QA-as-marketing — live day 2 of the challenge, capture 2 real moments (30–40 min)

**Why:** We sell an experience we should be having ourselves. Doing day 2 keeps your streak honest, stress-tests the challenge flow, and real moments (streak tick, leaderboard move) are tomorrow's most authentic content raw material.
**Where:** Buzzend app on your phone.
**Tools:** Phone, screen recorder, shared assets folder, tracker Notes.

**Steps:**
1. Do your 10 counted squats for day 2 of **7-Day Squat Starter** — phone propped, camera counting, no shortcuts.
2. Screen-record the 2 moments worth keeping: the streak ticking up after the set, and your position moving on the challenge leaderboard.
3. Privacy pass: only team/consenting friends' names visible — crop or re-record if a stranger's name shows.
4. Drop both captures in the shared assets folder labeled `day6-streak` and `day6-leaderboard`. These are RAW material — rule 4 says they don't go public until framed in brand style (Gaurab's Day-5 Canva frames are the pattern).
5. Log any friction, confusing screen, or miscount in tracker Notes — QA feedback for the product team, content angles for us.

**Deliverable:** 2 labeled captures in the shared folder + friction notes in the tracker.
**Done when:** Both captures exist, your streak shows day 2 complete, and Notes has at least one observation.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 5 · Task 1 (you joined the challenge yesterday — this is your day 2).
