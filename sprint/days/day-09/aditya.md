## Task 1 · 🔥 Produce Reel #3 — streaks, with a REAL streak on screen (75–90 min)

**Why:** "Don't break the chain" is a feeling everyone knows, and it's the TOGETHER pillar's retention story. It publishes today, and the first rule is that every number on screen is real — so the streak you record must be an actual streak.
**Where:** Buzzend app (screen recorder) + CapCut.
**Tools:** Phone, CapCut, `buzzend-footage/` (master set + day-8 batch), `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-system.md` (Before/After and Transformation categories), `buzzend-marketing/04-content-pillars/pillars.md` (Pillar 3).

**Steps:**
1. Screen-record your own streak screen — you've done a counted set daily since onboarding, so the flame should show 6+ days. If your streak broke, don't wait on anyone: use a teammate's real streak capture only if one is already in **READY/**; otherwise record your own real screen whatever it shows and tell the restart honestly ("broke it at day 4, back on day 2 of the new chain") — never a mockup, never an edited number. Do Not Disturb on, clean capture.
2. Write your own 60–80-word script — 10 minutes, structure from `buzzend-marketing/07-scripts/how-to-write-a-script.md`: hook from Before/After or Transformation in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (e.g. "Week 1: I forgot to work out 4 days. This week: day 6 of the chain."), the streak screen as the payoff, one line on why the chain keeps you honest (the camera counted every day of it).
3. Assemble in CapCut: hook 0–2s with the counter or flame already on screen, camera b-roll of you actually working out (master set / day-8 batch) intercut with the streak screen-capture, auto-captions proofed.
4. Keep it warm, not grindy — Pillar 3 is belonging, not "no excuses" culture. 15–30 seconds.
5. End card + spoken CTA **"Try your first AI workout."**; fill the §40 line (device, position, verified count for any counted set shown).
6. Self-check against the review card (checklist #7 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/workflow-templates.md`): real counts on the exact phone, one CTA, no banned words. Export `day9-reel3-streak-master.mp4` + cover frame (flame/streak moment) to **READY/** by midday, log the path. Avinaya verifies async same day; fixes ship as follow-ups. Nothing waits.

**Deliverable:** `day9-reel3-streak-master.mp4` + cover frame in **READY/**, path in the tracker.
**Done when:** The master is in READY/ before the posting slot and the streak on screen is genuinely someone's real streak.
