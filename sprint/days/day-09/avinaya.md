## Task 1 · 🔥 Produce and publish Reel #3 — streaks (90–120 min)

**Why:** Streaks are the feature that keeps people opening the app, and "don't break the chain" is a feeling everyone knows. Reels #1–2 taught us the format; this one adds real proof — an actual streak, not a mockup.
**Where:** Your phone (screen recording + camera), CapCut or the IG editor, Instagram + YouTube.
**Tools:** iPhone screen recording, CapCut, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`, Growth Tracker.

**Steps:**
1. Pick the hook from `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (or Riya's `sprint-15` shortlist in `buzzend-marketing/06-content-ideas/idea-backlog.md`) — a streak-flavored one, e.g. "Day 1 of doing squats until my phone says 50" adapted to your real streak day, or a "don't break the chain" line. Write it as on-screen text for the first second.
2. Screen-record the real thing: open Buzzend, record your streak screen showing your actual streak from the flagship "7-Day Squat Starter". Never fake or inflate the number — if your streak broke, that honesty is the video ("I broke my streak. Starting again today.").
3. Check the frame: only team members and consenting friends visible (our flagship challenge is friends-only). If any other name shows, re-record tighter or fall back to `assets/screens-clean/real-streak.png` / `streak.png`.
4. Film 3–5 seconds of yourself doing today's reps — a human doing a squat beats pure UI every time.
5. Edit: hook text in the first second → your reps → the streak screen → end card with ONE CTA ("Don't break the chain — try your first AI workout."). 1080×1920, under 30 seconds, captions on (most people watch muted).
6. Publish to IG Reels and YouTube Shorts. Caption per template 5 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/content-templates.md`; store links stay in bio, from `sprint/config.js`.
7. Send Gaurab the exported video file (not a watermarked download) — he mirrors it to TikTok today.
8. Log both URLs in the tracker `Social` tab and stay online 30–60 min after posting.

**Deliverable:** Reel #3 live on IG + Shorts, export sent to Gaurab, URLs in the tracker.
**Done when:** Both URLs are in the `Social` tab and every number shown on screen is real.

## Task 2 · 🟡 Same-day reply to every comment and DM (30–45 min)

**Why:** Outreach DMs are landing and Reel #3 is fresh — this is exactly when a fast, human reply converts a viewer into an install and a comment into our next hook.
**Where:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook — comments and DMs.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Two sweeps today (midday + evening): every comment on every platform gets a reply — short, warm, human; corporate tone banned.
2. Pin the best comment on Reel #3.
3. Skeptical comments are gifts: reply kindly, then flag them in team chat as comment-reply video candidates ("Someone said it's not real-time. Okay — watch.").
4. Paste every genuine question verbatim into the hooks-inbox table of `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` with date + where it came from.
5. Answer every DM; anything from a creator on the outreach list goes to Riya to keep statuses accurate.

**Deliverable:** Zero unanswered comments/DMs at end of day; new verbatim rows in hooks-inbox.
**Done when:** Both sweeps done and the hooks-inbox has today's questions word-for-word.
