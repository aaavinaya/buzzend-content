# Day 9 — Riya

**Objective:** Double the outreach pipe to 15 creators contacted and give our community a visible home on the profile.
**Total time:** ~2.5h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Send 10 more personalized creator DMs (75–90 min)

**Why:** Outreach is a numbers game played one person at a time — 15 genuine messages this week should produce 2–3 real conversations. Same personalization bar as yesterday; the moment it feels copy-paste, stop and rewrite.
**Where:** IG/TikTok DMs + YouTube contact emails, from the Buzzend account; `Outreach` tab.
**Tools:** Phone, Growth Tracker, `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/outreach-templates.md`.

**Steps:**
1. First: update yesterday's 5 rows — anyone replied overnight? Set `Replied` / `Interested` and answer them within the hour; flag warm ones to Avinaya.
2. Pick the next 10 un-contacted creators across both lists (Avinaya's IG/TikTok rows + Gaurab's 3 marked YouTube rows).
3. For each: 2–3 recent posts watched, one specific first line naming a real post, 60–90 words, one ask — written fresh from the template every time.
4. YouTube creators: use the contact route in their row (About-page email or linked IG) — same message, email-shaped if needed.
5. Space the sends across the day; 10 in one burst looks like a bot.
6. Log all 10 in `Outreach`: Date + Status = `Sent`.
7. Evening sweep: update every status so the tab reflects reality — Avinaya's Day-11 follow-ups depend on these being accurate.

**Deliverable:** 10 new DMs sent; `Outreach` shows 15 contacted with current statuses.
**Done when:** 15 rows have a date + live status, and no reply is older than a few hours unanswered.
**Depends on:** Avinaya · Day 8 · Task 1 + Gaurab · Day 8 · Task 1 (the remaining un-contacted creators).

## Task 2 · 🟡 Create the Instagram "Community" highlight + first repost (30–45 min)

**Why:** When a creator or new follower checks our profile, a Community highlight full of real people is the strongest social proof we own. Even before we have UGC, the shelf should exist and invite it.
**Where:** Instagram (Buzzend account) + Canva.
**Tools:** Phone, Canva, `assets/screens-clean/leaderboard.png`, `brand/design-guide.md`, `buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`.

**Steps:**
1. Make a highlight cover in Canva: Energy Orange, simple "Community" label, matching the brand rules in `brand/design-guide.md`.
2. Sweep mentions, tags, story-mentions and DMs since Day 1: has anyone posted about Buzzend or shared a challenge screen?
3. If yes: DM them first — "This is great — can we share it?" (playbook wording). Only after an explicit yes: repost to our story and save it into the new highlight.
4. If no UGC yet: create a "your reps could be here" story in Canva — `assets/screens-clean/leaderboard.png` + one line ("Your reps could be here. Join a challenge.") — post it and save it to the highlight so it isn't empty.
5. Add the highlight cover and set the name to **Community**.
6. Note in the tracker `Social` tab: highlight live, UGC reposted (who) or placeholder used.

**Deliverable:** Live Community highlight with at least one story in it.
**Done when:** The highlight shows on the profile, and any reposted content has a logged permission yes.
