## Task 1 · 🔥 Week-1 analytics deep dive — fill the Week-1 row and name the winner (75–90 min)

**Why:** Seven days of posting means nothing until we know which format moved installs. This is the sprint's first real verdict, and Riya's entire week-2 plan is built from it this afternoon.
**Where:** Growth Tracker + App Store Connect + Play Console + each platform's analytics.
**Tools:** Laptop, store logins, template #11 in `buzzend-marketing/16-templates/planning-templates.md`, `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md`.

**Steps:**
1. In `Daily Numbers`, add a bold **Week 1** summary row: total iOS installs and total Android installs for days 1–7, next to the Day-1 baseline (7× the daily baseline) — the delta is our first growth claim.
2. From App Store Connect and Play Console, compute the store funnel for the week: impressions → product page views → installs, with the two conversion percentages (installs ÷ page views is the one that matters).
3. In `Social`, complete every posted piece's row (Reel #1, post #2 + stories, Reel #2, poll): reach, likes, comments, saves/shares — plus follower delta per platform vs Day 1.
4. Rank the posts by the winner rule — store visits → installs → first workouts, never likes. Use bio-link taps and same-day install bumps as the trail; where attribution is fuzzy, say so rather than guessing precisely.
5. Write a 5-line summary at the top of a new `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/week-1.md` (template #11 structure): the 5 numbers that matter, best post and WHY, worst post and WHY — and name the ONE winning format in bold (e.g. "real-count reel" vs "challenge announcement still").
6. Post the report link in team chat before lunch — Riya plans week 2 from it today.

**Deliverable:** Week-1 row in the tracker + `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/week-1.md` with the winning format named.
**Done when:** Every posted piece has complete numbers, both store funnels are computed, and the 5-line summary is in team chat.

## Task 2 · 🔥 Write the week-2 decisions list — repeat / change / stop (30 min)

**Why:** Numbers without decisions are trivia. Three bullets turn the week-1 verdict into what the whole team does differently tomorrow morning.
**Where:** Bottom of `buzzend-marketing/14-weekly-reports/week-1.md` + team chat.
**Tools:** Laptop, the decision rules in `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md` (CONTINUE / IMPROVE / REPURPOSE / STOP / TEST AGAIN).

**Steps:**
1. **REPEAT:** one bullet — the winning format from Task 1 and how often week 2 repeats it (with one variation each time, one variable changed).
2. **CHANGE:** one bullet — the thing that half-worked (e.g. good watch time, no link taps → fix the CTA; good reach on one platform only → repurpose per the REPURPOSE rule).
3. **STOP:** one bullet — the angle or habit that lost twice or ate time without moving the bottom of the funnel. Write one honest line about why.
4. Paste all three in team chat and get a 👍 from Gaurab and Riya today — Riya's week-2 plan and Gaurab's Day-10 experiment prep both build on them.
5. Copy the three bullets into the `Experiments` tab as this week's hypotheses (template #13 format: one variable, a hypothesis, verdict pending).

**Deliverable:** 3 decision bullets in the week-1 report, in team chat, and in `Experiments`.
**Done when:** Both teammates have acknowledged the decisions in chat.
