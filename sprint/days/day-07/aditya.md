## Task 2 · 🟡 Turn the week-2 plan into a production list (45–60 min)

**Why:** Riya's 5-post week-2 plan lands this afternoon. Each row says WHAT publishes; only you can say what it costs to make — film, reuse or design — and when it must be shot. Writing that down today is what makes Day 8's batch-filming day a plan instead of a scramble.
**Where:** Growth Tracker (your queue table) + team chat.
**Tools:** Laptop, Riya's approved week-2 plan (in the tracker), the `buzzend-footage/` index, `social-media/rendered/` (unused day3–day7 files), `buzzend-marketing/15-team-workflow/production-pipeline.md` (the batching rules).

**Steps:**
1. Read the approved plan's 5 rows and Avinaya's REPEAT/CHANGE/STOP decisions — nothing in your list may contradict them.
2. For each post, decide the production route: **reuse** (name the exact file — master set, b-roll, `social-media/rendered/` leftovers), **film** (goes on tomorrow's batch list), or **design** (Canva, with which `assets/screens-clean/` imagery).
3. For every "film" row, write the shot in one line: exercise · hook it serves · wide/close · roughly how long.
4. Set the delivery date per row, working back from its publish day: edit day, approval day (Avinaya's pass), publish day.
5. Paste the production list under the plan in the tracker and post it in team chat — tomorrow morning you film from it, no decisions left.

**Deliverable:** A 5-row production list (route · source/shot · delivery date) in the tracker.
**Done when:** Every week-2 post has a named source file or a slot on tomorrow's batch list, and Riya has seen the list.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 7 · Task 1 (the approved week-2 plan).
