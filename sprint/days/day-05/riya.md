## Task 2 · 🟡 Story extras — countdown + poll, reply to everything (30–45 min)

**Why:** Sticker taps and replies are heavyweight ranking signals (`buzzend-marketing/12-community/community-playbook.md`), and the poll seeds the exact skepticism our content answers — every reply is market research.
**Where:** Instagram Stories.
**Tools:** Phone, `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` (hooks-inbox table).

**Steps:**
1. Post a Story with a countdown sticker ending tomorrow morning: "Day 2 of the Squat Starter starts in…".
2. Post a second Story with a poll: **"Can AI really count your squats?"** — options "no way 🙅" / "prove it 👀".
3. Reply personally to every poll vote reply and DM — a "no way" is a gift: answer warmly and point them to Reel #1 ("watch it count — then come try"). Never argue.
4. Paste the exact wording of every skeptical or curious reply into the hooks-inbox table in `buzzend-marketing/05-hooks/hook-bank.md` — verbatim audience phrases outrank anything we invent.
5. Check back twice before end of day; the target is replies to 100% of responses.

**Deliverable:** 2 interactive Stories live + hooks-inbox rows for every notable reply.
**Done when:** Every response has a personal reply and at least the poll's exact best phrases are in the hook bank.
**Depends on:** Riya · Day 5 · Task 1
