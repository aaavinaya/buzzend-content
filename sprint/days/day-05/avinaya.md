# Day 5 — Avinaya

**Objective:** Put one real, public, join-able challenge live in the app — the thing every post and invite this week points at — and capture Day-4's first results before they scroll away.
**Total time:** ~2h

## Task 1 · 🔥 Create the "7-Day Squat Starter" challenge and write the 5-step join doc (60–75 min)

**Why:** Yesterday we told people Buzzend exists; today we give them one concrete, low-bar thing to DO. Posts, stories, and invites all fail if "join the challenge" isn't dead simple — so the challenge must exist first, and the join path must be written down tap by tap.
**Where:** Buzzend app on your iPhone, then a short doc in this repo.
**Tools:** iPhone with Buzzend, a second device (or Gaurab's Android) for the fresh-eyes join test, code editor.

**Steps:**
1. In the app, open the challenges area (Challenges tab / Discover) and tap Create challenge.
2. Configure it with existing options only: name **7-Day Squat Starter** · exercise **Squats** · **10 reps/day** · **7 days** · visibility **Public**. Camera-verified rep counting is what Buzzend challenges do — do not invent settings; if an exact option (e.g. rep target) isn't offered, pick the closest and note what you actually chose.
3. Do today's 10 squats yourself, camera-counted, so the leaderboard is never empty when the first invitee opens it.
4. Now walk the JOIN path as a stranger (second device or fresh session): from app open → find the challenge → join → first counted squat. Write down every tap exactly — is it findable via Discover? Search? Only invite link?
5. Write `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squat-starter.md`: exactly 5 numbered steps, one action each, starting at "Install Buzzend — App Store / Play Store link from `sprint/config.js`" and ending at "do your first 10 counted squats". Plain words per `buzzend-marketing/00-strategy/what-is-buzzend.md` — no tech-speak, no banned words.
6. Post the doc in team chat and stay reachable — Gaurab and Riya both join from it this morning and will flag any step that doesn't match reality.

**Deliverable:** Live public **7-Day Squat Starter** challenge + `buzzend-marketing/12-community/how-to-join-squat-starter.md` posted in team chat.
**Done when:** Gaurab and Riya have both joined using ONLY the 5 steps, and at least 3 names show on the leaderboard.

## Task 2 · 🟡 First numbers check — record Day-4 results (30–40 min)

**Why:** Reel #1 and the first 15 personal invites went out yesterday. Capturing the first real numbers now, while they're one click away, makes the Day-7 review a 10-minute job instead of an archaeology dig.
**Where:** Growth Tracker + App Store Connect + Play Console + each social app's analytics.
**Tools:** Laptop, store logins, `Daily Numbers` and `Social` tabs.

**Steps:**
1. Open every Reel #1 post (URLs are logged in `Social` from Day 4) and record per post: reach, views, likes, comments, saves/shares.
2. App Store Connect → Analytics → Metrics: yesterday's Total Downloads. Play Console → Statistics: yesterday's new installs. Enter both in today's `Daily Numbers` row.
3. Compute the installs delta vs the Day-1 baseline for both stores and write it in the row (e.g. "iOS +4/day vs baseline 2/day").
4. Add current challenge joins (the leaderboard count from Task 1) to the `Challenge joins` column.
5. Write ONE line in Notes: the single most surprising number and your best guess why. Use the funnel in `buzzend-marketing/13-analytics/metrics-for-beginners.md` — bottom of funnel first, likes last.

**Deliverable:** Day-4 per-post numbers in `Social` + today's `Daily Numbers` row with installs delta and one-line observation.
**Done when:** Every Reel #1 post has all five numbers filled and the Notes line exists.
