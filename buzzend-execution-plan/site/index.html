The 5 metrics that matter (§29)

When time is short, track only these

  • Store visits
  • Installs
  • First-workout (activation) rate
  • D7 retention
  • Referrals / shares

Weekly reviews

45–60 min, same evening, numbers first — adjectives banned. Every review answers §50: Who responded? What message worked? Did they actually use Buzzend? File as reviews/week-N.md.

Week 1 · Day 7

Was the foundation finished?

  • POSITIONING / ICP / COMPETITORS all READY?
  • Tracked links attribute per platform — prove with a test tap
  • Baseline row complete? No baseline = Week 2 invalid
  • All 5 exercises have a §40-clean take
  • V01 24h read: drop before 2s / 10s / later?
Week 2 · Day 14

Which hook wins — by the winner rule

  • 6-video comparison table with attribution confidence
  • Protocol respected? Contamination = learning, not result
  • Top 2 hooks kept · 1 killed · one sentence each
  • Challenge CTA vs default CTA read
  • “What do I believe now?” paragraph
Week 3 · Day 21

Did conversion improve?

  • Both store pages updated + preview staged
  • Install ÷ store-visit before vs after (note ship date)
  • Onboarding: taps + time-to-first-rep, top-3 tickets
  • The Day-20 activation sentence, with numbers
  • 1 stay-reason + 1 leave-reason, verbatim

Retention truth + Month 2 plan

  • D1 · D7 · first D30 · 2nd-workout rate · streaks · shares
  • §36 loop end-to-end: share → link → install → challenge
  • Churn categories vs funnel data — do they corroborate?
  • §51 template: worked / didn't / product behavior
  • Decisions: Continue · Stop · Test → MONTH_1_REPORT.md

Reference

The fixed vocabulary. Every bio, caption, store line and script copies from here — wording is never re-invented.

Message ladder
Buzzend uses your phone camera to count your workout reps and coach you while you exercise.
  • 5s: “My phone just counted my squats.”
  • 10s: “Put your phone down, start moving, and Buzzend counts your reps while you work out.”
  • Caption: “Stop counting. Start moving.”
  • Campaign: Put Your Phone Down Challenge
  • CTA: Try your first AI workout.
Audience

The At-Home Starter · 18–35

Beginner–intermediate · home/small space · no equipment · smartphone-heavy · wants consistency and visible progress. Loses count, hates complicated programs, doesn't want a trainer.

Secondary: Challenge Friend · Busy Professional · Returning Exerciser.
Not now (§10): athletes, gym lifters, nutrition, medical/rehab, trainers, schools, children.

Banned claims (§7)
perfect formguaranteedinjury-proofreplaces your trainermedical-grademost accurateworld's firstscientifically proven

Also never lead with: MediaPipe, pose landmarks, computer vision, algorithms. Benefit language only.

§40 pre-publish checklist
  • Exact exercise tested on the exact device + camera position
  • Displayed count confirmed against reality
  • Coaching behavior confirmed
  • No edit implies functionality that doesn't exist
  • Only assets/screens-clean imagery; demo identities only
