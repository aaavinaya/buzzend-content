# Buzzend — Social Media Execution Plan

Platform-by-platform breakdown of the strategy (§20 channels, §21 pillars,
§22 mix, §35 community) plus the Month-1 day-by-day publishing schedule.
Asset IDs come from `CONTENT_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`.

**Channel priority is fixed by the strategy (§20):**
Priority 1 = Instagram Reels · TikTok · YouTube Shorts · ASO.
Facebook is **not** a P1 channel — it exists because the week-1 static kit
supports it; treat it as a zero-extra-effort mirror. Reddit is research
only. Discord: not yet (§35). Do not add platforms this month.

---

## Instagram (Reels + Stories) — primary community surface

- **Purpose:** discovery via Reels + the main community/relationship surface (§35).
- **Audience:** At-Home Starter 18–35; secondary Challenge Friend.
- **Content:** Reels = the daily video slot (all pillars). Stories = 2–3/week
  (ST-* assets, polls, reposts). Feed statics from `social-media/rendered/`
  as support.
- **Frequency:** 5 Reels/week + 2–3 Stories/week.
- **Formats:** 9:16 Reels 15–30 s, burned captions; Story polls/questions;
  occasional carousel from WH-4 educational stills.
- **Hooks:** spoken + on-screen text in first 2 s (hook bank).
- **CTA:** "Try your first AI workout." + "link in bio" — never raw URLs
  in captions (link tool: the `ig` tracked link).
- **Engagement:** reply to 100% of comments (standing daily block);
  pin the best "is this real?" comment and answer it on camera later.
- **Community:** polls, challenge announcements, user reposts (with
  permission), milestone Stories (§35).
- **Metrics:** views, watch time, retention, profile visits, link taps
  (`ig` link), follows; weekly into the dashboard.
- **30-day target:** post every planned slot (20 Reels); ≥1 Reel with
  ≥2× baseline store-visit rate; comment response rate 100%.

## TikTok — discovery engine

- **Purpose:** raw discovery; where "wait, the camera counts?" spreads (§35).
- **Audience:** same ICP; skews younger — keep beginner tone, no gym-bro voice.
- **Content:** same daily video, native upload (no IG watermark);
  reply-to-comment videos once questions accumulate (Month 2 lever).
- **Frequency:** 5/week, evening slot (18:00–21:00 until Day 8 fixes it).
- **Hooks:** first-frame text matters more here — cover text = hook.
- **CTA:** "Try your first AI workout." + link in bio (`tt` link).
- **Engagement:** 15 min/day comment block; save every recurring question
  to `hooks-inbox`; creator collabs start Month 2 (§34), not now.
- **Metrics:** views, avg watch time, retention %, shares, saves,
  profile visits, `tt` link taps.
- **30-day target:** 20 posts; identify which hook style TikTok rewards
  vs IG (they often differ — record it in the Day 14/30 reviews).

## YouTube Shorts — search-adjacent discovery

- **Purpose:** longevity — Shorts surface for months; captures "AI rep
  counter" search intent.
- **Content:** same daily video; titles are searchable phrases, not hooks
  ("AI counts my squats in real time — Buzzend" beats "POV:…").
- **Frequency:** 5/week.
- **CTA:** pinned comment + description with the `yt` tracked link.
- **Metrics:** views, avg view duration, subs, `yt` link taps.
- **30-day target:** 20 Shorts; a title/hook learning written in the
  Month 1 report.

## Facebook — passive mirror (secondary)

- **Purpose:** presence + the week-1 static campaign audience; zero extra
  production.
- **Content:** mirror the daily video or the matching
  `social-media/rendered/day*-feed.png` static with its calendar caption.
- **Frequency:** ≤1/day, only if it costs <10 min; drop first when time
  is short.
- **Metrics:** link taps only. Do not optimize for FB this month.

## Reddit — research only (§35)

- Participate genuinely in home-workout/beginner-fitness subreddits:
  answer questions, follow sub rules. **No promo posts, no link drops,
  no pretending to be a regular user.**
- Harvest: exact language people use about counting reps/home workouts →
  `hooks-inbox`.
- Weekly: ~30 min as part of the weekend community slot.

## Weekly rhythm behind the schedule (§49)

Mon research/plan · Tue record · Wed publish+engage · Thu educational ·
Fri publish+review analytics · weekend challenge/community + feedback.
The schedule above already encodes this — follow the table, the rhythm
takes care of itself.

## Platform rules that never change

1. One tracked link per platform (`ig`/`tt`/`yt`) — attribution is the
   whole point of Month 1.
2. Native uploads only; no cross-platform watermarks.
3. Same-day, same-time posting during test week (Days 9–13) — the test
   dies otherwise.
4. Winner rule (§33): judge by store visits → installs → first workouts.
5. Every caption uses message-ladder wording; banned claims list applies
   to captions and comments you write.
