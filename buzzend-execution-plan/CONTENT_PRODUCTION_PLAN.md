## Content pillars (§21) and month mix (§22)

| Pillar | Purpose | Share of output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · AI in Action | Product proof — counter visibly working | ~40% |
| 2 · Fitness Problems | Education/discovery — mistakes, framing, "why didn't it count?" | ~25% (with Pillar 6) |
| 3 · Relatable Fitness | "Losing count at 17", starting again Monday | ~20% (with Pillar 4) |
| 4 · Challenges | 50 squats, 7-day streak, friend vs friend | (in the 20%) |
| 5 · Build in Public | Testing rep counting, improving coaching | ~15% |
| 6 · Beginner Confidence | "Start with 10", five minutes beats zero | (in the 25%) |

**Cadence:** ≥5 short-form videos/week from Day 7 (§22, §48) + 2–3
Stories/week (§27) + 1 community/research activity/week (§49).

## The video formula — every video, no exceptions (§23)

0–2 s hook (show the unusual thing) → 2–6 s proof (person + counter) →
6–15 s explanation ("Buzzend uses your phone camera to count reps while
you work out.") → 15–25 s benefit ("So you can focus on moving instead of
counting.") → CTA ("Try your first AI workout." / challenge videos:
"Challenge a friend.").

**Production rules:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 · burned-in captions ·
15–30 s total · cover frame shows person + counter · native upload per
platform, never watermarked re-uploads · §40: the count on screen is
always real.

## CTA strategy

- Default: **"Try your first AI workout."** (activation-aligned, §19)
- Challenge content: **"Challenge a friend."** (tested Day 13; §33 #8)
- Store buttons/link-in-bio wording matches the video CTA that drove the
  tap. Month 2 runs the formal CTA A/B (§27).

## Repurposing strategy

1. **One shoot, many cuts:** each Day 5 exercise set must yield ≥3
   videos (proof cut, question cut, education cut).
2. **Video → Story:** every published video's best 3 s becomes a Story
   with a poll/question sticker within 48 h.
3. **Video → store:** best-performing footage feeds the Day 18 store
   preview; winning hook language feeds screenshot 1.
4. **Comments → content:** every recurring question becomes a
   reply-video or an educational post (Pillar 2).
5. **Statics:** the 14 finished week-1 posts (`social-media/rendered/`)
   are the Story/feed support library — captions ready in
   `social-media/week1-content-calendar.md`. Do not let them replace
   video slots; video is the proof medium.
6. **Never delete losing videos** — mark ARCHIVED in this register with
   one line on why they lost.

## Production guardrails

- §40 checklist before every shoot: exact exercise + camera position +
  device verified; displayed count confirmed; no implied functionality.
- Banned claims/words list (§7) applies to captions, on-screen text, and
  spoken audio.
- Only `assets/screens-clean/` imagery in outward material; approved demo
  identities only (Ema William, Adesh Pokhrel, Kiran Shah…).
- Filming real people: only yourself or people who have agreed on camera.
